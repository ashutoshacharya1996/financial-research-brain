## Game Theory View

GPU supply (NVIDIA) and anchor tenants (Together AI) hold bargaining power; the builder (LT) captures EPC and ownership economics only if utilisation holds. PARAS faces a capital-intensity cliff: a Rs 6,200 cr plan against a roughly Rs 480 cr revenue base requires partners or state support that are not yet disclosed.
