## Game Theory View

**Who captures the economics:** The GPU vendor and the anchor tenant (a US AI cloud) hold most bargaining power; LT as host/builder earns an infrastructure return, and the carve-out into Vyoma suggests LT wants a separately funded vehicle for a capital-heavy business.

**Who gets squeezed:** Smaller Indian entrants announcing semiconductor/AI capacity without anchor customers or funding (PARAS's MoU is in this category until funded).

**Who gains bargaining power:** Whoever controls power availability and land with grid connection — LT's 150 MVA readiness is the scarce input, not the building.
