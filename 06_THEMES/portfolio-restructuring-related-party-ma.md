## Game Theory View

Who captures the economics? Promoter groups consolidating into a listed vehicle control both sides of valuation (GABRIEL/AIPL), so the independent valuation report and minority-shareholder votes are the checks. LT's counterparty on Vyoma.AI is a related party it created in April 2026. Government-owned HAL absorbing a nil-consideration, not-arm's-length JV stake shifts liabilities onto its own balance sheet. Minority holders and rating agencies gain bargaining power by pricing governance risk.
