## Game Theory View

**Who captures the economics:** GPU and hardware vendors (NVIDIA) and the AI-cloud tenant (Together AI) capture most of the value. LT gets EPC/integration plus the hosting platform (Vyoma). For OSAT, government incentives under the India Semiconductor Mission decide project viability, which gives the state most of the bargaining power.
**Who gets squeezed:** Small entrants without anchor customers. PARAS has no named OSAT customer.
**Who gains bargaining power:** Players that own land, power and campus capacity for data centres (LT's Chennai campus).

## Counter Arguments

- **Thin corroboration** — only LT has orders. PARAS's OSAT is an MoU without a funding plan or customer.
- **BEL contradicts** — it dropped data-centre solutions from its FY27 pipeline.
- **LT execution slippage** — the Vyoma BTA completion moved from 31-Oct-2026 to 31-Mar-2027 with no reason stated.
- **Capital intensity vs balance sheets** — PARAS's ₹6,200cr is about 8x its equity. RBI tightening (repo 5.50%, per LT's Q2 outlook) raises funding costs.
