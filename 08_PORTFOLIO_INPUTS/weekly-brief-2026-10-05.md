# Weekly Brief — 5 October 2026
*5-minute read. Plain English. What to do.*

---

## The Short Version

The engine hadn't run since June, so this brief catches up on the April–June quarter for all six companies. Sales are growing fast, but profits are not keeping up at most of them. Think of a restaurant packing more tables while each table earns a little less. Defence is still the strongest area, but its biggest pending contract (BEL's QRSAM) is not confirmed in any official filing we have.

## What Changed

| Company | Plain-English take |
|---|---|
| **PARAS** | Sales up about 37% and profit up about 45%, in line with what management promised in June. Its credit rating was upgraded. It also signed a ₹6,200 crore chip-factory MoU, which is huge for a company with ₹478 crore of yearly sales. |
| **BEL** | Sales up 25%, profit only up 8%. Its order backlog shrank slightly to ₹72,258 crore. The giant QRSAM order is still unconfirmed. |
| **HAL** | Steady: sales up about 14%, profit up about 15%. It bought out its helicopter-training partner for nothing, and its finance director retired on 30 Sep. |
| **LT** | New orders up 14%, but sales up only 7% and margin slipped from 9.9% to 9.0%. This is the "soft first half" management warned about. It won two road contracts in Dubai. |
| **GABRIEL** | Sales up about 19%, but margins are thinner. It is spending about ₹935 crore for a 30% stake in HL Klemove India, funded by ₹1,000 crore of bonds. |
| **JNKINDIA** | Profit jumped from a very small base. But its backlog fell and its first export order (UAE) was cancelled. |

## Theme Scorecard

- Defence: 9.2 → 8.5. Still strongest, a bit softer.
- Capex cycle: 7.5 → 7.0.
- Auto EV: 7.0 → 6.5.
- Exports: 6.5 → 5.0. Weakening.
- Margin expansion: 6.5 → 4.5. Dropped below the bar and excluded.

## What To Do

1. **PARAS:** worth a full stock-picker review, but find out first what its order backlog is now and how the chip project would be paid for.
2. **BEL and JNKINDIA:** keep watching. Both report Q2 results soon. Wait for those numbers.
3. **Nothing here is a buy or sell call.** The work is still the research queue.
4. **Know the gaps:** about 150 routine filings since June have not been read, and every "~" figure is computed from the filing's own prior-year column.
