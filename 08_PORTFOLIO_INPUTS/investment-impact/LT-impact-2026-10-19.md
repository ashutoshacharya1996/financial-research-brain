## Buy Blockers

1. **Cash conversion and earnings quality** — Q1 operating cash flow turned negative and profit growth leans on non-operating items.
   - Current status: group net cash from operations Rs (20.2) bn vs Rs 30.5 bn; CFO excl. FS Rs 43 bn vs Rs 58 bn; EBITDA -3% while PAT +14% (other income Rs 2,377 Cr, Nabha divestment tax effect); receivable-delay credit provisions disclosed.
   - Required for unlock: Q2 FY27 operating cash flow positive and CFO excl. FS back at or above the Rs 58 bn prior-year level, with no further receivable-delay provisions in Infra & Utilities.
   - Timeframe: Q2 FY27 results, expected late Oct / Nov 2026. Addressable.

2. **Margin and execution slippage with unverified guidance** — Revenue and margin are both moving away from the FY27 guide, and no call evidence exists.
   - Current status: revenue +7% vs a 10-12% guide; EBITDA margin 9.0% (-90 bps YoY, -140 bps vs Q4 FY26); Infra & Utilities revenue -3%, Energy-Green -11%; no numeric FY27 guidance and no Q1 call transcript.
   - Required for unlock: a transcript or Q2 print showing revenue growth at or above 10% for H1-to-date run-rate and EBITDA margin back at or above 9.9%, with guidance explicitly reiterated.
   - Timeframe: Q2 FY27 results. Addressable (call transcript is "unknowable" until it is obtained).

3. **Middle East concentration plus missing portfolio and valuation context** — Source of order strength is also the source of execution drag, and the decision cannot be tied to the portfolio.
   - Current status: International 56% of Q1 inflow, 52% of book; management cites "supply chain constraints in the Middle East" for the revenue miss; Energy-Conventional inflow -90%. No live price, holdings or slot assigned.
   - Required for unlock: International share and Middle East execution commentary stable or improving at Q2; live Kite holdings, a slot assignment and a current valuation check on file.
   - Timeframe: Q2 FY27 for the commentary; portfolio and valuation inputs can be supplied at any time.

Governance overlay is minor and not a blocker: Rs 1,400 Cr Data Center slump sale to Vyoma.AI (stated arm's length; business FY26 revenue only Rs 36.6 Cr), Realty hive-down passed with 99.07% votes. Related-party flags are worth watching, but not decisive.
