## Buy Blockers

1. **Guidance integrity (addressable, unknowable from files)** — FY27 EBITDA 28% vs 21-23% and inflow Rs 55,000 Cr vs >Rs 20,000 Cr in one transcript.
   - Current status: Unresolved; recording not in repo; audio cover letter only; PDF re-read agrees with extraction.
   - Required for unlock: Written company clarification or a listen to the recording confirming one set; ideally restated at Q2 results.
   - Timeframe: Q2 FY27 results (expected around late Oct to early Nov 2026; date not in files).

2. **Order book contraction and QRSAM non-signing (addressable)** — Book -2.2% to Rs 72,258 Cr; Q1 inflow Rs 3,754 Cr = 0.68x revenue and ~6.8% of the Rs 55,000 Cr figure (~18.8% of Rs 20,000 Cr); QRSAM past its "maximum by September" date with no signing found; post-call orders total only Rs 3,299 Cr.
   - Required for unlock: Q2 inflow at or above revenue (book stops falling), QRSAM CCS clearance, or Shatrughat/Samaghat (~Rs 9,000 Cr) conversion by about Jan 2027.
   - Timeframe: Q2 results; Jan 2027 for the 3-6 month window.

3. **Profit conversion and missing portfolio/valuation context (addressable)** — PAT +8.17% vs revenue +25.27%; EBITDA 25.83% vs ~29%; no cash flow disclosed; live price and holdings could not be pulled (Kite not logged in), so portfolio fit cannot be confirmed.

   - Timeframe: Q2 FY27 results; Kite check before next run.
