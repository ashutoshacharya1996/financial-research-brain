## Investment Impact Layer

**Growth:** Transformational if sustained — ₹50,000cr in 60 days is ~9% of the FY26 order book already; timeline to P&L is multi-year (EPC revenue recognition is percentage-of-completion), so FY27/28 guidance revision is the near-term signal to watch, not immediate revenue.

**Margins:** Unconfirmed. The prior dashboard fair-value model assumed stable ~12% EBITDA margin; Q4 FY26 showed compression to 10.4%. No new margin data exists in this extraction window — this is the single largest open question.

**Cash Conversion:** Not assessed this run — no working-capital data in the current extraction batch. EPC businesses are inherently working-capital intensive; the Realty demerger may also have transitional cash/balance-sheet effects.

**Moat:** Intact and, if anything, strengthened — winning two Middle East Ultra-Mega contracts and a first-of-kind AI-infrastructure order both require scale and credibility few Indian competitors have (per dashboard: "only 3-4 companies can execute at >₹5,000cr project scale").

**Valuation:** Last known: PE ~28×, price ₹3,620 vs base fair value ₹3,800 (fair-value zone, ~5% margin of safety) as of 2026-06-20. This predates the order surge and needs a live refresh before any conviction change.

   - Required for unlock: Q2 FY27 results (due ~Oct/Nov 2026) showing EBITDA margin back toward or above 12%
   - Timeframe: Next quarterly earnings

2. **Realty demerger execution risk** — NCLT hearing scheduled Oct 6, 2026; the process is not yet complete and could introduce transitional accounting or governance complexity.
   - Required for unlock: NCLT approval and clean execution of the demerger

3. **Valuation is stale** — last dashboard fair-value assessment (PE 28×, fair-value zone) is dated 2026-06-20, before this order surge; the market may have already repriced LT upward, eroding the margin of safety.
   - Required for unlock: Live price/valuation refresh
