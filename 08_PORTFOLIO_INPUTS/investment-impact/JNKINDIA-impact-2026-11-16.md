## Opportunity Signal

**What changed this week:**
JNKINDIA received a "Major" (~₹500cr per management) heater-package order for a Nigeria project on 2026-11-03. The order came from promoter JNK Global, is a related-party transaction and is back-to-back. It follows the 2026-07-16 cancellation of the independent UAE/ADNOC TA'ZIZ order and a Q2 FY27 print with revenue up 3.4% and the order book down to ₹1,662cr.

**Why it matters:**
The Nigeria order rebuilds the pro forma order book to about ₹2,162cr (derived: ₹1,662cr + ~₹500cr, about 2.4x TTM revenue). It is described as "the largest export order on us till date", so the June export thesis has been restored in size. In quality it has been weakened: the only independent export customer was lost, and management states "All export projects ... JNK Global will be getting the orders and they will be subcontracting to us. It is a back-to-back basis." Meanwhile the linked capex-cycle theme was downgraded to Declining and the opportunity to Monitoring (confidence 7.5 → 6.0; JNKINDIA fit 7.0 → 4.5).

**Evidence quality:**
- Source(s): Reg 30 Nigeria order filing (2026-11-03); Reg 30 UAE cancellation (2026-07-16); Q2 FY27 press release, investor presentation and call (2026-11-06; transcript filed 2026-11-13); board outcome / CFO change (2026-11-05); CRISIL rating letter (2026-09-24); Q1 FY27 PR/deck/call (2026-08-11/12); FY26 PR (2026-05-20); FY26 Annual Report (2026-09-03). All tier_1.
- Strength: Concrete (the order is filed) but **related-party**, so as evidence of independent export demand it is Emerging at best
- First mention date: 2026-11-03 (Nigeria order); the export thesis was first filed 2026-06-08 (UAE, since cancelled)
- Prior mentions: Recurring. The UAE order was filed 06-08 and cancelled 07-16. The Q1 call (2026-08-12) said all exports route via JNK Global. The Q2 call (2026-11-06) gave the Nigeria value and the Kenya/Dangote pipeline.

## Fact Check

**What we assume this signal implies:**
The working assumption is that export orders are scaling, the order book is rebuilding after two quarterly declines, and H2 FY27 execution can deliver the maintained 20–25% revenue / 12–14% EBITDA guidance.

**What we verified:**
- **Nigeria order is filed.** The 2026-11-03 Reg 30 filing reads: "received a Major* order from JNK Global Co., Ltd., Korea ... Heater Package for an overseas project in Nigeria"; "JNK Global is one of the promoters of the Company"; "The order will fall within related party transactions and the same is at 'arm's length basis'"; "18 Months for FOB delivery". The value is not in the filing. "~₹500cr" comes from the Q2 call, and management admitted the filed 300–600 range was an "error".
- **UAE order cancelled.** The 2026-07-16 filing reads: "informing that the aforesaid order stands cancelled", with "not receiving approval from the project licensor" as the reason, and "no impact on declared revenue and associated profit".
- **Order book declined, as filed:** ₹1,961.4cr (31-Mar-26) → ₹1,801cr (30-Jun) → ₹1,662cr (30-Sep), which is −15.3%. FY27 inflow to 5 Nov is "~530" (≈₹500cr Nigeria + ₹30cr flares).
- **Q2 FY27:** total operational revenue ₹187.5cr (+3.4% YoY), EBITDA ₹20.8cr (11.1%), PAT ₹13.3cr (+2.0%). H1 revenue was ₹370.2cr (+30.4%) at a 10.6% EBITDA margin.
- **Guidance maintained:** "we continue to maintain our FY27 guidance of 20%-25% revenue growth and in EBITDA margin of 12%-14% on the operational revenue." The derived H2 requirement is ₹627–669cr (FY26 ₹830.8cr × 1.20–1.25, minus H1 ₹370.2cr). That is +14.7% to +22.3% vs H2 FY26 ₹546.9cr and about 63–64% of the year.
- **Cash and working capital (Sep-26 vs Mar-26):** cash & equivalents ₹6.9cr vs ₹32.1cr; trade receivables ₹398.5cr vs ₹352.7cr (+13%); H1 operating cash flow ₹2.8cr vs ₹10.3cr; trade payables ₹155.6cr vs ₹191.3cr.
- **Credit:** CRISIL A-/Stable (outlook raised from Negative); facilities ₹604cr from ₹457cr (2026-09-24).
- **CFO change:** interim CFO Anand Agarwal resigned effective 2026-11-05 and Praveen Sood was appointed from 2026-11-12. He is the third CFO since Dec-2025 (Pravin Sathe resigned 2025-12-16).

**Signal flags (Step 2):**
- The order is stated in a filing, not inferred. ✔
- **Contradictory evidence exists.** The independent export order was cancelled, and the replacement is related-party. The 2026-08-14 corporate deck still shows "14-15% / 25-30%" guidance against the 12–14% / 20–25% stated on both calls. ⚑
- Freshness: the order is 13 days old and the results are 10 days old. ✔

**What we still don't know:**
- Margin, advance and payment terms on the Nigeria order. Management: "due date starts only from the receipt of the advance", and the advance had not been received as of 2026-11-06.
- Whether JNKINDIA can win **any** export order that is not routed through the promoter. Candidates: Kenya inquiries (orders "in four to five months") and Dangote balance scope ("we are not confirming definitely it will come or not").
- Receivable ageing at Sep-26 (not disclosed). At Mar-26, 35.5% of standalone gross receivables were more than 1 year past due.
- The status of the JNK Global activist dispute ("sub judice").

## Investment Impact Layer

This layer assesses whether the opportunity signal requires deeper underwriting.

### Growth Impact
- **Current growth trajectory:**
  - FY26 total operational revenue was ₹830.8cr (FY26 total revenue incl. other income ₹838.0cr, +68.0%).
  - H1 FY27 was +30.4%, but Q2 slowed to +3.4%.
  - TTM total operational revenue is ₹917.1cr (derived).
  - Guidance is FY27 +20–25%, which implies ~₹997–1,039cr (derived).
- **Impact if signal plays out:** ~₹500cr over 18 months is roughly ₹330cr/yr of execution capacity (derived), about 36% of TTM revenue. It supports FY28 visibility; management says "about INR1,500 crores of revenue ... available for the execution in the next financial year". The order offsets but does not reverse the decline: the pro forma book of ~₹2,162cr is only ~10% above March-26's ₹1,961cr, and that gap is entirely the related-party order.
- **Strength of impact:** Meaningful for revenue visibility, Marginal for thesis quality, because it does not prove independent export demand
- **Timeline:** Revenue starts only after the advance is received. Most of the impact falls in FY28 (Q1–Q4). The near-term test is Q3 FY27 (Oct–Dec), where management already flagged "October, November, we don't improve so much".
- **Confidence:** Medium. The order is concrete, but its timing depends on the advance and on a promoter counterparty.

### Margin Impact
- **Current margin regime:** EBITDA is now reported excluding other income.
  - FY26: 12.5%. H1 FY27: 10.6%. Q2 FY27: 11.1%. Both H1 and Q2 are below the 12–14% guide floor.
  - FY26 PAT margin was 7.7%; H1 FY27 was 6.2%.
  - Employee cost rose 46% YoY in Q2 (₹21.0cr vs ₹14.4cr), and finance cost rose 75% (₹4.9cr vs ₹2.8cr).
- **Impact if signal plays out:** The order's margin is unknown, and the back-to-back structure means the promoter sits between JNKINDIA and the end customer. To reach the 12% floor for FY27, H2 needs a ~12.8% EBITDA margin; reaching 14% needs ~15.9% (derived). Management also flags commodity risk: "copper, nickel, or any other aluminum fluctuating, that's a business risk".
- **Strength of impact:** Marginal. There is no evidence of ≥100bps margin lift.

### Cash Conversion
- **Working capital profile:**
  - DSO is ~161 days (derived: receivables ₹398.5cr ÷ TTM revenue from operations ₹905.1cr × 365). It was ~157 days at Mar-26, and the standalone FY26 receivable turnover of 2.37x implies ~154 days.
  - Cash ₹32.1cr → ₹6.9cr. Other bank balances rose to ₹212.4cr; whether these are liened as BG margin money is not disclosed.
  - H1 operating cash flow ₹2.8cr against H1 PAT ₹22.9cr, a conversion of ~0.12x.
  - Working capital consumed ₹14.1cr in H1, and taxes paid were ₹15.2cr.
  - Borrowings ₹88.1cr vs ₹74.1cr.
  - IPO proceeds (₹281.7cr) were fully used, ₹264.7cr of it for working capital.
- **Impact if signal plays out:** The order is FOB, advance-triggered and 18 months long. The ₹604cr CRISIL limits (₹524cr of them LC/BG) indicate that bonding capacity is being expanded to support bidding. No capex is required: management states no capacity expansion and "no such plan" for fund-raising.
- **Risk:** High
- **Recommendation:** Cash conversion is the primary blocker. Portfolio fit cannot be assessed until Q3 FY27 operating cash flow and receivables are seen, and a live portfolio check is needed before any fit stage.

### Moat / Competitive Position
- **Is this defensible?** Partly. JNKINDIA is a specialist in fired heaters and combustion equipment; the Combustion segment is ~92% of H1 segment revenue. Management reports a ~30% heater bid-to-win ratio. The UAE cancellation itself shows that licensor technical approval acts as an entry barrier, and JNKINDIA did not hold that approval.
- **Is it repeatable?** Not shown independently. Nigeria is "a repeat order which we had successfully executed 10 years back", placed on JNK Global. Repeatability therefore runs through the promoter, not JNKINDIA's own customer relationships. The non-heater win rate is lower, at 10–15%.
- **Is it at risk?** Yes:
  - Export dependence on a promoter entity that is in a "sub judice" activist dispute.
  - The JNK Global cooperation agreement was "not yet renewed" formally (per the delta).
  - Commodity pass-through risk.
  - The Chemdist / green-hydrogen timeline has been pushed out "about two years". Its order book is ~₹20cr, and it was 7.7% of H1 revenue against a 10–15% expectation.

### Valuation Lens
- **Current valuation** at ₹366.55 (yfinance 2026-11-15, tier_3). Arithmetic:
  - Market cap ₹2,055.5cr (yfinance) ÷ ₹366.55 → ~5.61cr shares (FV ₹2).
  - TTM EPS = FY26 ₹11.61 − H1 FY26 ₹2.56 + H1 FY27 ₹4.41 = **₹13.46** → **P/E 27.2x** (yfinance shows 27.1x). On FY26 EPS the P/E is 31.6x.
  - P/B = ₹2,055.5cr ÷ equity ₹591.3cr (Sep-26) = **3.5x**.
  - EV = mcap + debt ₹88.1cr − cash ₹6.9cr = ₹2,136.7cr → **EV/Sales 2.33x** (TTM ₹917.1cr) and **EV/EBITDA 18.2x** (TTM ₹117.3cr). If other bank balances of ₹212.4cr are treated as free cash, EV is ₹1,924.3cr, giving 2.10x and 16.4x.
- **FY27 earnings range on guidance** (derived):
  - Revenue ₹997–1,039cr × PAT margin 6.2% (H1 FY27) to 7.7% (FY26) = PAT ₹61.8–80.0cr.
  - That is EPS ₹11.0–14.3, so the forward P/E is **25.7x–33.3x** at ₹366.55.
- **Fair value implied by signal:** The filings give no basis for a multiple, so the following is an analyst assumption, not filed. Applying 20–25x, which is below the current multiple because growth has stalled and the export channel is related-party, to the top of the FY27 EPS range (₹14.3) gives ~₹286–358. At the bottom (₹11.0) it gives ~₹220–275.
- **Current price vs. fair value:** At fair value to a premium. Even the top-of-guide case gives ≤₹358, against ₹366.55.
- **Context:** The price is −23% vs ₹475.50 on 2026-06-28, −24% vs ₹479.45 on 2026-06-22 and −34% vs the 52-week high of ₹558. The de-rating from P/E ~41 to ~27 followed lower growth, so it does not create a margin of safety.
- **Valuation risk:** Medium–High

## Underwriting Blockers

These are specific reasons the stock is not ready for underwriting now, even if the opportunity signal is real. The section maps to the template's blocker section; it is renamed to avoid transaction language.

1. **Cash conversion is not working** (Addressable, within 1–2 quarters)
   - Current status:
     - H1 FY27 operating cash flow ₹2.8cr vs PAT ₹22.9cr (0.12x) and vs ₹10.3cr in H1 FY26.
     - Cash ₹32.1cr → ₹6.9cr. Receivables ₹352.7cr → ₹398.5cr (+13% in 6 months), about 161 DSO (derived).
     - Borrowings ₹74.1cr → ₹88.1cr, and Q2 finance cost +75% YoY.
     - 35.5% of Mar-26 standalone receivables (₹108.7cr of ₹306.2cr) were more than 1 year past due.
   - Required for unlock:
     - FY27 operating cash flow at least 0.5x PAT, with H2 alone ≥₹30cr.
     - Receivables growth below revenue growth, so DSO trends below ~150 days.
     - Sep-26 or Dec-26 receivable ageing disclosed, with the >1-year bucket not rising.
   - Timeframe: Q3 FY27 results (expected ~Feb 2027) for receivables/DSO; FY27 annual cash flow (May 2027) for the cash-flow test.

2. **Order quality and book replenishment** (Addressable, ~2 quarters)
   - Current status:
     - The book fell 15.3% (₹1,961.4cr → ₹1,662cr) over two quarters.
     - FY27 YTD inflow is ~₹530cr, of which ~94% (~₹500cr) is the single related-party order. That order is ~23% of the ~₹2,162cr pro forma book.
     - The independent UAE order (₹100–300cr class) was cancelled.
     - The bid pipeline fell from ~₹6,000cr to ~₹5,000cr.
     - H2 must deliver ₹627–669cr (+14.7% to +22.3% YoY) to meet guidance, after ₹30–40cr slipped in Q2.
   - Required for unlock:
     - At least one export or large domestic order **not** routed via JNK Global (Kenya, Dangote balance, NTPC bid due 2026-11-20, or the ~₹1,500cr of non-heater bids).
     - A Dec-26 order book ex-related-party at or above ₹1,662cr.
     - Q3 FY27 revenue on a run-rate consistent with ₹627–669cr for H2.
   - Timeframe: Q3 FY27 results (~Feb 2027). Dangote/Kenya windows run Jan–Apr 2027.

3. **Governance and disclosure quality** (mix of Unknowable and Addressable)
   - Current status:
     - Third CFO since Dec-2025: the interim CFO lasted ~8 months (2026-03-12 → 2026-11-05), and the departure was disclosed alongside the Q2 results.
     - Conflicting guidance documents: the 2026-08-14 deck shows 25–30% / 14–15%, the calls state 20–25% / 12–14%, and the CEO said "we'll recheck them".
     - The original Q1 standalone deck was wrong and was revised on 2026-08-12, and the Nigeria filing range was described as an "error".
     - All exports depend on a promoter entity whose board faces a "sub judice" activist dispute. 7.26% of votes were against the AGM material RPT item.
   - Required for unlock:
     - The new CFO stays through FY27 results.
     - A single reconciled guidance statement.
     - Nigeria order terms (advance, margin) disclosed.
     - Clarity on the JNK Global dispute and renewal of the cooperation agreement.
   - Timeframe: Q3 FY27 call for CFO commentary and guidance. The JNK Global dispute is **Unknowable** without management access.

Secondary (not ranked above): valuation is 27.2x TTM P/E and 18.2x EV/EBITDA for a business whose Q2 growth was +3.4%, and it sits at or above the 20–25x assumption-based range even on top-of-guide EPS.

## What Would Change This Decision?

If we saw evidence of **(a) confirmed portfolio context (Kite holdings and slot) AND (b) Q3 FY27 showing H2 tracking ₹627–669cr, receivables growing slower than revenue, and at least one independent (non-JNK Global) order**, this impact note would shift to **Upgrade Watchlist Priority**.

- If both (b) and a full-year operating cash flow of at least 0.5x PAT are shown, with the new CFO in place and guidance reconciled, flag for a **Re-run India Stock Picker (Deep Dive)**. That still requires portfolio context.
- If Q3 FY27 order book ex-related-party falls below ₹1,500cr (the opportunity record's thesis breaker), or cash declines further with receivables still rising, or guidance is cut, shift to **Ignore For Now**.
- If the JNK Global dispute disrupts the export channel, shift to **Ignore For Now**.
