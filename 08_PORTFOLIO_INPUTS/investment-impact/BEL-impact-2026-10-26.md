## Fact Check

**What we assume this signal implies:**
Defence order flow is broad, cash conversion is improving, and a QRSAM award would lift the order book sharply.

**What we verified:**
- Revenue: "INR5,533 crores ... as compared to INR4,417 crores ... growth of 25.27" (call). PAT only +8.17%.
- EBITDA margin: "The EBITDA margin up to Q1 is 25.83%." Management: product mix, "not due to input cost".
- Receivables: "around 140 days. It was 176 days as on 31st March."
- Order book fell Rs 73,882 cr to Rs 72,258 cr; seven releases sum to Rs 4,882 cr.
- No QRSAM award or CCS filing in extracted data through 2026-10-25, after the "maximum by September" date.

**What we still don't know (OPEN, not resolved):**
- Guidance conflict, same call: Q&A "we will cross 28%" EBITDA and "INR55,000 plus crores" inflow vs closing remark "EBITDA margin 21% to 23%" and "more than INR20,000 crores". Which is operative is unknown.
- Q1 inflow conflict: "INR3,754 crores" vs "INR307 plus crores".
- Whether QRSAM is inside or additive to the Rs 72,258 cr book; BEL share (~70%, ~Rs 21,000 cr) is an analyst estimate.
