## Investment Impact Layer

This layer assesses whether the opportunity signal requires deeper underwriting. It is built only from the extracted record; no market data was available.

### Growth Impact
- **Current growth trajectory:** Q1 revenue +7% YoY (Q4 FY26 +11%, FY26 +12%); FY27 guide last cited in June as 10-12% for both revenue and inflow (not restated in Q1). Order inflow +14% is above that range; revenue is below it.
- **Impact if signal plays out:** Rs 7.79 trn book on Q1 revenue of Rs 67,942 cr annualises to roughly 2.9 years of book-to-revenue cover (simple arithmetic on Q1 x4 = about Rs 2.72 trn). For FY27 revenue to land at +10% from Rs 2.86 trn (FY26), the remaining three quarters must grow about +10.1% YoY on average; for +12%, about +12.1% (arithmetic from the stated Q1 base: Q1 FY26 Rs 63,679 cr, FY26 about Rs 2.86 trn). Since Q2 is also guided "softer", H2 would have to run above the guided range, the same H2-dependent shape management described.
- **Strength of impact:** Meaningful (demand), but conversion-limited. Conventional Energy inflow was -90% YoY (Rs 3,053 cr) on "deferment of certain anticipated orders".
- **Timeline:** Evidence in P&L at Q2 FY27 results (date not in record; late Oct / Nov typical) and Q3.

### Margin Impact
- **Current margin regime:** Group EBITDA 9.0% (-140 bps QoQ, -90 bps YoY). Segments: I&U 5.1% (5.5%), Conv. Energy 7.6% (7.5%), Green 6.0% (6.1%), Manufacturing & Products 15.2% (17.5%, -230 bps), TPS 19.2% (19.5%), Realty 36.9% (48.8%, -1,190 bps).
- **Impact if signal plays out:** Operating leverage from a higher execution run-rate could recover margin toward the Q4 level of 10.4% (about +140 bps), but management attributes Q1 compression to "lower execution levels, forex variation in IT subsidiaries and higher ECL provisions", and the ECL element is a credit cost, not purely volume. Mix is shifting to I&U and offshore wind, where segment margins are lower than Manufacturing & Products.
- **Strength of impact:** Marginal-to-Meaningful and unproven. The June "PP&M ~7.8% flattish" reference cannot be tracked because segments were realigned from 2026-04-01 (unreconciled).

### Cash Conversion
- **Working capital profile:** NWC 4.9% of revenue (10.1% a year ago); net debt/equity 0.39 (0.35 at Mar-26); gross debt/equity 1.01 (0.95); CFO excl. Financial Services Rs 43 bn vs Rs 58 bn. Financial Services book Rs 1,29,634 cr (+27% YoY).

1. **Conversion gap, revenue and EBITDA** — Revenue growth below guided run-rate and operating profit falling despite record book.
   - Current status: Revenue +7% YoY vs 10-12% FY27 range last cited; EBITDA Rs 6,116 cr, -3% YoY; margin 9.0% vs 10.4% (Q4 FY26) and 9.9% (Q1 FY26); PAT +14% flattered by finance cost Rs 539 cr vs Rs 782 cr and divestment-related items.
   - Required for unlock: Q2 FY27 revenue growth at or above +10% YoY and EBITDA margin back at or above 10.0% (current 9.0%; Capex record thesis breaker is "single-digit growth with margin below 9%").
   - Timeframe: Q2 FY27 results (late Oct to Nov 2026); classification: addressable.

2. **Receivable and cash-flow quality** — New ECL provisions on delayed collections plus falling operating cash.
   - Current status: I&U margin 5.1% (5.5% YoY) explicitly hit by "expected delays in collection of receivables"; CFO excl. Financial Services Rs 43 bn vs Rs 58 bn (-26% YoY); net debt/equity 0.39 vs 0.35 at Mar-26; ECL quantum not extracted.
   - Required for unlock: ECL provision amount disclosed and not rising QoQ; CFO excl. FS at or above the Rs 58 bn prior-year level in H1; net debt/equity not above 0.39.
   - Timeframe: Q2 FY27 results; classification: addressable (quantum is unknown until the PDF is read).

3. **Missing management call content and numeric FY27 guidance** — The June guidance and the "H2 pickup as Middle East normalises" condition are neither reaffirmed nor revised, and the written Outlook adds "could interrupt the growth momentum".
   - Current status: 0 of 3 Q1 call artefacts contain content (transcript, audio, and no Q1FY27 folder); 0 numeric guidance lines in the Q1 pack and presentation; West Asia/supply-chain language present in 3 separate Q1 passages.
   - Required for unlock: Extracted Q1 and Q2 transcripts with explicit FY27 revenue/inflow/margin guidance, or numeric guidance in the Q2 pack.
   - Timeframe: Next extraction cycle for Q1 transcript (cheap fix); Q2 call for guidance; classification: addressable (and unknowable on the guidance until stated).
