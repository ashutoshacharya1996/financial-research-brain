## Opportunity Signal

**What changed this week:**
Q1 FY27 order inflows rose 14% to Rs 1,08,014 cr and the order book hit a record Rs 7,78,954 cr, but revenue grew only 7% against a 10-12% guide and EBITDA margin fell 90 bps to 9.0%.

**Why it matters:**
LT is the bellwether for the Capex theme, which fell from 7.5 to 6.3 (Weakening) largely on this miss. Strong inflows with weak conversion means the thesis depends on execution catching up, tested by the Q2 call on 27 Oct 2026.

## Fact Check

**What we assume this signal implies:**
Record backlog converts to revenue in H2, margins recover, and working capital stays tight.

**What we verified:**
- Revenue: Rs 67,942 cr, +7% YoY (Q1 FY26 Rs 63,679 cr). EBITDA Rs 6,116 cr vs Rs 6,318 cr ("(3%)"); margin 9.0% vs 9.9%.
- Cause stated: "lower execution levels, forex variation in IT subsidiaries and higher ECL provisions"; "expected delays in collection of receivables".
- NWC 4.9% of revenue vs 10.1% a year earlier ("520 bps YoY improvement"); CFO (excl. FS) fell to Rs 43 bn from Rs 58 bn.
- Outlook: "extended disruption in supply chains and elevated energy prices could interrupt the growth momentum."

**What we still don't know (OPEN, not resolved):**
- FY27 guidance of 10-12% is not restated and not withdrawn; Q1 release says "No numeric guidance".
- Whether Limited Notices to Proceed (Lara III, Chandrapura, Tashkent) are counted in the Rs 1,08,014 cr inflow.
- PAT +14% bridge (other income, exceptionals) is not itemised.
- Q1 call transcript absent; management tone rests on release and deck.
