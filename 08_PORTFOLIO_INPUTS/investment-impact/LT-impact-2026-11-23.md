## Opportunity Signal

**What changed this week:**
LT booked a record Q2 FY27 order inflow of ₹1,47,665cr (+28% YoY). That took H1 inflow to ₹2,55,678cr (+22% vs a 10–12% FY27 guide) and the order book to ₹8.535trn (+28%), while net working capital fell to a "record low" 1.7% of revenue. Over the same window, EBITDA margin fell for a third straight quarter to 8.7%.

**Why it matters:**
The June worry ("H1 FY27 softer due to execution headwinds") was confirmed in Q1 (revenue +7%) but reversed earlier than guided in Q2 (revenue +16%). Demand and cash generation are now both proven in tier-1 documents, and India's largest EPC is the main carrier of the Capex Cycle theme (7.0) and the Working Capital theme (5.2). The open question has moved from "will orders come?" to "will orders convert into operating profit?". That is a valuation and quality-gate question, which is exactly what the India Stock Picker is built to answer.

## Fact Check

**What we assume this signal implies:**
Order intake is running well ahead of guidance, revenue visibility is multi-year (~2.8x TTM revenue), and working capital is releasing cash. If margins stabilise, earnings should compound off a larger base.

**What we verified:**
- Record inflow is stated verbatim: "Record Order Inflows at ₹ 147,665 crore, up 28%" (Q2 results).
- NWC is stated verbatim: "NWC% TO REVENUE 1.7% 850 bps YoY improvement ... A Record Low" (Q2 deck). It was 4.9% in Jun-26 and 10.2% in Sep-25.
- EBITDA margin is stated: Annexure 1 shows "10.0% 8.7% EBITDA %" (Q2 FY26 vs Q2 FY27). Q1 FY27 was 9.0% and Q4 FY26 10.4%.
- The prospects pipeline is stated: "~ Rs 15 trillion" (Q1 deck), then "~ Rs 10 trillion" (Q2 deck).
- International share is stated: "International orders stood at ₹ 92,375 crore, contributing 63% to the total order inflow" (Q2). It is 54% of the order book.
- PAT quality was checked. Q2 PAT was ₹5,202cr (+33%) against EBITDA +1%. Other income was ₹2,306cr (+67%, including a "one-time gain on divestment of SWC business in LTTS"), finance cost fell 38%, and there was a deferred-tax credit of ₹337cr.

**What we still don't know:**
- **FY27 guidance status.** No Q1 or Q2 FY27 earnings-call transcript was collected, so whether the 10–12% revenue and inflow guide or the ~7.8% PP&M margin guide was revised is not found.
- **Margin on the record orders.** The ultra-mega Middle East and LNTP awards carry undisclosed margins. I&U cites "a higher proportion of jobs below margin recognition threshold", which may reverse as jobs mature (unverified).
- **How much of the NWC release is customer advances** on fresh ultra-mega orders rather than better collections. H1 ECL rose 26% to ₹2,353cr.

## Investment Impact Layer

This layer assesses whether the opportunity signal requires deeper underwriting.

### Growth Impact
- **Current growth trajectory:** H1 FY27 revenue ₹1,46,692cr (+11%; Q1 +7%, Q2 +16%), inside the 10–12% FY27 guide. H1 inflow +22%, about 10 points above the guide. Order book ₹8.535trn ≈ 2.8x TTM revenue (derived).
- **Impact if signal plays out:** The order book supports revenue growth at or above the top of the 10–12% guide into FY28. Lakshya 2031 targets a 12–15% revenue CAGR (June evidence). New segments appearing in order flow (offshore-wind HVDC, BESS, AI data centre, PT&D) widen the addressable market.
- **Strength of impact:** Meaningful
- **Timeline:** Q3 FY27 (late Jan 2027) and Q4 FY27 (May 2027) revenue prints. Ultra-mega orders take 2–4 years to execute.

### Margin Impact
- **Current margin regime:** EBITDA 8.7% (Q2 FY27) vs 10.0% (Q2 FY26); H1 8.8% vs 10.0%. PP&M is 7.0% in H1 vs the ~7.8% FY27 guide. CarbonLite is at 5.8% vs 19.0%. In Q2, margins fell YoY in I&U, Energy-Conventional, Energy-Green and TPS; only M&P rose.
- **Impact if signal plays out:** Record inflow is mix-dilutive near term (a higher PP&M share and early-stage jobs below the margin-recognition threshold). Each 100 bp of EBITDA margin on ~₹3,00,900cr TTM revenue is roughly ₹3,000cr of EBITDA (derived). Margin direction is the swing factor for earnings, not order volume.
- **Strength of impact:** Meaningful (currently negative; could turn if jobs cross the recognition threshold)

### Cash Conversion
- **Working capital profile:** NWC/revenue is 1.7% (Sep-26), vs 4.9% (Jun-26) and 10.2% (Sep-25). Q2 CFO ex-FS was ₹128bn (+170%) and H1 ₹180bn (+70%). H1 consolidated OCF was ₹5,750.53cr vs ₹3,816.13cr. Net D/E is 0.33 and cash plus current investments ₹974bn. Moody's assigned Baa1/Stable, two notches above the sovereign.
- **Impact if signal plays out:** Advances on ultra-mega orders fund execution with no balance-sheet strain. Collection delays in I&U (state/utility clients) are the offset.
- **Risk:** Low (watch ECL, which is ₹2,353cr in H1, +26%)
- **Recommendation:** Needs live portfolio check before portfolio fit stage.

### Moat / Competitive Position
- **Is this defensible?** Yes, on scale. LT wins ultra-mega international orders directly (three Middle East ultra-megas in Aug-2026, the TenneT 2 GW HVDC framework with Hitachi Energy) and has an investment-grade balance sheet that few Indian EPCs can match. The game-theory view in the capex theme record says "Scale is winning the order flow".
- **Is it repeatable?** Yes. Inflow set records in both Q1 (+14%) and Q2 (+28%), across Middle East hydrocarbon, power T&D, renewables and AI infrastructure.
- **Is it at risk?** Partly. Customers hold pricing power: record inflow arrived alongside the lowest EBITDA margin in the tracked series. Middle East concentration is rising, and management flags "varied downside risks". Several large wins are LNTP or conditional.

### Valuation Lens
- **Current valuation:** Kite LTP 2026-11-23 ₹4,192.10. Trailing P/E ~32.3x (derived from 32.4x at the ₹4,209.1 close on 2026-11-22, `prices-2026-11-22.json`). The 52-week range is ₹3,288.1–4,440.0. P/E compressed from 35.9x (June 22) as EPS rose. The price fell ~13.7% to a mid-October trough, then recovered ~16% after Q2.
- **Fair value implied by signal:** Not determinable from current evidence. There is no LT India Stock Picker review or dashboard. Fair value depends on the margin path, which is the open question.
- **Current price vs. fair value:** Unknown. This is the ISP's job.
- **Valuation risk:** Medium. ~32x trailing for a business growing 11% with a falling margin needs either a margin recovery or acceleration to justify it. Part of the trailing EPS is below the line (other income, tax).

### Portfolio Fit
- **Overlap with existing holds?** LT is **not held** (Kite holdings 2026-11-23, 43 positions). No infra/EPC name is held directly. There is partial adjacency with **BHEL** (50 sh; capital goods/power; overlaps LT's thermal LNTPs and power EPC) and **BEML** (22 sh; capital goods/defence). **TATAPOWER** (63) and **WAAREEENER** (5) overlap LT's Energy-Green leg only loosely.
- **Sector balance impact:** This would raise capital-goods exposure beyond BHEL and BEML, but it would be the only diversified EPC/infra holding. Defence exposure is not materially changed (LT's defence leg is indirect).
- **Slot available?** Yes. The large-cap infra/EPC slot is open. Whether it is a Core or Extended-watch slot is for the Financial Advisor / Portfolio Fit stage. Holdings context is confirmed, which satisfies Rule 7 for Deep Dive routing.

## Buy Blockers

List 2–3 specific reasons NOT to buy this stock right now, even if the opportunity signal is real:

1. **Margin compression for three quarters running** — Operating margin is falling while orders hit records.
   - Current status: EBITDA margin 10.4% (Q4 FY26) → 9.0% (Q1 FY27) → 8.7% (Q2 FY27), vs 10.0% in Q2 FY26. PP&M is 7.0% in H1 vs the ~7.8% guide. Q2 EBITDA grew +1% YoY against inflow +28%.
   - Required for unlock: Q3 FY27 EBITDA margin back to ≥9.5%, or PP&M ≥7.5%, with management attributing the improvement to jobs crossing the margin-recognition threshold.
   - Timeframe: Q3 FY27 results (late January 2027). **Addressable.**

2. **Earnings quality: PAT growth is below the line** — Reported PAT growth overstates operating progress.
   - Current status: Q2 PAT was +33% vs EBITDA +1%. Other income was ₹2,306cr (+67%, including a one-time LTTS SWC divestment gain), finance cost fell 38%, and there was a ₹337cr deferred-tax credit.
   - Required for unlock: Q3 FY27 EBITDA growth ≥ revenue growth, with PAT growth no more than ~5 points above EBITDA growth.
   - Timeframe: Q3 FY27 results (late January 2027). **Addressable.**

3. **Middle East concentration and unconfirmed guidance** — The growth driver is also the main risk, and the guide has not been verified.
   - Current status: 63% of Q2 inflow and 54% of the order book are international, and Q2 outlook says "The Middle East continue to pose varied downside risks". The prospects pipeline fell from ~₹15trn to ~₹10trn. FY27 guidance status is not found because transcripts were not collected.
   - Required for unlock: collect the 2026-10-27 Q2 call transcript and confirm the FY27 guide and Middle East margin commentary. Domestic share of inflow should hold at ≥35% in Q3.
   - Timeframe: transcript collection on the next run (immediate). The concentration itself is **structural** to LT's strategy and is a sizing input, not a gate.
