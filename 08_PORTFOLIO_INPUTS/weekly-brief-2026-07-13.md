## Jargon Glossary

| Term used | What it actually means |
|---|---|
| Credit rating (Moody's Baa1) | A grade from an independent agency on how likely a company is to pay back its debts on time. Higher grades mean lower borrowing costs. |
| Sovereign rating | The credit rating of the Indian government itself. A company rated above it is unusual — it means the agency trusts that company's finances more than the country's. |
| EBITDA margin | How much profit a company keeps after paying its running costs, before tax. |
| Order book | The total value of contracts a company has won but hasn't finished delivering yet. |
| Corroboration count | How many companies mentioned the same theme independently — the more companies, the more likely it's a real trend and not a one-off. |
