## What the Engine Found This Week

### Research Queue — 10 Stocks Worth Your Time

These are not buy calls. Think of this as the engine saying "these are the restaurants worth putting on your shortlist this month." You still need to go eat there yourself.

**1. Larsen & Toubro (LT)**
*Why it made the list:* Picture a builder whose phone won't stop ringing, but who earns less on every job. L&T signed a record ₹1.48 lakh crore of work in one quarter, while its profit per ₹100 of sales slipped for a third quarter (to ₹8.70).
*What this means:* Demand isn't the problem. Turning work into profit is.

**2. Bharat Electronics (BEL)**
*Why it made the list:* Think of a bakery selling bread faster than ever while fewer new orders come in. BEL's sales jumped 26% but its pile of signed work shrank 7%.
*What this means:* It needs about ₹45,000 crore of new contracts by March to hit its own target.

**3. Paras Defence (PARAS)**
*Why it made the list:* Like a student who promised straight A's and is getting them. Paras promised 30–40% growth in June, is growing 40%, and is now collecting real cash.
*What this means:* Small shareholders voted down a deal with a company the boss is linked to, and Paras hasn't shared its order backlog since June.

**4. Hindustan Aeronautics (HAL)**
*Why it made the list:* Like an airline beating its passenger targets but not showing anyone its new planes. HAL is growing faster than promised (+15%), but nothing confirms that the new fighter jets due in Aug/Sep were delivered.
*What this means:* The numbers are good, but key facts are missing.

**5. Gabriel India (GABRIEL)**
*Why it made the list:* June's star player got injured. Its sunroof business's profit fell about 70%, and it borrowed ₹1,000 crore to purchase a stake from its own owners.
*What this means:* Sales still grow, but June's reasons for excitement have reversed.

**6. JNK India (JNKINDIA)**
*Why it made the list:* Picture a shop whose biggest new customer walked out. JNK's UAE order was cancelled, its backlog shrank 15%, and it lowered its growth target while calling it "maintained."
*What this means:* It stays on the list as a warning sign.

### The 3 Big Ideas This Week

These cleared a higher bar — the engine has real evidence, not just mentions.

---

**Idea #1: Defence — Factories Busy, New Contracts Late**

*What's happening, in plain English:*
Picture a packed restaurant still waiting on a huge catering booking that keeps getting pushed back. BEL, HAL and Paras are delivering 15–40% more than last year, but the big new contracts keep slipping.

*Why now specifically:*
- A ₹20,000 crore warship-electronics deal (NGC) was cleared, and BEL expects it to arrive by March.
- BEL's yearly target needs about ₹45,000 crore more in new contracts by March.

*The companies to look at:*
BEL, HAL, PARAS.
BEL is the biggest test. HAL builds the aircraft. Paras makes the optics that go inside.

*What could go wrong:*
- The missile contract (QRSAM) has slipped three times.
- HAL and Paras both have warning signs about who checks the bosses.

*How confident is the engine:* 7.4/10, down from 9.0. It's still real, but less convincing than in June. It is now "monitoring", not "active".

*Your next move:* Watch BEL's NSE announcements for any single order above ₹5,000 crore before 31 March.

---

**Idea #2: India's Build-Everything Boom**

*What's happening, in plain English:*
Think of a town where everyone renovates at once. Builders are swamped, but they keep less per job. L&T just had its biggest order quarter ever, with about 60% of it from abroad.

*Why now specifically:*
- L&T has ₹8.5 lakh crore of signed work waiting.
- L&T's late-January results will show whether profit per job stops shrinking.

*The companies to look at:*
LT, as India's biggest builder. BEL, HAL, PARAS and GABRIEL are also spending on their own factories.

*What could go wrong:*
- The RBI raised interest rates to 5.50%, which slows new Indian projects.
- L&T's pipeline of future bids shrank by a third in one quarter.

*How confident is the engine:* 7.0/10. It is steady: all six companies show it, but profits lag.

*Your next move:* Open L&T's Q2 results presentation (27 Oct 2026) and find the slide with margins by business.

---

**Idea #3: Big Contractors Are Getting Paid Faster**

*What's happening, in plain English:*
Imagine a shop that used to wait six months for customers to pay, and now waits under five. BEL, L&T and Paras all have much more cash coming in.

*Why now specifically:*
- BEL now collects in about 140 days, down from 176.
- Paras went from burning cash to bringing in ₹86 crore.
- L&T's cash tied up in daily operations is at a record low.

*The companies to look at:*
LT, BEL, PARAS. All three turned cash-positive in the same window.

*What could go wrong:*
- Some of this is customer advance payments, which get used up later.
- Gabriel and JNK went the opposite way.

*How confident is the engine:* 5.2/10. This is early: three companies is the bare minimum.

*Your next move:* In the Q3 results (late Jan–Feb), check "cash from operating activities" for all three.

## Jargon Glossary

| Term used | What it actually means |
|---|---|
| Order backlog | Contracts signed but not yet delivered — future sales already locked in |
| Profit per ₹100 of sales (EBITDA margin) | What the company keeps from each ₹100 of sales after running costs, before tax and interest |
| Cash from operating activities | Real cash brought in by the normal business — harder to dress up than profit |
| QRSAM / NGC | A big Army missile contract and a Navy warship-electronics programme — BEL's two expected big orders |
| Related-party deal | A deal with a company the owners or bosses are linked to |
| Postal ballot | A shareholder vote held online or by post instead of at a meeting |
| Deep review (India Stock Picker) | The full check of price, quality and fit before any decision |
| Monitoring | Still a real idea, but weaker — watched more closely, given less weight |
