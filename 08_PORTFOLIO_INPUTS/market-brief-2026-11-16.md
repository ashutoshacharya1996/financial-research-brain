## 3. Take note
- **Interest rates may rise.** With inflation above 6%, some economists now see the repo rate going to 6% by February (Business Standard). Rate hikes usually hurt mid and small caps more than large caps. Your SIPs are built for this.
- **The rally is narrow.** Nifty is up 9.1% in a month, but Midcap is up only 3.7% and Smallcap 0.7%. Mid and small caps are still expensive: PE (price ÷ earnings) is 28.2 for Midcap and 32.6 for Smallcap, against 20.5 for Nifty. Watch for recency bias: a strong month is not a reason to change plans.
- **War chest:** Nifty is 14.8% above your 22,000 trigger. No action.

## 4. Opportunities in play
- **IT stocks.** The sector is 19.5% below its 52-week high and down 12.6% over a year. Its PE of 19.4 is below the Nifty's. FIIs put $1.4 bn into tech in October (CNBC TV18). What would confirm it: IT holding above its 200DMA (200-day average price), now +4.7%, and stable December-quarter earnings.
- **Large caps over small caps, on valuation.** A Nifty PE of 20.5 against 32.6 for Smallcap means any war-chest lump sum would historically have fared better on the large or flexi side at gaps like this. What would confirm it: the gap narrowing without Nifty falling.
