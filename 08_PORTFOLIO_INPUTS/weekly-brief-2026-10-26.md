## Jargon Glossary

| Term used | What it actually means |
|---|---|
| QRSAM | A large air-defence missile system order the government has not yet signed |
| Order book | The total value of contracts a company has won but not yet delivered |
| EBITDA margin | How much profit the company keeps after paying its running costs, before tax |
| Receivable days | How many days customers take to pay their bills |
| YoY | Compared to same period last year |
| Management guidance | What the company's bosses said publicly about what to expect next |
| Related-party deal | A deal between the company and another business linked to its owners |
| NCD | A loan the company raises by selling bonds to investors |
| ECL provisions | Money set aside because some customers may pay late or not at all |
| Keep Tracking | Watch it, but do not do deeper work yet |
