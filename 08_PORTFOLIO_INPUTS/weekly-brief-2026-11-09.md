## What the Engine Found This Week

### Research Queue — Stocks Worth Your Time

These are not buy calls. Think of this as "restaurants worth visiting this month". You still have to taste the food.

**1. Bharat Electronics (BEL)**
*Why it made the list:* Sales for July to September were ₹7,291 crore against ₹5,764 crore a year ago, and profit rose to ₹1,541 crore from ₹1,286 crore. Management still says the huge QRSAM missile contract is "more than 99%" likely.
*What this means:* Imagine a shop with a giant order promised for months that keeps slipping. The shop is still busy, but its pile of future work has shrunk two quarters in a row (₹73,882 crore to ₹68,717 crore).

**2. Paras Defence & Space (PARAS)**
*Why it made the list:* Quarterly sales jumped from ₹103 crore to ₹147 crore, profit rose about 37%, the credit rating was upgraded, and it won a small government research order.
*What this means:* A small company growing fast and borrowing more cheaply. But profit grew a bit slower than sales, and it has signed a plan for a ₹6,200 crore chip-packaging plant with no timeline or funding stated.

**3. Gabriel India (GABRIEL)**
*Why it made the list:* Sales rose about 23%, and it finished buying stakes in two Korean-linked car-electronics businesses.
*What this means:* A shock-absorber maker turning into a driver-assistance supplier. The catch: its own borrowing went from ₹49 crore to about ₹1,122 crore in six months to pay for it.

**4. Larsen & Toubro (LT)**
*Why it made the list:* A record ₹1.48 lakh crore of new orders in the quarter, but it keeps a smaller slice of each rupee as profit (8.7% against 10.0% a year ago).
*What this means:* Lots of work coming in, thinner profit on each job, and part of the profit rise came from a one-time gain on selling a stake.

**5. Hindustan Aeronautics (HAL)**
*Why it made the list:* April to June profit rose about 15%, and it signed an engine deal with a French partner for helicopters.
*What this means:* Steady, but its auditors noted that its board did not meet the required rules on independent directors. We also have no July to September results for HAL yet.

**6. JNK India (JNKINDIA)**
*Why it made the list:* Kept as a warning sign. Growth slowed from 81% to 3%, its order pile shrank, cash fell from ₹32 crore to ₹7 crore, and the big UAE export order from June was cancelled in July.
*What this means:* A big new Nigeria order arrived in November, but it comes from a company linked to its own promoters, so it deserves extra checking.

### The 2 Big Ideas This Week

Only two ideas cleared the bar this time. A third did not.

---

**Idea #1: India's Defence Spending Story Is Still Real, but Slower Than Promised**

*What's happening:*
Think of a restaurant whose kitchen is full of orders but the biggest banquet booking keeps getting pushed. BEL, HAL and PARAS all reported growing sales. But BEL's biggest contract is now several months late, and a submarine deal may slip to next year.

*Why now specifically:*
- BEL says the QRSAM contract could be signed "very soon", and it would lift its pile of future work sharply
- BEL won about ₹5,791 crore of smaller orders between July and November
- PARAS and HAL both reported solid growth in filed results

*The companies to look at:* **BEL**, **HAL**, **PARAS**

*What could go wrong:*
- QRSAM has slipped three times now
- BEL's order pile has shrunk two quarters running
- HAL has a board-rules issue flagged by its auditors

*How confident is the data:* 8.2/10 (down from 9.0 last time). The results are strong, but the delays are a real mark against it.

*Your next move:* Watch BEL's stock-exchange announcements for an order-received filing on QRSAM.

---

**Idea #2: India's Factory-Spending Boom Is Still On, With Thinner Profits**

*What's happening:*
Orders are huge (LT's record) and companies keep announcing new plants, but profit per rupee of work is slipping at several of them.

*Why now specifically:*
- LT's ₹1.48 lakh crore of quarterly orders
- Gabriel's seating joint venture, expected to close by 31 December
- BEL still aims for ₹55,000 crore of new orders this year

*The companies to look at:* **LT**, **BEL**, **GABRIEL**, **PARAS**

*What could go wrong:*
- LT's profit margin is lower than a year ago
- JNK India's cash is nearly gone
- Several of the plans (PARAS's chip plant) have no dates

*How confident is the data:* 7.8/10 (up slightly from 7.5).

*Your next move:* Read LT's Q2 results note on why margins fell.

## Jargon Glossary

| Term used | What it actually means |
|---|---|
| Order book | Contracts won but not yet delivered and paid for — future revenue already locked in |
| QRSAM | A large air-defence missile contract, worth about ₹30,000 crore in total, of which BEL expects about ₹21,000 crore |
| PAT | Profit After Tax — the final profit number |
| EBITDA margin | How much of each rupee of sales is left as profit before interest, tax and depreciation |
| P/E | Share price divided by a year's profit. A higher number means you pay more for each rupee of profit |
| Corroboration | How many separate companies confirm the same pattern |
| Impact note | A gate check that decides whether a stock deserves a deeper valuation review |
