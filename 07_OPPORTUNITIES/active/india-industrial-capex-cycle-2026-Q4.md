## Risks

- **QRSAM remains unsigned past its own July 2026 target:** guided at BEL's June 19 investor meet; no confirmation through September 6 — now the theme's single largest open catalyst risk; per BEL investor meet cadence through 2026-09-06
- **PARAS's ₹6,200cr semiconductor commitment carries undisclosed execution/financing risk:** no timeline, funding source, or customer offtake agreement disclosed for a capex commitment ~4x annual revenue; per Press release — PARAS — 2026-07-22
- **Order-to-revenue conversion lag persists as a general risk:** BEL, HAL, and LT collectively hold multi-year order books; strong inflows are visible but revenue conversion is multi-year; near-term earnings may disappoint even with thesis intact
- **LT's order surge has not yet been confirmed by a margin recovery:** the Q4 FY26 margin compression (10.4% EBITDA, PAT -3% YoY) that previously made LT this theme's counter-case has not been re-tested with a new results print in this window — order momentum is confirmed, profit conversion is not yet
- **JNKINDIA and LT's Middle East execution is improving but still early-innings:** JNKINDIA's Iraq branch has zero delivery track record yet (newly approved); LT's two new Middle East Ultra-Mega orders are wins, not yet delivered projects
- **Interest rate sensitivity:** India industrial capex cycle is partly rate-sensitive; RBI increases in H2 FY27 could defer discretionary industrial projects
