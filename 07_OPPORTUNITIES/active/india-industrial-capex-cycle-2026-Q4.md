## Risks

- **Order-to-revenue conversion lag, now visible:** LT book +27% vs revenue +7%; BEL large platform orders convert "after two years".
- **LT margin and cash flow:** EBITDA 9.0%, negative group operating cash flow, receivable-delay provisions; Q1 PAT growth leaned on other income and divestment.
- **Middle East concentration:** LT International is 56% of inflow / 52% of book while the same region is the cited execution drag; JNKINDIA's headline UAE order was cancelled for licensor approval.
- **Governance and guidance integrity:** five of six names flagged by the overlay; JNKINDIA and BEL guidance cannot be used for estimates until clarified.
- **Acquisition-led rather than capex-led spending at GABRIEL; unfunded mega-MoU at PARAS.**
- **Rate sensitivity (unchanged):** a rate rise could defer discretionary projects.

## Thesis Breakers

- LT Q2 revenue growth stays below ~10% with margin below 9% and inflow growth slows.
- LT order book stops growing, or Middle East inflow reverses.
- JNKINDIA order book falls again in Q2 without a major conversion.
- Theme score falls below 5.0 or is marked Declining at the next run (then close this record).
