## Why Now

- **Three tier-1 operating-cash-flow inflections in the same window** (BEL, LT, PARAS). In the 2026-06-22 deltas, "working capital improvement" was absent at all three.
- **BEL NGC (~₹20,000cr) and QRSAM booking by 31-Mar-2027.** New defence contracts typically carry customer advances. This tests whether BEL's cash profile holds once the advances behind the H1 swing (other current liabilities −₹1,136cr) are used up. Per BEL Q2 call 2026-11-03.
- **LT HMRL divestment closing by 2026-12-31** (extended twice), then **Q3 FY27 results (late Jan – Feb 2027)** for LT, BEL and PARAS. These are the duration test: does OCF stay positive as advances unwind? Per LT concession-divestments filing 2026-09-30.

## Risks

- **Timing, not structure.** BEL's swing coincides with advances being consumed, and LT's NWC benefits from advances on new ultra-mega orders. Both can reverse.
- **Receivable stress at LT.** H1 bad debts plus ECL were ₹2,353cr (+26%), and I&U cites "expected delays in collection of receivables".
- **Split universe.** GABRIEL (standalone H1 OCF −42.7%; receivables +25.8%) and JNKINDIA (H1 OCF ₹2.8cr; cash ₹6.9cr) deteriorated. HAL's inventory/WIP is still building.
- **PARAS inventory build** (+21% in six months) partly offsets its OCF gain.

## Thesis Breakers

- Q3 FY27 OCF turns negative again at two or more of BEL, LT and PARAS.
- BEL receivable days move back above 160, or LT's NWC/revenue moves back above 5%.
- LT ECL keeps rising more than 25% YoY in Q3 FY27, which would mean collections, not advances, are the problem.
