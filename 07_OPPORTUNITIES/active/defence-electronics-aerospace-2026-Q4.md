## Why Now

- **NGC cleared and signed; ~₹20,000cr of BEL orders expected "99%" in FY27.** BEL Q2 call: "at least NGC is cleared, and it was signed also by the parent PSUs, GSL and GRSE"; "tentatively … around INR20,000 crores … all of them, 99%, will come in this financial year"; "some order may slip to April also". Per Earnings Call — BEL — 2026-11-03 (03_EXTRACTED_DATA/BEL/2026/Q2FY27/earnings-call.md).
- **The FY27 inflow test lands Dec 2026 – Mar 2027.** BEL is "sticking to" ₹55,000cr including QRSAM (₹25,000cr without it), against H1 inflow of ₹7,334cr. About ₹10,000cr had been booked FY27-to-date including October–November releases, so ~₹45,000cr is needed in Dec–Mar (derived, BEL delta). This is a dated, binary validation point.
- **AMCA (L&T–BEL 50:50) price-bid outcome "Hopefully, before March"**, per the BEL Q2 call. This is a second dated catalyst touching BEL and LT.
- **Execution confirmed in tier-1 filings at all three pure-plays.** H1 FY27 revenue: HAL +14.7%, BEL +25.97%, PARAS +40.3%. All are running at or above their own guidance. Per Q2 FY27 results: HAL 2026-11-12; BEL 2026-10-30; PARAS 2026-10-29.
- **HAL pipeline step: DAC AoN for ALH (2026-09-07)**, within ~₹1.10 lakh cr of total AoNs. This is the step before a contract; ALH quantity and value were not stated.

*Superseded June catalysts (kept for audit):* "QRSAM signing July 2026" (missed; now "next CCS"); "LCA Mk1A delivery Aug/Sep 2026" (window passed, unconfirmed); "MALE UAV evaluation" (no outcome in five months); "BEL pipeline of 4 named programmes" (P-75I now likely FY28; data-centre stream dropped from the calls).

## Risks

- **Order-timing risk has materialised.** QRSAM went from "July 2026" to "maximum by September" to "Hopefully, in next CCS". P-75I "may slip to next year". BEL's order book fell two quarters running (₹73,882 → ₹72,258 → ₹68,717cr).
- **Margins below guidance.** HAL Q1 FY27 was 27.8% (ICRA) vs "around 30%, 31%". BEL H1 was 27.43% vs ">28%" (now "around 28%"), with material cost ≈54% of revenue vs 47–49% (derived). A BEL wage revision is due from January 2027.
- **Governance.** HAL's Audit Committee/NRC is not validly constituted (EoM in Q4 FY26 and Q1 FY27; AGM 2026-08-28). HAL acquired the HATSOFF stake at nil consideration, "not at Arm's length". HAL's ICRA rating was withdrawn at the company's request. PARAS's ₹150cr related-party transaction was defeated, then re-filed with a corrigendum.
- **Private SI competition.** Adani won the Netra 2 SI role ahead of BEL. Bargaining power is moving toward private integrators.
- **Valuation (Kite LTP 2026-11-23, derived trailing P/E):** BEL ~45.8x, HAL ~33.2x, PARAS ~96.4x. The market de-rated BEL (P/E 52.1 → 45.9) and PARAS (125.5 → 97.1) between June and November despite earnings growth.
- **Budget dependency.** The FY28 Union Budget (February 2027) sets the next allocation.

## Thesis Breakers

- QRSAM and the bulk of NGC (~₹20,000cr) are **not booked by 31-Mar-2027**, so BEL's FY27 inflow lands below the ₹25,000cr ex-QRSAM guide. The theme agent will then re-test the theme for Declining.
- HAL's Q3 FY27 disclosure still shows **no LCA Mk1A delivery** and a margin below 28%.
- PARAS's postal-ballot re-vote on the Paras Anti-Drone related-party transaction fails again, or PARAS discloses an order book below ~₹700cr (the level needed to support the FY28 guide).
- FY28 defence capital outlay is cut in nominal terms in the February 2027 Union Budget.
