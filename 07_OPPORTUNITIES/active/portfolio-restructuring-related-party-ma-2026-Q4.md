## Risks

- **Minority-shareholder dilution and pricing:** the largest GABRIEL deal has a promoter-group counterparty; independent valuation (KPMG/BDO) figures are not in evidence
- **Leverage:** GABRIEL moves from net cash to Rs 1,000-1,200 cr expected debt; Crisil downgrade trigger at debt/EBITDA above 2.5x; gearing guided "below 0.30 time"
- **Data conflicts left open:** HL Mando consideration (Rs 2,231 cr vs Rs 1,881 cr), HL Klemove adjusted EBITDA (printed Rs 1293 cr vs Crisil ~Rs 129 cr), FY26 EBITDA-margin bases (9.7% / 9.8% / 9.4%), FORVIA counterparty naming, LT Realty scale (0.59% vs ~1.5% of revenue)
- **Not an operating signal:** a corporate-action cluster may not map to earnings upside; GABRIEL margins fell in the same quarter
- **Governance housekeeping at GABRIEL:** Company Secretary resignation, NSE XBRL/PDF cash-flow deficiency letter

## Thesis Breakers
