## Signal Notes

Five material changes vs the 2026-06-22 baseline report:

1. **LT's "H1 soft, H2 recovery" thesis from the last report is now confirmed correct.** The prior report flagged LT's Middle East execution headwind as a live earnings-miss risk. This run shows LT winning two Ultra-Mega Middle East contracts (ADNOC, Strategic Offshore) as part of a seven-order, ₹50,000+cr surge — the single largest positive change in this run, and it resolves what was previously the capex-cycle theme's biggest counter-argument.

2. **QRSAM has gone from "imminent" to "overdue."** The prior report treated BEL's QRSAM signing as expected within days (July 2026). It is now unconfirmed through September 6 — a full month past management's own guided date, with the P-75I/AMCA pipeline names also going quiet. This is the single most important negative change this run and downgrades the defence theme from 9.2 to 8.8.

3. **A new company-specific risk surfaced: HAL governance non-compliance.** SEBI Reg 17(1) board-composition violation and an unconstituted audit committee since April 5, 2026 — not present in any prior report, and material enough to route to Investment Impact alongside HAL's genuinely positive operational news (Aravalli JV, revenue acceleration).

4. **Margin Expansion / Operating Leverage crosses the Opportunity Record threshold for the first time** (2 → 3 companies) on the strength of JNKINDIA's Q1FY27 EBITDA +3.1x YoY — the most dramatic single data point in this theme's history.

5. **Two single-company signals are being watched but do not yet qualify as themes:** PARAS's ₹6,200cr semiconductor OSAT pivot and LT's entry into hyperscale AI-infrastructure EPC (Vyoma.AI/NVIDIA B300). Both are folded into the capex-cycle theme as evidence; either would trigger a new standalone theme if a second company corroborates it next run.
