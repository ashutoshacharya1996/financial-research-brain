## Guidance
Arvind Kamath: "Accordingly, we continue to maintain our FY27 guidance of 20%-25% revenue growth and in EBITDA margin of 12%-14% on the operational revenue."
H2 weighting: "Historically, H2 contributes approximately 60%-70% of our annual revenue" (Dipak Bharuka). "balance 60%-65% revenue is always carved in the second half of the year." (Arvind Kamath)
Near-term: "October, generally October, November, we don't improve so much." / "But the last quarter, we expect the maximum recovery." (Dipak Bharuka)

## Management Commentary — Demand
"Internationally, Africa continues to be an important focus area, particularly countries such as Nigeria, Kenya, Ethiopia, where investment in refining, petrochemicals, and fertilizers are creating demand for specialized engineering and process equipment."
"Large and technically complex projects can have longer decision-making cycles."
Largest export: "we also received the largest export order on us till date." / "this is a repeat order which we had successfully executed 10 years back, and they have placed the repeat order on JNK Global" (Arvind Kamath)
Dangote balance: "Right now, the order comprises of something what is agreed, and balance of the scope is under the discussion. [...] we are not confirming definitely it will come or not." / "We can expect in another two to three months, this can get materialized."
Kenya refinery: "The inquiries for Kenya would be expected in some time." / "Inquiries will come in two to three months, and the order finalization expected in four to five months."
BPCL Vizag: "the tenders are likely to come in next six to eight months' time, and ordering we are expecting to materialize in next 12 months' time." "Total opportunity size, you can say, will be around INR5,000 crores for people like us."
Nigeria additional: "In the bid pipeline which is there is almost something like INR700 crores-INR800 crores for the existing Nigeria and the fertilizer opportunity."
Non-heater: "about INR2,500 crores is the non-heater opportunities which we have, out of which around INR1,000 crores is where the bid is, bid pipeline, we already bid [...] And INR1,500 crores is something which we would bid soon". NTPC: "Bid due date is 20th November as of now."
Win rates: "in the heater segment, the bid-to-win ratio has been about 30%. The non-heater segment [...] at least it would be about anywhere between 10% to 15% or so."

## Management Commentary — Execution
"part of it is related with the logistical challenges, particularly due to the geopolitical situation, and part is also due to the severe monsoon we had faced in some part of India, which has impacted our fabrication and site erection progress." (Dipak Bharuka)
Analyst challenge: "given the weakness in H1 and especially in Q2, and we are still maintaining the guidance of 20%-25% growth, what are the levers" — Reply: "the growth on revenue is 30% already."

## Management Commentary — Margins
"As already, the EBITDA also we have achieved in excess of 11%, and the EBITDA margin or EBITDA values are about 50% higher than the last half, last year for the H1." (Arvind Kamath)
Commodity: "if you talk about copper, nickel, or any other aluminum fluctuating, that's a business risk." / "At least ongoing project, we do not see that having any significant impact on our numbers. But yes, going forward, as a part of project business, that remains one of the major risk."

## Management Commentary — Capacity / Diversification / Chemdist
"But our main focus is on the R&D and on the renewable fuel, green hydrogen side, which will take some time, maybe about two years or so, to actually culminate into a larger amount of revenues or the larger project business opportunities." (Arvind Kamath on Chemdist)
"Around 50% of it is other than the, basically the core heater opportunities."

## Working Capital / Funding (verbatim)
"As of now, there is no such plan, Ram. We are quite confident that this year and next year, with whatever the customer payment terms what we have, and reserves and the bank limits what we have, we should be able to fund the working capital."
Related-party / disclosure: Dipak Bharuka: "there was some error in the disclosure, error to the extent we should not have given 300 to 600. We should have given 500 to something" / "Going forward, we will be narrowing that range".
Payment trigger: "their contractual time is not started because there are conditions in the contract that your due date starts only from the receipt of the advance. We are ready to receive the advance, and that is the reason we are not hurrying up to get the order from JNK Global."
