## Guidance

"Accordingly, we continue to maintain our FY27 guidance of 20%-25% revenue growth and in EBITDA margin of 12%-14% on the operational revenue."

"we are confident of achieving the 20%-25% guidance which we have given for the year. Similarly, the EBITDA guidance also we are confident of achieving."

H2 weighting: "Historically, H2 contributes approximately 60%-70% of our annual revenue"

Order inflow: "in our project business, it becomes a bit difficult to exactly mention the order book target per se figures." ... "So what is important is the very healthy bid pipeline, which is about INR5,000 crores, which is a firm bid pipeline, which could get finalized by Q4, or it could some get spilled over to Q1 of next year as well."

Win rates: "in the heater segment, the bid-to-win ratio has been about 30%. The non-heater segment ... we are confident that at least it would be about anywhere between 10% to 15% or so."

Funding: "We are quite confident that this year and next year, with whatever the customer payment terms what we have, and reserves and the bank limits what we have, we should be able to fund the working capital."

## Management Commentary — Demand

"The performance reflects strong underlying business momentum despite temporary execution challenges during Q2 arising from shipment delays and monsoon-related disruptions."

"part of it is related with the logistical challenges, particularly due to the geopolitical situation, and part is also due to the severe monsoon we had faced in some part of India, which has impacted our fabrication and site erection progress."

"October is okay, just closing, and November is due to the festival reason. Generally, we pick up in December and January."

"Internationally, Africa continues to be an important focus area, particularly countries such as Nigeria, Kenya, Ethiopia, where investment in refining, petrochemicals, and fertilizers are creating demand for specialized engineering and process equipment."

Nigeria order: "we have already received the largest export order on JNK India till date." ... "this is a repeat order which we had successfully executed 10 years back"

Balance Nigeria scope: "Right now, the order comprises of something what is agreed, and balance of the scope is under the discussion." ... "We can expect in another two to three months, this can get materialized."

Kenya: "Inquiries will come in two to three months, and the order finalization expected in four to five months."

BPCL Andhra/Vizag (not in pipeline): "the tenders are likely to come in next six to eight months’ time, and ordering we are expecting to materialize in next 12 months’ time." ... "Total opportunity size, you can say, will be around INR5,000 crores for people like us."

NTPC: "Bid due date is 20th November as of now."

## Management Commentary — Margins

"As already, the EBITDA also we have achieved in excess of 11%, and the EBITDA margin or EBITDA values are about 50% higher than the last half, last year for the H1."

Commodities: "there are some contracts like a PSU contract for BPCL and all that, where we have a clause of steel price variation, where client compensates. But if you talk about copper, nickel, or any other aluminum fluctuating, that's a business risk."

## Management Commentary — Capacity / Expansion

"Heating equipment will continue to remain our core strength. However, we believe our engineering, fabrication, and project execution capabilities provide us with more opportunities to participate in a wider range of businesses."

JNK Chemdist: "our main focus is on the R&D and on the renewable fuel, green hydrogen side, which will take some time, maybe about two years or so, to actually culminate into a larger amount of revenues"

Non-heater pipeline: "about INR2,500 crores is the non-heater opportunities which we have, out of which around INR1,000 crores is where the bid is ... And INR1,500 crores is something which we would bid soon"

## Named Catalysts

- Balance scope of Nigeria (Dangote) project from JNK Global — "two to three months"
- Kenya refinery (Dangote) — order finalization "four to five months"
- BPCL Andhra refinery + petchem — ordering ~12 months; ~₹5,000 Cr opportunity for "people like us"
- NTPC bid due Nov 20, 2026
- Additional Nigeria/fertilizer offers ~₹700–800 Cr in pipeline
- H2 FY27 revenue recovery Dec–Mar

## Named Risks

- Q2 revenue deferral (~₹30–40 Cr) from shipment/logistics (geopolitical) and monsoon
- Uncertainty over size of JNK Global/Dangote award: "Unless we have the order in hand, I cannot say we have the order, neither I can say it is certain."
- Order disclosure range error: "there was some error in the disclosure, error to the extent we should not have given 300 to 600. We should have given 500 to something"
- Commodity (copper, nickel, aluminium) price risk
- JNK Chemdist larger revenue "maybe about two years or so" away
- Long decision cycles: "Large and technically complex projects can have longer decision-making cycles."
