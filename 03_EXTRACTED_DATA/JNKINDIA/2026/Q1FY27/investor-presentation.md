## Guidance
Not stated on the Management Commentary slide. (A separate corporate presentation of 2026-08-14 states 14-15% / 25-30%; see events/corporate-presentation-emkay-2026-08-14.md.)

## Management Commentary — Demand
Arvind Kamath: "Our order book on June 30, 2026 is Rs 1,801 cr, and our growth visibility remains strong, with a bidding pipeline of Rs. ~6,000 cr across domestic and international markets."

Order Cancellation Update (slide 5): "The large export order received on June 8, 2026, was cancelled solely due to the inability to secure the requisite technical approval from the licensor, highlighting the high entry barriers in the industry related to technical approvals and a proven track record. Importantly, this was an isolated incident, with no material costs incurred and no execution having commenced."

## Management Commentary — Margins
"Importantly, we have maintained an EBITDA margin of 11.8% in Q1FY27, representing our ability to deliver strong growth while maintaining a stable margin profile."

## Management Commentary — Capacity / Expansion
"Continued focus on diversifying the order pipeline across geographies and sectors (new sectors metals and minerals)". Mundra: "5,000 MT installed capacity, with 50 kWp solar power" (20,243 sq. m.; "Dedicated for Exports" per slide 3 of corporate deck).
