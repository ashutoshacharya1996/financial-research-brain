## Named Catalysts

- **MOA amendment (subject to shareholder approval)**: New main-object sub-clauses inserted for "design, engineering, supply, fabrication, transportation, logistics, heavy-lifting, marine and offshore operations" and for "engineering, procurement, construction, turnkey contracting... across the mining industry, steel industry, cement industry, metallurgical industry, mineral processing, and other similar or allied heavy industries in India and abroad." New ancillary clauses permit owning/chartering marine vessels, barges, tugs, offshore support vessels, floating cranes, and executing BOO/BOT/BOOT/EPC/PPP concession-model projects via SPVs. Expected benefit per filing: "Expands the Company's value chain... Opens new revenue streams by enabling the Company to bid for and execute large-scale, turnkey industrial projects in the heavy industries sector (steel, mining, cement etc.)... Empowers the Company to undertake complex, long-term infrastructure projects." Capex funding: "a mix of internal accruals and/or borrowings as deemed appropriate."
- **Iraq branch office**: Board approved setting up an overseas Branch Office in the Republic of Iraq (subject to necessary approvals)
- **Senior Management change**: Mr. Pravin Pulujkar (22+ years procurement experience) and Mr. Prasad Phatak (30+ years EPC project management) designated as Senior Management Personnel effective August 11, 2026; Mr. Ravikumar Mudali Vallathur ceased to be designated SMP effective same date (continues in employment, role change only — not a departure)
