## Guidance

- "Our revenue growth guidance of around 20% to 25% remains intact. We also maintain our full year EBITDA margin guidance of about 12% to 14%." (Chairperson, Arvind Kamath)
- Discrepancy on record: analyst Kamlesh Bagmar: "we were guiding roughly around 14% to 15% margin. So now we are saying 12% to 14%." Management (Dipak Bharuka): "what we have announced was 12% to 14%. But for some reason, we are missing something, these numbers, we'll recheck them. But there is no change from like what -- from the last 3 months or 6 months, nothing has changed on -- as far as our margin projection goes. It remains the same."
- Q1 FY27 investor presentation (Aug 14 deck) states "EBITDA margin guidance of 14 -15% and revenue growth of 25-30%". The call transcript states 12% to 14% EBITDA margin and 20% to 25% revenue growth.
- Medium term (Dipak Bharuka): "coming to around 3 to 5 years, we want to move this non-heating segment to around 40% of our revenue"
- Chemdist revenue (Arvind Kamath): "we are expecting the revenue anywhere about 10% to 15% of JNK India revenue in this financial year or next couple of years" and "gross margins ... around, say, 20% or so."
- Chemdist breakeven: "we should be able to get it into the green" by year-end (Dipak Bharuka)
- Order inflow hit rate: "traditionally, our hit rate has been 20% to 25%" (Arvind Kamath); new segments: "we are expecting our hit ratio should be anything around 10% to 12%, not more than that." (Dipak Bharuka)

## Management Commentary — Demand

- "Our order book as on 30th of June 2026 stood at INR 1,801 crores, providing us with a very healthy base of executable projects for the year."
- "Our current overall opportunity pipeline is more than INR 6,000 crores with a broadly balanced 50:50% mix between international and domestic opportunities."
- "about 60% of the current opportunity pipeline is related to the heating equipment, while the remaining 40% comprises process plants and other special fabricated equipment and adjacent technology-led EPC opportunities."
- "Africa remains an important market for us, particularly countries such as Nigeria and Ethiopia, where significant investments are being planned across refining, petrochemicals and fertilizers."
- Pipeline timing (Arvind Kamath): "these could get finalized in anywhere about, say, 3 to 6 months or 6 to 8 months' time. So overall, we expect that all this pipeline should get finalized in this financial year."
- Dangote (Dipak Bharuka): "Phase 2 is still under the discussion, and we stand a good chance of getting it."
- Refinery and fertilizer orders (Arvind Kamath): "the order finalization should happen in Q2, Q3."
- JNK Global dependence: "There are 4 projects in our order book from JNK Global ... BPCL Bina project ... one USA project ... Petronas Phoenix Project."

## Management Commentary — Margins

- "Our objective is not simply to grow the order book, but to build a sustainable order pipeline with healthy margins, manageable execution requirements and attractive long-term opportunities."
- "as the business scales up through the upcoming quarters, we expect the operating leverage to improve meaningfully."
- Pricing / commodity (Dipak Bharuka): "as far as ongoing projects are concerned, these are already factored in our costing. And most of the orders are already placed, and we placed the order on our sub vendor on a fixed price basis."
- New business margins (Arvind Kamath): "we are always looking at in the similar margin range of whatever our guided EBITDA margins are there, anywhere from 12% to 14% in that range."
- BPCL Bina: "overall, EBITDA would be in line with whatever we have guided for this year."
- Seasonality (Arvind Kamath): "Historically, Q1 contributes about 10% to 15% of our full year revenue, while H1 contributes 30% to 35%. H2 accounts for the remaining 60% to 70% of our annual revenue."

## Management Commentary — Capacity / Expansion

- "we are taking further steps to diversify our business into new and adjacent opportunities that can become important growth avenues for JNK India over the medium term." Segments: "Our upcoming avenue are the offshore and metals and minerals industries with a focus also on renewable energy."
- Offshore / metals projects (Dipak Bharuka): "we are first -- we are bidding for a couple of projects on the material handling side"; "Nano Urea plant, which is being proposed by some of the PSUs in India."
- Technology partners (Dipak Bharuka): "when it comes to the technology, we need a technology partner, and we are already tied up with 3, 4 such technology partners with whom we are currently bidding for the ongoing opportunities."
- Iraq (Arvind Kamath): "that's why we have just opened -- or we have kind of taken a Board approval yesterday to register a branch office."
- Iraq (Dipak Bharuka): "this will be more of a registered paper office."
- TAM (Arvind Kamath, in answer on offshore): "the TAM is somewhere around $300 million to $500 million. And in metals and minerals, it is about $500 million to $1 billion." (offshore figure is the first clause; "currently, only in India")
- Chemdist green hydrogen (Arvind Kamath): "for the green hydrogen current order execution, what they are doing is about INR 50 crores. And that should get completed almost say, in this year and may get spilled over some to Q1 of next year."
- Chemdist technology licensing (Dipak Bharuka): "Yes. I mean that is the ultimate aim."

## Named Risks

- Cancelled export order: "The large export order received on June 8, 2026, was subsequently canceled solely due to the technical approval requirements." Management: "this cancellation does not result in any material cash loss for the company."
- Licensor approval (Dipak Bharuka): "they said that cannot be applied to something which is ongoing. So, for future projects, we have received the qualification from them."
- JNK Global governance dispute (analyst question): "though it is -- the matter is sub judice, we would not like to comment on that as of now."
- Chemdist losses: "the lower revenue typically seen in the Q1 results in operating losses."
- New-segment hit rate: "we are expecting our hit ratio should be anything around 10% to 12%."
- JNK Global agreement: "okay, it is not yet renewed, but there is -- I think it has the expiry of 3 years or until it gets revised, if I'm not wrong." (analyst cited DRHP 3-year cooperation agreement)
- Working capital / bank guarantees: "we might need a bank guarantee limit enhancement basically. To that extent, we might have to approach the banks for the new contract, in terms of the bank guarantee limits, non-fund-based limits"
- Financial statement discrepancies: management acknowledged "there was some kind of error in the standalone numbers" (Arvind Kamath) and that a revised filing was being made.
- Accounting change: "we changed accounting from output method" (analyst question); management: "whatever now we are going on the input method."
