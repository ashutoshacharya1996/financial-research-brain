## Guidance
Arvind Kamath: "Our revenue growth guidance of around 20% to 25% remains intact. We also maintain our full year EBITDA margin guidance of about 12% to 14%."
Seasonality: "Historically, Q1 contributes about 10% to 15% of our full year revenue, while H1 contributes 30% to 35%. H2 accounts for the remaining 60% to 70% of our annual revenue."
Analyst (Kamlesh Bagmar) on margin: "in the last con call, if I am correct, we were guiding roughly around 14% to 15% margin. So now we are saying 12% to 14%. Can you clarify on that?" Dipak Bharuka: "For our understanding, what we have announced was 12% to 14%. But for some reason, we are missing something, these numbers, we'll recheck them. But there is no change from like what -- from the last 3 months or 6 months, nothing has changed on -- as far as our margin projection goes. It remains the same."
Medium term: "around 3 to 5 years, we want to move this non-heating segment to around 40% of our revenue" (Dipak Bharuka).

## Management Commentary — Demand
"We continue to see encouraging traction across both domestic and international markets."
"On the export side, Africa remains an important market for us, particularly countries such as Nigeria and Ethiopia, where significant investments are being planned across refining, petrochemicals and fertilizers. We also continue to see opportunities across the Middle East and other international markets."
On Dangote: "Phase 2 is still under the discussion, and we stand a good chance of getting it." / "we do not have anything which we can disclose publicly until we get some commitment from -- or some official commitment from the client."
Orders timing (Arvind Kamath, in reply to Rupesh Tatiya): "both refinery and fertilizer, the order finalization should happen in Q2, Q3."
Pipeline conversion: "overall we expect that all this pipeline should get finalized in this financial year."

## Management Commentary — Order cancellation (UAE / TA'ZIZ)
"The large export order received on June 8, 2026, was subsequently canceled solely due to the technical approval requirements. The international EPC contractor who had awarded the contract to JNK India was confident of securing the required technical approval from the licensor. However, the approval did not materialize in time."
"the cancellation happened at a very early stage, and we had not incurred costs against this order. Therefore, this cancellation does not result in any material cash loss for the company."
"this is very rarest of rare case rather in last 15 years, this is the first time we have gone through something like this." (Dipak Bharuka)
"for future projects, we have received the qualification from them. [...] they said that cannot be applied to something which is ongoing."

## Management Commentary — Margins
"if you break it down, for JNK India alone, we have achieved an EBITDA of 14%, which is as per the guideline" (Arvind Kamath)
Commodities: "most of the orders are already placed, and we placed the order on our sub vendor on a fixed price basis. So that whatever the fluctuation is happening, it is not having any major impact on our margins for the ongoing projects."
Chemdist: "we expect that they would also be in the similar line as around, say, 20% or so." (gross margin); BPCL Bina: "overall, EBITDA would be in line with whatever we have guided for this year."
New segments: "we are always looking at in the similar margin range of whatever our guided EBITDA margins are there, anywhere from 12% to 14% in that range."
Analyst noted employee cost increase compressing margin: "our gross margin has actually improved, but EBITDA margin has come down. And one of the reasons which I can see is increase in the employee cost." Reply: "Yes, that's correct, Deepak."

## Management Commentary — Capacity / Expansion / Diversification
"Our upcoming avenue are the offshore and metals and minerals industries with a focus also on renewable energy."
TAM: "offshore, currently, only in India, the TAM is somewhere around $300 million to $500 million. And in metals and minerals, it is about $500 million to $1 billion."
Hit ratio in new segments: "we are expecting around 10% as our hit ratio, at least to start within the next couple of years."
Iraq: "we have taken the approval to register the office there, and we will not have any setup as of now." ("registered paper office")
Chemdist: "for the green hydrogen current order execution, what they are doing is about INR 50 crores. And that should get completed almost say, in this year and may get spilled over some to Q1 of next year." / "by year-end, we should be able to get it into the green." / JNK Chemdist revenue "anywhere about 10% to 15% of JNK India revenue in this financial year or next couple of years."
JNK Global: "we are doing BPCL Bina with JNK Global" ; "there are 4 projects in our order book from JNK Global, which are like BPCL Bina, project is there, then one USA project is there and Petronas Phoenix Project." ; "All export projects, we will be doing together. I mean, JNK Global will be getting the orders and they will be subcontracting to us. It is a back-to-back basis."
Cooperation agreement: "it is not yet renewed, but there is -- I think it has the expiry of 3 years or until it gets revised, if I'm not wrong."
