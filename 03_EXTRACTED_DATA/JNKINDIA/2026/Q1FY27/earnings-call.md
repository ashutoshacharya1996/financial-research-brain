## Guidance

"Our revenue growth guidance of around 20% to 25% remains intact. We also maintain our full year EBITDA margin guidance of about 12% to 14%."

On order inflow: "we have a pipeline of about INR 6,000 crores, Kamlesh. You must have seen that. And traditionally, our hit rate has been 20% to 25%. So I think we're kind of expecting the similar hit rate this year as well."

Medium-term revenue growth: "we have given a revenue guidance for this year in terms of growth of about 20% to 25%. So I think that's the kind of a growth we do hope to achieve on a medium-term year-on-year."

Diversification target: "coming to around 3 to 5 years, we want to move this non-heating segment to around 40% of our revenue"

JNK Chemdist: "we are expecting the revenue anywhere about 10% to 15% of JNK India revenue in this financial year or next couple of years."

Chemdist break-even: "if you see overall year-end figures at the end of this year, we will be in the green."

Order timing: "See, both refinery and fertilizer, the order finalization should happen in Q2, Q3."

Funding: analyst asked "no significant debt raising, no significant fundraising, at least, let's say, next 4 to 6 quarters. That's a fair conclusion?" — Management: "Yes, debt raising, I mean, yes, absolutely. I think that's a fair conclusion. But from the contract receipt point of view, we might need a bank guarantee limit enhancement basically."

On margin-guidance discrepancy (analyst: "in the last con call ... we were guiding roughly around 14% to 15% margin. So now we are saying 12% to 14%"): "For our understanding, what we have announced was 12% to 14%. But for some reason, we are missing something, these numbers, we'll recheck them. But there is no change from like what -- from the last 3 months or 6 months, nothing has changed on -- as far as our margin projection goes. It remains the same."

## Management Commentary — Demand

"Historically, Q1 contributes about 10% to 15% of our full year revenue, while H1 contributes 30% to 35%. H2 accounts for the remaining 60% to 70% of our annual revenue."

"We are progressing on projects such as BPCL Bina project, where execution is underway and a significant portion of the project revenue is expected to be recognized during FY27 and also on FY28."

"While heating continues to remain a core strength for us, about 60% of the current opportunity pipeline is related to the heating equipment, while the remaining 40% comprises process plants and other special fabricated equipment and adjacent technology-led EPC opportunities."

"On the export side, Africa remains an important market for us, particularly countries such as Nigeria and Ethiopia, where significant investments are being planned across refining, petrochemicals and fertilizers. We also continue to see opportunities across the Middle East and other international markets."

On Dangote: "Now Phase 2 is still under the discussion, and we stand a good chance of getting it."

On export bidding structure: "All export projects, we will be doing together. I mean, JNK Global will be getting the orders and they will be subcontracting to us. It is a back-to-back basis. As far as the fired heater, reformer and cracking furnace are concerned."

On UAE cancellation: "The large export order received on June 8, 2026, was subsequently canceled solely due to the technical approval requirements." ... "Importantly, the cancellation happened at a very early stage, and we had not incurred costs against this order. Therefore, this cancellation does not result in any material cash loss for the company."

## Management Commentary — Margins

"for JNK India alone, we have achieved an EBITDA of 14%, which is as per the guideline"

"The business has a relatively high fixed cost base at this stage, and therefore, the lower revenue typically seen in the Q1 results in operating losses." (JNK Chemdist)

"Yes, the commodities prices are fluctuating. But as far as ongoing projects are concerned, these are already factored in our costing. And most of the orders are already placed, and we placed the order on our sub vendor on a fixed price basis."

New businesses: "in terms of the margins, we are always looking at in the similar margin range of whatever our guided EBITDA margins are there, anywhere from 12% to 14% in that range."

## Management Commentary — Capacity / Expansion

"Our upcoming avenue are the offshore and metals and minerals industries with a focus also on renewable energy."

TAM: "in, say, offshore, currently, only in India, the TAM is somewhere around $300 million to $500 million. And in metals and minerals, it is about $500 million to $1 billion."

Iraq: "we have kind of taken a Board approval yesterday to register a branch office."

Hit rate in new segments: "we are expecting our hit ratio should be anything around 10% to 12%, not more than that."

JNK Chemdist green hydrogen: "In JNK Chemdist, for the green hydrogen current order execution, what they are doing is about INR 50 crores. And that should get completed almost say, in this year and may get spilled over some to Q1 of next year."
