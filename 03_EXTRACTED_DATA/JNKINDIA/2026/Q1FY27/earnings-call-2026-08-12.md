## Guidance

"Our revenue growth guidance of around 20% to 25% remains intact. We also maintain our full year EBITDA margin guidance of about 12% to 14%." — Mr. Arvind Kamath (p.2)

Clarification on margin guidance (analyst noted last call guided ~14-15%): "For our understanding, what we have announced was 12% to 14%. But for some reason, we are missing something, these numbers, we'll recheck them. But there is no change from like what -- from the last 3 months or 6 months, nothing has changed on -- as far as our margin projection goes. It remains the same." — Mr. Dipak Bharuka (p.16)

Order inflow: "we have a pipeline of about INR 6,000 crores ... traditionally, our hit rate has been 20% to 25%. So I think we're kind of expecting the similar hit rate this year as well." (p.5)

Medium term growth: "I think we have given a revenue guidance for this year in terms of growth of about 20% to 25%. So I think that's the kind of a growth we do hope to achieve on a medium-term year-on-year." (p.5)

Debt/funding: "debt raising, I mean, yes, absolutely. I think that's a fair conclusion. But from the contract receipt point of view, we might need a bank guarantee limit enhancement basically." (p.17)

Chemdist: "our expectation is that by year-end, we should be able to get it into the green." (p.12); "we are expecting the revenue anywhere about 10% to 15% of JNK India revenue in this financial year or next couple of years ... gross margins ... around, say, 20% or so." (p.15)

## Management Commentary — Demand

"We continue to see encouraging traction across both domestic and international markets." (p.3)

"On the export side, Africa remains an important market for us, particularly countries such as Nigeria and Ethiopia, where significant investments are being planned across refining, petrochemicals and fertilizers. We also continue to see opportunities across the Middle East and other international markets." (p.3)

"both refinery and fertilizer, the order finalization should happen in Q2, Q3. Yes, that's correct." (p.14)

"the export opportunity is about 50%. That is around INR 3,000 crores. So that is mainly on the heating equipment." "the other opportunities are domestic, which is INR 3,000-plus crores. They are mainly more of non-heating equipment kind of a business." "we expect that all this pipeline should get finalized in this financial year." (p.6)

Dangote: "we were the supplier for the Phase 1. Now Phase 2 is still under the discussion, and we stand a good chance of getting it." (p.5)

Pipeline mix: "about 60% of the current opportunity pipeline is related to the heating equipment, while the remaining 40% comprises process plants and other special fabricated equipment and adjacent technology-led EPC opportunities." (p.2-3)

## Management Commentary — Margins

"Now what has happened is with Chemdist being -- I mean, okay, it's being the first quarter and just the initial stages. So there has been a loss of -- operating loss of about INR 3.6 crores. So that's why the EBITDA has come down to about 11.8%." (p.7)

"the commodities prices are fluctuating. But as far as ongoing projects are concerned, these are already factored in our costing. And most of the orders are already placed, and we placed the order on our sub vendor on a fixed price basis." (p.8)

New segments: "we are always looking at in the similar margin range of whatever our guided EBITDA margins are there, anywhere from 12% to 14% in that range." (p.9)

BPCL Bina: "the execution would happen this year and next year, both the years uniformly. And overall, EBITDA would be in line with whatever we have guided for this year." (p.15)

## Management Commentary — Capacity / Expansion

"Our upcoming avenue are the offshore and metals and minerals industries with a focus also on renewable energy." (p.3)

"coming to around 3 to 5 years, we want to move this non-heating segment to around 40% of our revenue" — Mr. Dipak Bharuka (p.5)

TAM (India only): "offshore, currently, only in India, the TAM is somewhere around $300 million to $500 million. And in metals and minerals, it is about $500 million to $1 billion." Project size: "around $30 million to $50 million to $60 million kind of a project." (p.9)

Hit ratio in new sectors: "we are expecting our hit ratio should be anything around 10% to 12%, not more than that." (p.9)

Iraq: "we have just opened -- or we have kind of taken a Board approval yesterday to register a branch office." "Currently, our mandate is, this will be more of a registered paper office." (p.8, p.10)

Green hydrogen (Chemdist): "for the green hydrogen current order execution, what they are doing is about INR 50 crores. And that should get completed almost say, in this year and may get spilled over some to Q1 of next year." (p.13)

Working capital: "even in BPCL Bina, for example, the payment terms have been quite, I would say, more friendly where not too much of a negative cash flow is there" (p.11); JNK Global role: "they submit the bank guarantees and they get the payments and then the payment is passed on to us." (p.11)

Revenue recognition: "it's whatever now we are going on the input method." (p.16)

## Named Catalysts

- Order finalization for refinery and fertilizer export projects in Q2/Q3 FY27 ("order finalization should happen in Q2, Q3")
- Dangote Phase 2: "still under the discussion"; no commitment disclosed
- BPCL Bina: "a significant portion of the project revenue is expected to be recognized during FY27 and also on FY28" (p.2)
- JNK Chemdist expected to be in the green by FY27 year-end
- Bank guarantee limit enhancement with banks for new contracts
- Technology-partner tie-ups: "we are already tied up with 3, 4 such technology partners with whom we are currently bidding"
- Orders from JNK Global in order book: "4 projects in our order book from JNK Global ... BPCL Bina ... one USA project ... Petronas Phoenix Project" (p.10)

## Named Risks

- Order cancellation (8 Jun 2026 export order): "The large export order received on June 8, 2026, was subsequently canceled solely due to the technical approval requirements ... the approval did not materialize in time." "Importantly, the cancellation happened at a very early stage, and we had not incurred costs against this order." Licensor not named ("We cannot disclose the name of the licensor"). Licensor later qualified JNK for future projects: "for future projects, we have received the qualification from them." (p.3-4, p.15)
- Back-ended revenue / seasonality: "some degree of seasonality will remain." (p.2)
- JNK Global governance dispute (analyst question): "though it is -- the matter is sub judice, we would not like to comment on that as of now." Management: "JNK India itself is capable to execute any of these projects on our own as well." (p.14)
- JNK Global cooperation agreement: "it is not yet renewed ... But our agreement remains the same." (p.12)
- JNK Chemdist "relatively high fixed cost base at this stage" causing Q1 operating losses (p.4)
- Presentation error: standalone numbers "some kind of error"; revised filing made (p.14)
- Export projects with JNK Global are "back-to-back"; domestic qualification needed for standalone work (p.10)
