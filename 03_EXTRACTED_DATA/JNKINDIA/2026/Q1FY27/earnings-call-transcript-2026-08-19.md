## Management Commentary — Demand

- "We continue to see encouraging traction across both domestic and international markets. Our current overall opportunity pipeline is more than INR 6,000 crores with a broadly balanced 50:50% mix between international and domestic opportunities."
- "On the export side, Africa remains an important market for us, particularly countries such as Nigeria and Ethiopia, where significant investments are being planned across refining, petrochemicals and fertilizers."
- Pipeline split (Arvind Kamath): "the export opportunity is about 50%. That is around INR 3,000 crores." and "the other opportunities are domestic, which is INR 3,000-plus crores."
- Timing of pipeline (Arvind Kamath): "So overall, we expect that all this pipeline should get finalized in this financial year."
- New-segment hit ratio (Dipak Bharuka): "we are expecting around 10% as our hit ratio, at least to start within the next couple of years."
- TAM, India only (Arvind Kamath): "currently, only in India, the TAM is somewhere around $300 million to $500 million" (offshore) and "in metals and minerals, it is about $500 million to $1 billion."
- Dangote (Dipak Bharuka): "Now Phase 2 is still under the discussion, and we stand a good chance of getting it."

## Management Commentary — Margins

- Chemdist drag (Arvind Kamath): "So there has been a loss of -- operating loss of about INR 3.6 crores. So that's why the EBITDA has come down to about 11.8%."
- Chemdist contribution: "the JV has contributed 8.8% to the group revenue in the Q1 of FY27."
- Operating leverage: "as the business scales up through the upcoming quarters, we expect the operating leverage to improve meaningfully."
- Chemdist breakeven (Dipak Bharuka): "our expectation is that by year-end, we should be able to get it into the green."
- Input costs: "as far as ongoing projects are concerned, these are already factored in our costing. And most of the orders are already placed, and we placed the order on our sub vendor on a fixed price basis."
- New business margin (Arvind Kamath answering Nikhil Kanodia): "any business we enter, we kind of look to maintaining this margin."
