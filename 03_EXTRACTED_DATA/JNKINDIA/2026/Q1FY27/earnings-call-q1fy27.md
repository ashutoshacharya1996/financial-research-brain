## Guidance

"Our revenue growth guidance of around 20% to 25% remains intact. We also maintain our full year EBITDA margin guidance of about 12% to 14%." — Arvind Kamath

"Historically, Q1 contributes about 10% to 15% of our full year revenue, while H1 contributes 30% to 35%. H2 accounts for the remaining 60% to 70% of our annual revenue." — Arvind Kamath (seasonality guidance)

"For JNK Chemdist, we are expecting the revenue anywhere about 10% to 15% of JNK India revenue in this financial year or next couple of years... gross margins... around, say, 20% or so." — Arvind Kamath

"our expectation is that by year-end, we should be able to get it [Chemdist] into the green." — Dipak Bharuka

Note on prior guidance discrepancy raised on the call: "in the last con call, if I am correct, we were guiding roughly around 14% to 15% margin. So now we are saying 12% to 14%. Can you clarify on that?" (Kamlesh Bagmar, analyst) — Dipak Bharuka responded: "For our understanding, what we have announced was 12% to 14%... there is no change from like what -- from the last 3 months or 6 months, nothing has changed on -- as far as our margin projection goes. It remains the same." [Note: this contradicts the Q4FY26 call transcript, which explicitly guided 14-15% EBITDA margin — see earnings-call.md Q4FY26. Flagging as an inconsistency in stated guidance across quarters.]

## Management Commentary — Demand

"We continue to see encouraging traction across both domestic and international markets... about 60% of the current opportunity pipeline is related to the heating equipment, while the remaining 40% comprises process plants and other special fabricated equipment and adjacent technology-led EPC opportunities." — Arvind Kamath

"On the export side, Africa remains an important market for us, particularly countries such as Nigeria and Ethiopia, where significant investments are being planned across refining, petrochemicals and fertilizers." — Arvind Kamath

"on the Dangote, as you know, we had -- we were the supplier for the Phase 1. Now Phase 2 is still under the discussion, and we stand a good chance of getting it." — Dipak Bharuka

"both refinery and fertilizer, the order finalization should happen in Q2, Q3." — Arvind Kamath

## Management Commentary — Margins

"our gross margin has actually improved, but EBITDA margin has come down... for JNK India alone, we have achieved an EBITDA of 14%, which is as per the guideline... Now what has happened is with Chemdist being -- I mean, okay, it's being the first quarter and just the initial stages. So there has been a loss of -- operating loss of about INR 3.6 crores." — Arvind Kamath

"the commodities prices are fluctuating. But as far as ongoing projects are concerned, these are already factored in our costing... most of the orders are already placed, and we placed the order on our sub vendor on a fixed price basis. So that whatever the fluctuation is happening, it is not having any major impact on our margins" — Dipak Bharuka

## Management Commentary — Capacity / Expansion

"we are taking further steps to diversify our business into new and adjacent opportunities... Our upcoming avenue are the offshore and metals and minerals industries with a focus also on renewable energy." — Arvind Kamath

"coming to around 3 to 5 years, we want to move this non-heating segment to around 40% of our revenue" — Dipak Bharuka

"why JNK has opted to open an overseas office in Iraq... we -- our mandate is, this will be more of a registered paper office. Going forward, we will evaluate the opportunities based on case-to-case basis, we'll start with the proper sales office." — Dipak Bharuka

"on the metals and minerals side, now we are first -- we are bidding for a couple of projects on the material handling side... electric arc furnaces, which they require for the steel melting and ore melting." — Dipak Bharuka

"the TAM every business has a different in, say, offshore, currently, only in India, the TAM is somewhere around $300 million to $500 million. And in metals and minerals, it is about $500 million to $1 billion." — Arvind Kamath

## Named Catalysts

- Iraq branch office — Board approval taken to register overseas branch office in Republic of Iraq (approved "yesterday" relative to call date); registered-office-only initially, sales office to follow based on opportunities
- Dangote Phase 2 (Nigeria) — still under discussion, "good chance of getting it" (repeat order)
- Refinery and fertilizer order finalizations expected Q2/Q3 FY27
- Export bid pipeline (~Rs. 3,000 cr, ~50% of total) expected to finalize "soon"
- Domestic non-heating pipeline (~Rs. 3,000+ cr) — finalization expected in 3-6 / 6-8 months
- Offshore/metals & minerals/renewable energy diversification — technology partnerships with 3-4 partners already tied up
- JNK Chemdist expected to reach breakeven ("into the green") by year-end FY27
- NTPC-related fertilizer/renewable fuel opportunities (Nano Urea, green ammonia, green hydrogen plants) — part of bid pipeline

## Named Risks

- Large export order (received June 8, 2026) cancelled — "solely due to the technical approval requirements. The international EPC contractor who had awarded the contract to JNK India was confident of securing the required technical approval from the licensor. However, the approval did not materialize in time." No material cash loss; described as "exceptional and rare occurrence... not related to our execution capability, technical performance, efficiency or commercial competitiveness." First such incident in "last 15 years" per Dipak Bharuka.
- JNK Global (Korea parent/partner) activist investor dispute — "there is some fight going on between an activist investor, MJ Partners. And with the Board... matter has landed in the court" — raised by analyst Rupesh Tatiya as a risk to bank guarantee support for Nigeria project bidding. Management response: "we are not comprehending any issues at JNK Global... though it is -- the matter is sub judice, we would not like to comment on that as of now... JNK India itself is capable to execute any of these projects on our own as well."
- Standalone financial results filing contained an arithmetic/presentation error on PAT margin rows in the original investor presentation, corrected same-day: "I think there was some kind of error in the standalone numbers... Mainly profit after tax margins. Those numbers are more of arithmetic errors on the bottom few rows, and that is being corrected." — Dipak Bharuka (see investor-presentation-q1fy27-original-superseded.md)
- New segment (offshore/metals & minerals) hit rate expected to be lower: "around 10% to 12%, not more than that" vs 20-25% in core heating business, given lack of track record
- Q1 seasonally the weakest quarter — 10-15% of full-year revenue
