## Guidance

- Revenue: "Our revenue growth guidance of around 20% to 25% remains intact."
- EBITDA margin: "We also maintain our full year EBITDA margin guidance of about 12% to 14%."
- Margin guidance clarification, Q&A: analyst: "we were guiding roughly around 14% to 15% margin. So now we are saying 12% to 14%." Management: "For our understanding, what we have announced was 12% to 14%. But for some reason, we are missing something, these numbers, we'll recheck them."
- Medium-term mix: "coming to around 3 to 5 years, we want to move this non-heating segment to around 40% of our revenue"
- Chemdist revenue: "we are expecting the revenue anywhere about 10% to 15% of JNK India revenue in this financial year or next couple of years."
- Chemdist gross margin: "they would also be in the similar line as around, say, 20% or so."
- Chemdist profitability: "our expectation is that by year-end, we should be able to get it into the green."
- Seasonality: "Historically, Q1 contributes about 10% to 15% of our full year revenue, while H1 contributes 30% to 35%."

Note: the Aug 14 corporate presentation states "On track for EBITDA margin guidance of 14 -15% and revenue growth of 25-30% for FY27". This conflicts with the call guidance above.

## Management Commentary — Demand

- "We continue to see encouraging traction across both domestic and international markets. Our current overall opportunity pipeline is more than INR 6,000 crores with a broadly balanced 50:50% mix between international and domestic opportunities."
- "On the export side, Africa remains an important market for us, particularly countries such as Nigeria and Ethiopia, where significant investments are being planned across refining, petrochemicals and fertilizers."
- "about 60% of the current opportunity pipeline is related to the heating equipment, while the remaining 40% comprises process plants and other special fabricated equipment and adjacent technology-led EPC opportunities."
- Hit rate, traditional: "our hit rate has been 20% to 25%"
- Hit rate, new segments: "we are expecting our hit ratio should be anything around 10% to 12%, not more than that."
- Pipeline timing: "we expect all this pipeline should get finalized in this financial year."

## Management Commentary — Margins

- "for JNK India alone, we have achieved an EBITDA of 14%, which is as per the guideline"
- "there has been a loss of -- operating loss of about INR 3.6 crores. So that's why the EBITDA has come down to about 11.8%."
- Commodity costs: "as far as ongoing projects are concerned, these are already factored in our costing. And most of the orders are already placed, and we placed the order on our sub vendor on a fixed price basis."
- Margin range for new businesses: "we are always looking at in the similar margin range of whatever our guided EBITDA margins are there, anywhere from 12% to 14% in that range."
- Chemdist operating leverage: "we expect the operating leverage to improve meaningfully."

## Management Commentary — Capacity / Expansion

- "Our upcoming avenue are the offshore and metals and minerals industries with a focus also on renewable energy."
- Diversification sizing: "the TAM is somewhere around $300 million to $500 million" (offshore, India); "in metals and minerals, it is about $500 million to $1 billion" (India)
- Project size: "anywhere between something like, say, around $30 million to $50 million to $60 million kind of a project."
- Iraq: "we have kind of taken a Board approval yesterday to register a branch office. And we do have an agent there"
- Iraq mandate: "Currently, our mandate is, this will be more of a registered paper office."
- Export execution model: "All export projects, we will be doing together. I mean, JNK Global will be getting the orders and they will be subcontracting to us. It is a back-to-back basis."
- Qualification gap: "we need a technology partner, and we are already tied up with 3, 4 such technology partners"
- Power segment: "Yes, but that's not in the power sector. That's in the -- they are putting up the Nano Urea plant."

- Seasonality: "some degree of seasonality will remain."
