## Named Risks

- "Dependence on Capital Expenditure Cycles": "A significant portion of industry revenue depends on large refinery and petrochemical projects. Delays in multi-crore projects can materially impact order inflows and execution schedules. Lower crude oil prices or economic slowdowns may reduce future investments."
- "Project Execution and Supply Chain Risks": "Supply chain disruptions, logistics bottlenecks and approval delays can affect timelines. Cost overruns and delayed completion can adversely impact profitability."
