## Named Catalysts

- Dangote Phase 2 refinery (Nigeria) — fired heater and reformer inquiries received, prequalified; fired heater finalization expected Q1 FY27, reformer Q2/Q3 FY27
- Waste gas handling export opportunity (~Rs. 200-250 cr) — commercial discussions final stage, expected close within 1-2 weeks of call date
- Fertilizer project reformer — bid submission Q1 FY27, finalization expected Q2 or Q3 FY27
- Refinery bid (unnamed) — finalization expected Q1 FY27 (June/July)
- Middle East fertilizer/petchem opportunity — $200-300m over next 2-3 years
- Russia project — advanced discussion stage, finalization expected in a quarter or two
- Cracking furnace opportunity (BPCL Andhra, possible Africa cracker) — expected FY28
- JNK Chemdist green hydrogen project (Hydrogen Valley, Pune) — awarded, to be executed in FY27

## Named Risks

- "Clean aviation fuel project" — price bid opened, JNK not lowest bidder, opportunity lost
- Domestic large refinery projects (BPCL Andhra, IOCL Paradip, Haldia, MRPL) delayed 6 months to a year due to "current situations, and also the crude oil pricing, which is affecting the Indian refiners a bit"
- Commodity/raw material pricing exposure on multi-year contracts — "we do not have the clauses to pass it on to the customer" for most non-PSU contracts, though direct commodity exposure is described as "quite limited"
- Geopolitical/war situation — "there could be a little bit of issues in terms of the commodity pricing," minor shipment delays noted
- Historically negative operating cash flow (improving but only marginally positive)
