## Named Risks

- "Dependence on Capital Expenditure Cycles: A significant portion of industry revenue depends on large refinery and petrochemical projects."
- "Raw Material Price Volatility: Steel accounts for a major share of equipment manufacturing costs ... Sudden increases can pressure margins, particularly under fixed-price contracts."
- "Intense Industry Competition", "Project Execution and Supply Chain Risks: Large industrial projects often involve execution periods of 12-36 months."
- IPO proceeds delay: "there is a delay in the utilisation. The balance amount of ₹ 2.65 million shall be utilized in Fiscal 2027."
- Cautionary statement: "Investors should not rely solely on projected opportunities, order book size, or past revenue growth"
