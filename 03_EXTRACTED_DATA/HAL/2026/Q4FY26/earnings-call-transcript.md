## Guidance

- Revenue (Barenya Senapati, Director Finance): "As far as revenue growth is concerned, we are hopeful that we'll be maintaining a double-digit growth in the current financial year '26-'27."
- Revenue quantified: "So double- digit means you cannot expect a significant increase like 15% to 20%. Yes, it will be in the range of 10% to 12% minimum."
- EBITDA margin: "And the EBITDA margin, we have always maintained around 30%, 31%, and it will be maintained."
- Order pipeline: "In total, we expect to receive orders of INR90,000 crores, including the ROH orders, during the next 2 years."
- LCA Mk1A deliveries (Ravi Kota, CMD): "we are hopeful that by August or September, we should be able to start the deliveries." / "That's 20, this year, we are planning to deliver."
- HTT-40: "And we are planning around 20 plus this year to deliver."
- AL-31FP / RD-33: "Our target is around 32, 35 engines per annum of AL-31 FP. And RD-33, only 15 engines are left, and those 15 will be delivered in the current financial year, '26, '27."
- Mix: "The composition only, it will be now 30-70, it will be in the range of 50-50."

## Management Commentary — Demand

- "The order pipeline looks even more promising with additional anticipated contracts of 143 ALH for the Army, IAF Su-30 upgrade, upgrade of 40 Do-228, etc., are at various stages of approval."
- "Major orders bagged include contracts for the supply of 97 LCA Mark-1A to IAF, quantity 6 ALH Mark III MR to ICG, quantity 8 Do-228 to ICG, quantity 10 ALH Dhruv NG to Pawan Hans, quantity 2 Hindustan-228 to Guyana."
- "Deliveries of ALH helicopters, AL-35FP engines, RD-33 engines and ROH revenues helped offset delays in Tejas Mark-1A and HTT-40 programs."
- "spares sales have actually come down by around INR500 crores to INR600 crores in the current financial year compared to the last financial year. But the repair and overhaul are almost at the same level because we have not added any new platform"
- Su-30 upgrade: "we are expecting the CCS approval for the current financial year. So the design will take probably 5 to 6 years."
- Tejas Mk-II order: "we are expecting by the end of maybe another 2 years, down the line, we can expect the order."

## Management Commentary — Margins

- "See for '26-'27, already we have the inventory." / "So there will not be any cost increase as such for '26-'27."
- "And as far as the ERV exchange rate, this is already mitigated. It is factored into the contract."
- "material escalation is already factored into the contract itself."
- "Though revenue there -- I mean, the profitability on repair and overhaul, a little bit higher than manufacturing." / "And that 30% will be maintained."

## Management Commentary — Capacity / Expansion

- "Production capacity of 8 LCA Mark-1A per annum established with the inauguration of the third production line at HAL Nashik"
- "Additionally, we plan to develop manufacturing infrastructure for LCA Mark-II, GE 414 engines, and IMRH engines, infrastructure facilities for manufacturing SSLV, indigenization of aero engine projects"
- "We have 3 lines now, 2 in Bangalore and 1 in Nashik. Not a single production line is idle"
- "we are trying to outsource because we have partnered with so many people outside to do the main -- full structures so that I will be the core integrator."
- Hiring: "we have not closed our recruitment process also. It will be a balancing one, more of outsourcing, in-sourcing, and some recruitment will also be there in due course."

## Named Catalysts

- LCA Mk1A first deliveries "by August or September" 2026; ~20 aircraft targeted FY27 (engine-dependent: "From August, they have committed 2")
- HTT-40 deliveries FY27, "around 20 plus this year"
- Tejas Mk-II rollout: "we are expecting that by March, we should be able to roll out Mark II."
- Su-30 MKI (12): "as per contract, starts from '27, '28 ... only 1 aircraft due" then "complete all 11 In 28-29"
- LCH deliveries "as per contract '27-'28"
- Pipeline: 143 ALH (Army), Su-30 upgrade (CCS approval expected FY27), 40 Do-228 upgrade — ~₹90,000 crore orders over 2 years
- CATS Warrior UAV flight "expecting next year, not this year"

## Named Risks

- LCA Mk1A delay / testing: "There are few tests still undergoing now ... we are hoping to resolve everything and stabilize by August or September."
- GE F404 engine supply: "The supply chain issue is global. It has not spared anybody. Even the big players like GE or Honeywell"
- Vendor slowness: "I will not say disruption, but slowness is there."
- Revenue recognition lumpiness: "It's against delivery." (revenue recognised on delivery, not percentage-of-completion)
- Uttam radar not in 83-aircraft batch: "as of now, it is not coming in the 83 program. So in the 97 program, we are planning to integrate this."
- AMCA: HAL not assured of production role — "they have not indicated that the same person will do production."
- GE penalty unquantified: "we cannot quantify them right now."
