## Named Catalysts

- None named directly; results filing is procedural. Notable disclosed items:
  - Note 13: Offset Credit benefits accounting treatment change (per EAC/ICAI opinion received 7 March 2026) recognised prospectively — increased FY26 revenue by ₹113.17 crore and profit by ₹95.27 crore (non-recurring accounting policy change, one-time boost).
  - Note 12: FPQ (Firm Price Quote) finalization pending since 3rd PPRC (up to FY22-23); 4th PPRC still in progress — FY24, FY25, FY26 sales provisionally recognised using FY22-23 approved prices with indexation, creating pricing uncertainty until finalized.

## Named Risks

- Note 1: "In the absence of requisite number of independent directors, the Company is not able to reconstitute an Audit Committee in pursuance of Section 177 of the Companies Act, 2013 and regulation 18(1) of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015 till date of approval of financial results" — ongoing governance/compliance gap.
- HALBIT Avionics Private Ltd (JV): "Material Uncertainty Related to Going Concern" — net liability of Rs 1,279.95 lakh and net current liability of Rs 1,355.33 lakh as at 31 March 2026.
- HATSOFF Helicopter Training Private Ltd (JV): going-concern disclosure — current liabilities exceeded current assets by Rs 9,262.04 lakh; net worth Rs 2,337.46 lakh (up from Rs 337.10 lakh prior year); ongoing OEM (CAE Inc, Canada) obsolescence/delivery dispute on Military Dhruv Cockpit (delayed since 2008 contract).
- Multirole Transport Aircraft Ltd (JV): in-principle Board approval for voluntary liquidation, pending Government of India approval — wind-down in progress.
- Infotech HAL Ltd (JV): under NCLT corporate insolvency resolution process (petition admitted 22 August 2025) — see later dissolution filing dated August 2026.
- Karnataka High Court litigation on pay-fixation recovery: court ruled in favour of officers (2024-25) and, per order dated 10 March 2026, in favour of workmen — differential amounts withheld to be released/refunded during FY27, creating a cash outflow/liability not fully quantified in this filing beyond specific reversal entries (e.g., Rs 185.65 crore reversed for officers in FY25, Rs 163.90 crore for workmen in FY26).
- FPQ pricing not yet finalized beyond 2022-23 (4th PPRC in progress) — revenue recognized provisionally, creating retrospective pricing/true-up risk.
