## Guidance

- **Revenue growth FY27**: "double-digit growth in the current financial year '26-'27"; quantified later as "10% to 12% minimum" (Barenya Senapati).
- **EBITDA margin FY27**: "around 30%, 31%, and it will be maintained" (Barenya Senapati); reaffirmed later in the call — "that 30% will be maintained."
- **LCA Mark-1A deliveries FY27**: Target ~20 aircraft this financial year — "by end of this year around 15 to 20... so that's why I told 20 LCA we will deliver this year," starting from August 2026 ("June, we are expecting 1 engine now. Then afterwards, it is going to August. From August, they have committed 2.").
- **Capex plan**: "We plan to invest around INR12,000 crores in these projects by 2030" (LCA Mark-II, GE414 engines, IMRH engines, SSLV manufacturing infrastructure, aero-engine indigenization facilities).
- **Revenue mix shift**: Management expects manufacturing:ROH composition to move from ~28-30:70 toward "50-50" over time, while asserting no margin stress from this shift.
- **Su-30 MKI (12 aircraft) order**: "starts from '27, '28... only 1 aircraft due as per contract" that year; "next year, we are going to complete all 11 In 28-29" (11 units in FY29).
- **Su-30 upgrade programme**: Design phase expected to take "5 to 6 years"; CCS approval expected "for the current financial year"; production starts only after design completion.
- **Tejas Mark-II**: Prototype rollout targeted "last quarter of this year" — "we are expecting that by March, we should be able to roll out Mark II."

## Management Commentary — Demand

- "Considering the major orders in-hand, envisaging new orders, and to ensure timely delivery to the customers, production capacity ramped up for the following major projects, namely LCA and HTT-40 manufacturing at Bangalore and Nashik plants. LCH manufacturing at Bengaluru and Tumkuru plants will be the focus areas going forward."
- "Major orders bagged include contracts for the supply of 97 LCA Mark-1A to IAF, quantity 6 ALH Mark III MR to ICG, quantity 8 Do-228 to ICG, quantity 10 ALH Dhruv NG to Pawan Hans, quantity 2 H[elicopters]..." (sentence cut off in source at page break).
- On pipeline (~₹9,00,000 million / ₹90,000 crore over 2 years incl. ROH, referenced by an analyst and not disputed by management): "The major contributor will be the ALH 137 program. And as usual, your ROH program will be at the same level."
- On AMCA: "the current tender, whatever the tender that is going to come is, is that RFI, which was placed, is for the D&D program only, that is to build prototypes. And they have not indicated that the same person will do production. So we are hopeful that we will participate in that."
- On SU-57: "it's the government's call, so we cannot comment on that right now."
- ALH legacy contracts: "the deliveries of ALH, we have no issues. We have delivered everything as per contract."
- New civil/diversification programmes cited: SSLV Technology Transfer Agreement with ISRO/IN-SPACe/NSIL (signed 10 Sept 2025); SJ-100 civil commuter aircraft MoU with Russia's UAC (27 Oct 2025); Safran Aircraft Engines agreement (June 2025) for industrialization/production of LEAP engine rotating parts (A320neo, 737 MAX).

## Management Commentary — Margins

- "As far as revenue growth is concerned, we are hopeful that we'll be maintaining a double-digit growth in the current financial year '26-'27. And the EBITDA margin, we have always maintained around 30%, 31%, and it will be maintained." (Barenya Senapati)
- On input cost / commodity pass-through risk: "there will not be any cost increase as such for '26-'27... as the purchase orders have been placed for 83 LCA long back, our inventories have been purcha[sed]..." and "material escalation is already factored into the contract itself... we don't see any significant increase in the material cost also in our future program because 97 LCA, already we have placed purchase orders."
- On mix-shift margin risk (manufacturing growing faster than ROH): "there is no reduction in the repair and overhaul sales... And the manufacturing as well, manufacturing sales will grow. The composition only, it will be now 30-70, it will be in the range of 50-50... there will not be any stress on the profitability from the manufacturing contract... that 30% will be maintained."
- On provisioning: last year's lower comparability was due to a one-off — "last time, the provision we made because the 1 SU-30 aircraft got into an accident. So, for the replacement purpose, we made a provision of INR800 crores. And that situation is not there, in the current year... there cannot be any guidance percentage of revenue and in provision."

## Management Commentary — Capacity / Expansion

- "we are continuously investing in our future through investment in building capabilities in the form of capex and in our future capabilities through R&D expenditure."
- "we plan to develop manufacturing infrastructure for LCA Mark-II, GE 414 engines, and IMRH engines, infrastructure facilities for manufacturing SSLV, indigenization of aero engine projects, etc., along with facilities to support design and development activities for IMRH and other projects. We plan to invest around INR12,000 crores in these projects by 2030."
- On LCA production lines: "We have 3 lines now, 2 in Bangalore and 1 in Nashik. Not a single production line is idle because we are continuously building the structures and equipping them... we were able to do what we showed at the end of March, 21-22 structures ready."
- On manpower/outsourcing strategy: "we have partnered with so many people outside to do the main... full structures so that I will be the core integrator. And we are trying to in-source as much as possible so that my manpower cost remains as low as possible."
- IPR filings: "During the year, HAL has filed 223 IPR applications, which made the cumulative number of 2,842 IPRs filed by the company. Further 84 IPRs have been granted during the year, taking cumulative IPRs held by organization to 1,226."

## Named Catalysts

- LCA Tejas Mark-1A deliveries resuming/ramping from August–September 2026 (GE F404 engine deliveries: 1 engine in June, 2 from August, "by end of this year around 15 to 20" — guided 20 for FY27).
- Tejas Mark-II prototype rollout targeted by March 2027 (last quarter of FY27, per call framing).
- Su-30 MKI 12-aircraft order: first delivery FY28, remaining 11 aircraft completed by FY29.
- Su-30 upgrade programme: CCS approval expected in the current financial year (FY27); ~5-6 year design phase ahead of production.
- 97 LCA Mark-1A order to IAF, plus ALH Mark III MR (ICG), Do-228 (ICG), ALH Dhruv NG (Pawan Hans) orders bagged during FY26.
- Large forward order pipeline referenced by an analyst (~₹90,000 crore over 2 years including ROH) covering 143 ALH and 40 Dornier aircraft and other unconfirmed programmes (UH-M, 12 LUH Limited Series Production) — not yet finalized, so management declined to quantify.
- Diversification: SSLV Technology Transfer Agreement with ISRO/IN-SPACe/NSIL; SJ-100 civil aircraft MoU with Russia's UAC; Safran LEAP-engine rotating-parts industrialization agreement (global aerospace supply-chain entry).
- AMCA: HAL "hopeful" of participating (current RFI is design & development only; production allocation undecided).

## Named Risks

- GE F404 engine supply delays continuing to constrain LCA Mark-1A deliveries — attributed to a "global" supply chain issue ("casting issues" traced to Tier 2/Tier 3 suppliers) affecting GE and Honeywell alike; management flagged possible GE delivery penalties but declined to quantify ("It's not the right time to say what the penalties will be — we cannot quantify them right now").
- Execution/software refinement risk on LCA Mark-1A — "some refinements are required" in ongoing test iterations before IAF acceptance; management targets stabilization by August/September 2026.
- Uttam Radar integration is DRDO-controlled and not yet part of the 83-aircraft LCA program; planned for the 97-aircraft program instead — a scope/timeline dependency outside HAL's control.
- ROH/spares revenue growth has slowed — "spares sales have actually come down by around INR500 crores to INR600 crores in the current financial year compared to the last financial year."
- Manpower/capacity strain flagged by an analyst given "5, 6 programs in parallel" over the next two years plus rising private-sector competition for talent (management response: still balancing recruitment, no committed peak-delivery figure given for FY28/29).
- Prior-year provision spike (₹800 crore, from a Su-30 aircraft accident) makes YoY margin/provisioning comparisons non-representative; no forward provisioning guidance given (event-driven, not a run-rate).
