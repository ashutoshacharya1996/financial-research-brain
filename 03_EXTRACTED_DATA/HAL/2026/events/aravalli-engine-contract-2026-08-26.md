## Named Catalysts
- **Aravalli Helicopter Engine Development**: Final contract signed between HAL and Safran Helicopter Engines (via JV SAFHAL) for design, development, manufacture, supply, and lifecycle support of a new-generation high-power helicopter engine in the 3,500–4,000 shp power class.
- **IMRH & DBMRH Power Plant**: Aravalli engine selected to power HAL's upcoming 13-ton Indian Multi-Role Helicopter (IMRH) and its naval variant, Deck-Based Multi-Role Helicopter (DBMRH).
- **First Safran Co-Development at this class**: "This is the first time; Safran HE has taken up such a class of Engine as Co-development."
- **Civil market potential**: Offshore transportation, utility missions, VVIP transport identified as future growth avenues beyond military.
- **MRO capabilities**: Programme expected to catalyse indigenous MRO ecosystem in India.
- **Safran long-term partnership expansion**: Building on decades of partnership (Artouste engines for Cheetah/Chetak; Shakti engine family for ALH/LCH/LUH).
