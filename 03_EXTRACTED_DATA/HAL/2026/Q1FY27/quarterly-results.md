## Named Risks
- Joint Venture Going Concern: HATSOFF Helicopter Training JV has material uncertainty related to going concern; accumulated losses eroding networth (Rs. 3,129.90 lakhs as at 30-Jun-26).
- Inventory write-downs: Customer-owned LCA TD store inventory damaged in Sep 2022 flood; provision of Rs. 1,091 lakhs for replacement charges ongoing; excess provision of Rs. 4,499 lakhs reversed Mar 31, 2026.
- FPQ (Fixed Price Quotation) pricing pending: Prices for 2023-24 onwards yet to be firmed up; 4th PPRC (Periodic Price Revision Committee) under progress; prices provisionally recognized using 2024-25 indices without escalation.
- Board composition: Company not complying with SEBI Regulation 17(1) and Companies Act Section 149(4) due to insufficient independent directors; Audit Committee and Nomination & Remuneration Committee not constituted since 5-Apr-26.
- Employee cost increase: Gratuity ceiling increased from Rs. 20 lakhs to Rs. 25 lakhs from 01-Oct-25 (IDA revised to 51.80%); additional liability Rs. 32,733 lakhs in FY26, Rs. 237 lakhs in Q1FY27.
