## Named Risks

- Note 1 (unchanged from Q4FY26 filing): "In the absence of requisite number of independent directors, the Company is not able to reconstitute an Audit Committee in pursuance of Section 177 of the Companies Act, 2013 and regulation 18(1) of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015 till date of approval of financial results" — governance gap persists into Q1FY27.
- Note 7 (FPQ pricing): "Since FPQ finalisation has taken more than three years, on conservative basis the prices of 2024-25 have been considered for the financial year 2025-26 and June 2026 without any escalation" — pricing for FY24-25 onward still not finalized (4th PPRC pending), same structural risk carried forward from Q4FY26 filing.
- HALBIT Avionics Private Ltd (JV): "Material Uncertainty Related to Going Concern" persists — net liability of Rs 1,146.11 lakh and net current liability of Rs 1,221.72 lakh as at 30 June 2026 (improved slightly from March 2026: Rs 1,279.95 lakh / Rs 1,355.33 lakh).
- HATSOFF Helicopter Training Private Ltd (JV): going-concern note continues — current liabilities exceeded current assets by Rs 8,262.39 lakh as at 30 June 2026; unresolved CAE Inc (Canada) Military Dhruv Cockpit delivery dispute continues.
- HAL-Edgewood Technologies Private Ltd (JV): "has not prepared the financial statements for the three months period ended 30 June 2026, and accordingly not considered for consolidation" — ongoing wind-down/inactive JV (Board approved voluntary winding up in 2017).
- Multirole Transport Aircraft Ltd (JV) and Infotech HAL Ltd (JV): both continuing wind-down/insolvency processes noted (see events files for Infotech HAL's subsequent NCLT dissolution, 20 August 2026).
