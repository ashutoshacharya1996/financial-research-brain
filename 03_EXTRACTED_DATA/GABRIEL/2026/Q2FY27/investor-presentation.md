- Forvia JV: "JV between Gabriel India Ltd. (GIL) and Forvia to enter the business of manufacturing PV seat structures and complete seats in India"; "GIL holds 50% less 1 share in the JV (entity incorporated on 19th October 2026)"

- "Capex for the period ended Sep-26 stood at Rs.667 Mn."
- "Increase in Net Worth is on account of the Strategic Investments done through Project Jupiter and Project Rise."
