## Named Catalysts

- Gabriel India is acquiring equity shares of **HL Mando ANAND India Private Limited** (formerly known as Mando Automotive India Private Limited) from Asia Investments Private Limited (AIPL), a Promoter of the Company. Part of the consideration is being settled via a preferential allotment of 1,44,04,204 equity shares (non-cash consideration), aggregating ₹18,81,03,05,962.
- "In continuation of our earlier intimation dated July 21, 2026 and pursuant to the approval accorded by the members of the Company at the Annual General Meeting held on August 19, 2026 ... the Board of Directors ... through resolution passed by circulation today, i.e. September 11, 2026, has approved the allotment."
- This is consistent with the promoter group's (Anand Group) broader internal restructuring already flagged in company_master.csv notes (Scheme of Arrangement, effective May 22, 2026), bringing the Mando automotive JV/entity under the Gabriel India listed structure.
