### Transaction Structure
- Gabriel India to acquire 3,78,44,999 equity shares of HL Klemove India Pvt Ltd from HL Klemove Corporation (Korea), representing 30% minus 1 share of paid-up capital
- Resultant JV shareholding ratio: HL Klemove 70% + 1 share : Gabriel India 30% − 1 share
- Target Company's authorized share capital: INR 129 Cr; paid-up share capital: INR 126.15 Cr
- Board composition of Target Company post-deal: 10 directors — 6 nominated by HL Klemove, 4 by Gabriel India; two Co-Chairpersons (one from each party, alternating); Managing Director nominated by HL Klemove (reports functionally to both parties); 2 Joint MDs (one nominated by each party)
- A Corporate Services Agreement (CSA) will be executed between Anand Automotive Private Limited (a Gabriel promoter-group entity) and the Target Company for operational/management support services
- Existing License and Technical Assistance Agreement and Brand Sub License Agreement between HL Klemove and the Target Company continue in effect

## Named Risks

- "Post acquisition by Gabriel India, Target Company will become an Associate Company of Gabriel India and will be considered to be a Related Party of Gabriel India" — ongoing related-party monitoring implied
- Deal structured in two deferred tranches over up to 18 months, introducing execution/timing risk on the second (25%) payment
- "Applicable stamp duty, transfer taxes and other transaction-related costs, if any, shall be borne by Gabriel India" — additional transaction costs beyond headline consideration
- Governance complexity: MD nominated solely by HL Klemove despite Gabriel holding ~30% and having "functional reporting relationship to both HL Klemove and Gabriel India" — shared/co-chair governance structure with affirmative voting rights on reserved matters and standard deadlock clauses in the JVA
