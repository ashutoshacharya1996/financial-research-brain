## Guidance (agency expectations — not management guidance)

- "Project Jupiter ... is expected to be completed by September-October 2026."

## Management Commentary — Margins (as reported by Crisil)

- Q1 FY27 margin decline "owing to commodity cost inflation, lag in pass-through of raw material cost increases and the inclusion of relatively lower-margin businesses acquired under Project Rise."
- FY26 margin moderation "due to the inclusion of the relatively lower-margin MMAS business, elevated commodity costs and supply chain disruptions in the fourth quarter."

## Management Commentary — Demand

- Q1 FY27 growth "supported by robust performance across the two-wheeler segment, aftermarket business, new OEM programme launches and contributions from the acquired businesses."
