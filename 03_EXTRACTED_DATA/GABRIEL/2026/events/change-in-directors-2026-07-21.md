## Governance Change Summary

Board of Directors meeting held Tuesday, July 21, 2026 (4:00 PM–5:00 PM IST), on recommendation of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, approved (subject to member approval at the ensuing AGM):

a. "Appointment of Mr. Mahendra K. Goyal (DIN: 02605616) as Executive Director of the Company and as Group Chief Executive Officer and Managing Director ('Group CEO & MD') for a term of five consecutive years with effect from July 21, 2026 (immediately on approval of the Board) till July 20, 2031." He was previously a non-executive, non-independent director since October 22, 2024, now re-designated as executive director; becomes a Key Managerial Personnel "responsible for the overall supervision of the Company across all its business segments including Joint Ventures, Subsidiaries and Associates companies."

b. "Re-designation and variation in terms of appointment of Mr. Atul Jaggi (DIN: 07263848) as Managing Director (Ride Control) with effect from July 21, 2026 (immediately on approval of the Board) until October 17, 2029, being the tenure already approved by the members of the Company on January 15, 2025." Prior title was Managing Director (unqualified); "Except for the change in designation, all other terms and conditions of appointment ... shall remain unchanged."

### Mr. Mahendra K. Goyal — profile (per Annexure A2)
- Chartered Accountant, Company Secretary, Cost Accountant; Oxford Advanced Management Programme / Strategic Leadership certificates
- With ANAND Group since July 1995; currently Group CEO (since October 2024)
- "He has played a pivotal role in the Group's ongoing transformation initiatives, including Project RISE ... subsequent investments, Project Jupiter (a group restructuring by way of acquisition of shares of HL Mando ANAND India Pvt. Ltd.) and the investment in HL Klemove India Private Limited."
- Directly oversees Gabriel India, Dana Anand India, HL Mando Anand India, MAHLE ANAND Filter Systems, MAHLE ANAND Thermal Systems, and Valeo Friction Materials as Chairman or Board Member
- "Not related to any Director or Key Managerial Personnel of the Company"; "not debarred from holding the office of director by virtue of any order of SEBI or any other such Authority"

### Mr. Atul Jaggi — profile (per Annexure B2)
- Age 50; mechanical engineering degree (Thapar Institute), PGDBA (IMT Ghaziabad), master's in quality management (BITS Pilani); Six Sigma Black Belt; MIT Sloan and Oxford leadership program alumnus
- 25 years' experience; former Deputy Managing Director of Gabriel India; formerly Director on boards of Haldex India, Faurecia Emissions Control Technologies India, and Mahle Anand Filters
- "Under his leadership, Gabriel India's TWBU and CVBU divisions have grown significantly over the last few years, clocking year on year growth significantly ahead of the market."
- "Not related to any Director or Key Managerial Personnel of the Company"
