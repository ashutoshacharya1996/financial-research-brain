## Guidance

- "Nevertheless, leverage is expected to remain comfortable, with adjusted gearing below 0.30 time."
- "The broader business mix, increasing content-per-vehicle opportunities, scale benefits and growing contribution from technology-intensive businesses are expected to support operating margin of 9–10%, healthy cash generation and sustained return metrics over the medium term."
- Rating sensitivity (upward): "Substantial increase in revenue and significant product diversification while maintaining operating profitability at 12–14%"
- Rating sensitivity (downward): "Large, debt-funded capex or acquisitions leading to debt (including lease liability)/Ebitda of over 2.5 times on a sustained basis"

## Management Commentary — Demand

Not management commentary. Rating-agency statements, verbatim:
- "Gabriel has emerged as the principal automotive platform of the ANAND group following the ongoing consolidation of the group's automotive businesses and strategic partnership interests under Project Rise and Project Jupiter."
- "Around 70% of the identified businesses and partnership interests have already been or about to be consolidated under Gabriel since the commencement of the restructuring programme in June 2025"

## Management Commentary — Margins

Rating-agency statements (see Financial Metrics for figures): "operating margin moderated to 8.7% from 9.6% during the same period, owing to commodity cost inflation, lag in pass-through of raw material cost increases and the inclusion of relatively lower-margin businesses acquired under Project Rise."

## Management Commentary — Capacity / Expansion

- "Gabriel is expected to raise Rs 1,000–1,200 crore of debt primarily towards acquisition-related funding requirements."
- Project Jupiter expected completion: "the transaction is expected to be completed by September-October 2026."
- "Crisil Ratings has also noted the acquisition of a 30% less one share stake by Gabriel in HL Klemove India Pvt Ltd (HLKI) for $98.4 million"

## Named Catalysts

- Project Jupiter completion: "expected to be completed by September-October 2026."
- Promoter shareholding: "Promoter shareholding in Gabriel will rise to ~66.3% upon completion of Project Jupiter"
- "The company's ability to successfully integrate acquired businesses and derive anticipated synergies from the ongoing consolidation initiatives will remain monitorable." (rating agency; listed here as monitorable item)
- Group intent: "the group intends to progressively consolidate the balance businesses under Gabriel over the medium to long term"

## Named Risks

- "Susceptibility to pricing pressure from OEMs and peers" — "profitability remains susceptible to fluctuations in commodity prices, adverse product mix changes and delays in passing on cost increases to customers."
- "Susceptible to inherent cyclicality in the auto industry" — "Gabriel's performance remains linked to the automotive sector, which is inherently cyclical"
- Customer concentration: "customer concentration and pricing pressures from OEMs." (listed in Rating rationale)
- Debt-funded acquisitions: "Large, debt-funded capex or acquisitions leading to debt (including lease liability)/Ebitda of over 2.5 times on a sustained basis" (downward sensitivity)
