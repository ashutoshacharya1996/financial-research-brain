## Facts (verbatim)
- "Faurecia Automotive Seating India Private Limited ('FASI') and Gabriel India Limited ... propose to enter into a Joint Venture Agreement to undertake the business of developing, manufacturing, assembling, marketing, selling and distributing primarily passenger vehicle seats, either completely assembled seats or their components/sub-assemblies/systems (excluding mechanisms)".
- Shareholding "50% plus 1 equity share [FASI] and 50% less 1 equity share [Gabriel]". Initial investment "INR 20,00,00,000", Gabriel "INR 9,99,99,990"; at Closing additional "INR 80,00,00,000" (Gabriel Rs 40 cr). Post closing paid-up capital "INR 100,00,00,000"; Gabriel total "INR 49,99,99,990".
- Agreements: TIPLA with Forvia/Faurecia India; Corporate Service Agreement with "Anand Automotive Private Limited" (promoter group); "Business Transfer Agreement ('BTA') between FASI and JV Co. to acquire the existing frames business of FASI as a going concern on a slump sale basis, excluding mechanism."
- Objective: "diversifying its business into a powertrain agnostic product segment within the automotive components industry".
- Timeline: "By 31st December 2026 or any other date as may be agreed". Governance: "6 Directors (3 Directors from each Party)"; "Chairman (with casting vote): To be nominated from amongst the FASI Directors"; "COO to be appointed by FASI".
- Related party: initial subscription "does not fall within the purview of Related Party Transaction"; JV Co. "will become an Associate of Gabriel India".
- Board meeting "started at 08:30 pm and concluded at 09:05 pm"; filing signed by CFO Mohit Srivastava.
