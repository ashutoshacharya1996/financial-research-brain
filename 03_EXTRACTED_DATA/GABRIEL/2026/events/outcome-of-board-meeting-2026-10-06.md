## Management Commentary — Corporate Action

Board meeting held October 06, 2026 (08:30 pm to 09:05 pm). Verbatim:
- "Faurecia Automotive Seating India Private Limited ... ('FASI') and Gabriel India Limited ... propose to enter into a Joint Venture Agreement to undertake the business of developing, manufacturing, assembling, marketing, selling and distributing primarily passenger vehicle seats, either completely assembled seats or their components/sub-assemblies/systems (excluding mechanisms), in the identified territory (i.e., India and such other Countries as may be mutually agreed)."
- "Execution of the Joint Venture Agreement ('JVA') between FASI and Gabriel India to enable formation of a Joint Venture Company ('JV Co.') wherein FASI and Gabriel India will have shareholding in the ratio of 50% plus 1 equity share and 50% less 1 equity share respectively."
- "Gabriel India intends to subscribe 50% less 1 equity share of the JV Co., with the objective of diversifying its business into a powertrain agnostic product segment within the automotive components industry."
- "Business Transfer Agreement ('BTA') between FASI and JV Co. to acquire the existing frames business of FASI as a going concern on a slump sale basis, excluding mechanism."
- Related party: "Post incorporation, the JV Co. will become an Associate of Gabriel India and hence its Related Party."
- JV terms: "Board composition: 6 Directors (3 Directors from each Party)"; "Chairman (with casting vote): To be nominated from amongst the FASI Directors"; "COO to be appointed by FASI"; "Dy. COO to be appointed by Gabriel India"; "Standard clauses like deadlock, material event of default form part of the JVA."
- Nature of consideration (Annexure I item 7): "Cash consideration"
