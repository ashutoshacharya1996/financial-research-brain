### Transaction Economics (HL Mando Anand — "Project Jupiter")
- Stake acquired: 28.9% (28.99% per legal filing) from AIPL (promoter/holding company) for ~Rs 2,231 Cr
- Consideration split: ~Rs 1,881 Cr via share swap (preferential allotment to AIPL) + ~Rs 350 Cr cash
- Equity valuation of HL Mando Anand: ~Rs 7,695 Cr; Implied EV/Adj. EBITDA (FY26): ~11.3x
- Swap ratio: 355 Gabriel shares for every 1,000 HL Mando Anand shares; issue price Rs 1,305.89/share (higher of 10-day/90-day VWAP)
- Independent valuers: KPMG and BDO
- Expected EPS accretion: ~13% (incremental to ~38% EPS accretion already delivered from Project Rise), on proforma FY26 basis
- Shareholding impact: Promoters move from 63.55% to 66.29% (+2.74%); Public from 36.45% to 33.71% (-2.74%)

### Transaction Economics (HL Klemove India — JV, referred to as "Project Lavender" in the separate Acquisition filing)
- Stake acquired: 30% minus 1 equity share, from HL Klemove Corporation (Korea), for ~Rs 935 Cr (USD 98.44 Mn @ Rs 95/USD)
- Equity valuation: USD 328.13 Mn (~Rs 3,117 Cr)
- Payment: Tranche 1 (75%, ~Rs 701 Cr) and Tranche 2 (25%, ~Rs 234 Cr); Tranche 1 long-stop date Sept 15, 2026, Tranche 2 within 18 months of signing
- Funding: "combination of internal accruals and debt"
- Resultant shareholding: HL Klemove Corp ~71.9%, Gabriel India ~29.9%; Gabriel gets 4 of 10 board seats (affirmative rights)
