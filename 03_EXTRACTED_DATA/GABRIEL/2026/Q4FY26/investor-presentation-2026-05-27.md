## Guidance

- "Aspiration to hit Rs. 10 Bn in revenues by 2030" (IGSSPL sunroof JV specific revenue aspiration).
- "Manufacturing of solar dampers and e-bike forks expected from FY27" (export/product roadmap).
- Sustainability targets: "TO BE CARBON & WATER NEUTRAL BY 2030 WITH ZERO WASTE TO LANDFILL"; water-neutral operations by 2030 (50% achieved FY26); 50% of energy needs from renewable sources by 2030 (18% achieved FY26); carbon neutral operations by 2030 (40% achieved FY26).
- Vision: "TO BE AMONGST THE TOP 5 SHOCK ABSORBER MANUFACTURERS IN THE WORLD."

## Management Commentary — Demand

- 2W/3W segment: "Sales: 14.2% YoY growth* ... Key drivers: Strong demand from key customers." (FY26 GIL performance vs 2W industry 11.8% YoY growth)
- Passenger Vehicles: "Sales: 16.7% YoY growth* ... Key drivers: Mainly strong traction in Utility Vehicles" (vs PV industry 9.4% YoY growth)
- Commercial Vehicles: "Sales: 34.8% YoY growth* ... Key drivers: Rising demand of cabin dampers" (vs CV industry 13.1% YoY growth)
- E-bike/future mobility: "Samples delivered to European customers, followed by continued demand through additional sample orders."
- Solar dampers: "Orders won: 3 Customers catering to both domestic and export geographies. Sample submitted to export customers and feedback awaited."
- New Programs and SOP (Q4FY26): "SOP-Suzuki Access ABS, Greaves Magnum Grand; LOI from River Mobility" (2W/3W); "Constant Supplies to Key Customers" (PV — MSIL, M&M, Skoda Volkswagen); "SOP commenced for multiple programs of AL" (CV).

## Management Commentary — Margins

- "Note: EBITDA has been computed after eliminating the non-operational expenses and one-time impact of wage code. PBT excludes one-time wage code impact." (methodology note accompanying all margin disclosures)
- Standalone EBITDA margin trend: "9.3% 9.1% 9.0% 9.1% ... 8.9% 9.0%" (Q4FY25 to Q4FY26 / FY25 to FY26) — broadly stable/modest expansion.
- Standalone PAT margin: "3.9% 4.6% 4.6% 4.1% 4.5% 5.3% 5.4% 5.3% 6.1% 5.9% 5.7% 5.8% 5.8% 5.7% 5.7% 6.1% 5.5%" (Q4FY22 through Q4FY26 quarterly series).
- Fixed Asset Turnover declined: "4.4 5.7 6.6 7.2 7.1 6.6" (FY21-FY26), reflecting capex build-out (Chakan 2 plant, Hosur-2 land).

## Management Commentary — Capacity / Expansion

- "Capex for FY26 to the tune of Rs.1,893 Mn compared to Rs.1,281 Mn in FY25. Major capex spent on growth initiatives specifically on Chakan 2 plant, Hosur-2 land for expansion and other capex across GIL plants."
- IGSSPL (Inalfa Gabriel Sunroof Systems): "Setup a plant in Chennai with an annual production capacity of 200,000 sunroofs for anchor customers Hyundai and Kia. Second line of another 2,00,000 units became operational from FY26 at same location."
- MMAS asset purchase: "Acquired additional capacity of 3.2 Mn shock absorbers and 1 Mn gas springs ... GIL completed transaction on April 1, 2025 and accounted in its Standalone business."
- "Gabriel Pune Tech Centre" — IGBC Platinum-certified green building, recently launched R&D technology center at Chakan (in addition to Hosur), recognized by DSIR.
- R&D scale: "83 R&D Specialists, 100 Patents Filed" (34 granted to date).

## Named Catalysts

- Composite Scheme of Arrangement — NCLT sanctioned May 11, 2026, operative from May 22, 2026: demerger of Asia Investments Private Limited's Automotive Undertaking (Drive Train products/EV transmissions, Body-in-White/NVH products, synchroniser rings, aluminium forgings, brake fluids, radiator coolants, diesel exhaust fluids/Ad-Blue, PU/PVC adhesives) into Gabriel, bringing subsidiaries/JVs ACYM (76%), Dana Anand (25.1%), Henkel Anand (49%) under Gabriel.
- JV with Jinhap Korea (fasteners) — "Jinhap Gabriel Auto India Private Limited a subsidiary of GIL, effective February 27, 2026." Rationale: "Entry into fasteners to address anchor customer's localization needs."
- JV with SK Enmove (lubricants/functional fluids) — "GIL is holding 49% in SK Enmove Gabriel India Private Limited, making it a JV effective February 27, 2026." JVA signed at SK HQ, Korea, October 15, 2025.
- Revised IGSSPL JV with Inalfa — Board approved November 12, 2025; execution pending Ministry of Heavy Industries approval; will dilute Gabriel's IGSSPL stake from 100% to 65%.
- Awards: "Fastest Development Award from Suzuki Motorcycles at the Annual Supplier Conference 2026" (Q4FY26).
- Export growth drivers named: "Consistent supply to DAF (Netherlands)," "RFQs from major global CV OEMs," "Exports to Yamaha, Japan."
- E2W (electric two/three-wheeler) leadership: "GIL E2W market share at 60% in FY26"; "GIL E2W-E3W contribution to 2W/3W segment revenues in FY26" at 8%.
- Utility Vehicle strength: "65% Share of utility vehicle sales in GIL PV business"; "29% GIL share of business in total utility vehicle sales."
- Railways: long-standing OEM relationships across ICF, RCF, MCF, PLW, BLW, CLW, all zonal railways, and private OEMs (Medha, RKFL, Pushkar, Siemens); shock absorber content growing per coach/loco generation (2 variants/4 nos per Conventional Coach in 1978 up to 4 variants/20 nos per Vande Bharat Coach in 2022 and Siemens HP 9000 Loco in 2024).
- CORE 90 cost reduction program — structured 90-day cost/working-capital reduction drive spanning RMC, productivity, FOH, VOH and working capital project teams.
