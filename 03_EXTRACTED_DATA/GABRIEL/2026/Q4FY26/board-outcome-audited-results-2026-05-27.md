## Named Catalysts

- Final Dividend for FY 2025-26 of Rs. 3.10 per equity share recommended by the Board, subject to shareholder approval at the AGM, payable on or before September 24, 2026.
- Composite Scheme of Arrangement: NCLT Mumbai Bench sanctioned the Scheme via Order dated May 11, 2026 (merger of Anchemco India Private Limited with Asia Investments Private Limited; demerger and transfer of Automotive Undertaking of Asia Investments Private Limited into Gabriel effective April 1, 2026). "The Hon'ble National Company Law Tribunal, Mumbai Bench (NCLT) has sanctioned the Scheme vide its Order dated May 11, 2026."
- JV with SK Enmove Co., Ltd (South Korea) — JVA executed October 15, 2025, for engine oils, e-fluids (EV fluids), shock absorber oil, industrial lubricants, greases, e-thermal fluids; Gabriel/SKEN shareholding ratio 49:51. "the Company formally executed in October 15, 2025, to enable formation of a Joint Venture Company wherein SKEN and the Company will have shareholding in the ratio of 51:49 respectively."
- JV/subsidiary with Jinos Co., Ltd (South Korea) — subscription of equity in Jinhap Automotive India Private Limited (now Jinhap Gabriel Auto India Private Limited, "JGAIPL") for fasteners business (automotive & industrial applications).
- Investment of ₹1,38,24,425 by Gabriel in JGAIPL rights issue (11,05,954 equity shares at Rs 10 face value + Rs 2.50 premium); shareholding remains unchanged at 51%.
- Revised JV with Inalfa Roof Systems Group B.V. for Inalfa Gabriel Sunroof Systems Private Limited (IGSSPL) — Board approval November 12, 2025; pending Ministry of Heavy Industries approval; upon completion Inalfa to hold 35%, Gabriel to hold 65% (down from 100%).
- Asset Purchase Agreement with Marelli Motherson Auto Suspension Parts Private Limited (MMAS) — acquisition of passive shock absorber/strut/gas damper manufacturing assets completed April 1, 2025, for consideration of Rs. 521.39 million; resulted in capital reserve recognition of Rs. 43.20 million.
