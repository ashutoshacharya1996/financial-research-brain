## Management Commentary — Capacity / Expansion

- "Larsen & Toubro Vyoma is a next-generation digital infrastructure services provider promoted by L&T. Focussed on building AI-ready, hyperscale data centres, it offers a comprehensive suite of solutions, including colocation, GPU cloud and cloud-native platforms.
