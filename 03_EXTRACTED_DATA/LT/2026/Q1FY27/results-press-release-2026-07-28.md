## Management Commentary — Demand

- "The financial year has commenced against the backdrop of geopolitical uncertainties. The Company has managed to maintain momentum by rotating its focus across sectors and geographies while maintaining robust cash flows." (CMD S N Subrahmanyan)
- "significant order wins were achieved across multiple businesses such as Residential & Commercial buildings, Transportation Infrastructure, Ferrous Metals, Offshore Wind and the Heavy Engineering businesses."
- Energy-Conventional inflow: "The decline reflects the deferment of certain anticipated orders and the high base effect arising from an ultra-mega order secured in the CarbonLite Solutions business in the corresponding period of the previous year."
- Outlook: "Domestic demand continues to remain healthy, with sustained expansion across both manufacturing and services activity."

## Management Commentary — Margins

- I&U: "The contraction in margin is primarily on account of change in revenue mix and an increase in credit provisions on account of expected delays in collection of receivables."
- Manufacturing & Products: "The margin is reflective of change in the sales mix within the portfolio."
- TPS: "The marginal decline is reflective of higher manpower cost and forex variation adversely impacting hedged portfolio positions."
- Realty: "The decline in margin is primarily due to the change in sales composition."

## Management Commentary — Capacity / Expansion

- Portfolio exit: "During the quarter, we successfully concluded the sale of Nabha Power Limited, consistent with our stated strategy of exiting the concessions portfolio."
- "we have signed the share purchase agreement with Hyderabad Metro Rail Limited ("HMRL"), a Government of Telangana Enterprise, to divest 100% of our stake in Hyderabad Metro SPV."
- Capacity expansion / capex amounts: not stated.

## Named Risks

- Revenue: "Subdued revenue growth primarily attributable to execution stage of the order book and supply chain constraints in the Middle East" (deck, see investor-presentation-2026-07-28.md).
- Energy-Green revenue: "largely due to supply chain disruptions arising from the West Asia conflict in the Solar business."
- I&U revenue: "The subdued performance is largely attributed to execution challenges in the Water & Effluent Treatment business."
- Outlook: "However, the adverse implications of the extended disruption in supply chains and elevated energy prices could interrupt the growth momentum."
- "In energy markets, the resurgence of geopolitical tensions in West Asia and continued uncertainty around global trade policies remains key risks."
