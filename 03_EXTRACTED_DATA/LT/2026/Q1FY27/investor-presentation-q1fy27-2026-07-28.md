## Management Commentary — Demand

- "Order Inflow growth aided by ultra-mega offshore wind orders from Europe and sustained domestic private sector ordering" (Slide 8).

- Energy – Conventional: "Order inflow declined due to deferred order awards and the high base effect (CarbonLite Solution orders)" (Slide 17).
