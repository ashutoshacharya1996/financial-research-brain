## Guidance

No numeric guidance. Outlook (verbatim): "Against this ever changing economic and geopolitical landscape, the Company remains committed to delivering sustainable shareholder value by leveraging sector specific technology, investing in AI, and adopt digital solutions to enhance productivity and competitiveness. The Company is also focused on ensuring efficient and profitable execution of its robust order book, maintaining financial discipline, and allocating capital judiciously across targeted growth sectors."
CMD: "With a well-diversified portfolio spanning sectors and geographies, we remain confident of maintaining growth while capitalising on emerging opportunities."

## Management Commentary — Demand

- "The financial year has commenced against the backdrop of geopolitical uncertainties. The Company has managed to maintain momentum by rotating its focus across sectors and geographies while maintaining robust cash flows. The performance for the quarter reflects our portfolio resilience." (S N Subrahmanyan)
- "Domestic demand continues to remain healthy, with sustained expansion across both manufacturing and services activity."
- "significant order wins were achieved across multiple businesses such as Residential & Commercial buildings, Transportation Infrastructure, Ferrous Metals, Offshore Wind and the Heavy Engineering businesses."

## Management Commentary — Margins

- "However, the adverse implications of the extended disruption in supply chains and elevated energy prices could interrupt the growth momentum."
- "The contraction in margin is primarily on account of change in revenue mix and an increase in credit provisions on account of expected delays in collection of receivables." (Infrastructure & Utilities)

## Management Commentary — Capacity / Expansion

- "Effective from April 01, 2026, pursuant to the Company's Lakshya 2031 strategic plan, the Group has realigned its portfolio to enhance strategic focus, capital allocation efficiency and operational agility." (new reporting segments: Infrastructure & Utilities; Energy - Conventional; Energy - Green; Manufacturing and Products; Technology, Platforms & Services; Realty)
- "During the quarter, we successfully concluded the sale of Nabha Power Limited, consistent with our stated strategy of exiting the concessions portfolio."
- "we have signed the share purchase agreement with Hyderabad Metro Rail Limited (“HMRL”), a Government of Telangana Enterprise, to divest 100% of our stake in Hyderabad Metro SPV."
- Board approved Scheme of Amalgamation of L&T Power Development Limited (wholly owned subsidiary) into L&T; "No new shares or other equity interests shall be issued or no payment would be made in cash or kind"; networth/revenue FY26 standalone: L&T ₹ 74,532.90 cr / ₹ 1,53,680.17 cr; LTPDL ₹ 2,703.12 cr / Nil.
