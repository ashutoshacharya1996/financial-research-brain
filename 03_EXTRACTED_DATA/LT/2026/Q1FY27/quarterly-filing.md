## Management Commentary — Demand

- "significant order wins were achieved across multiple businesses such as Residential & Commercial buildings, Transportation Infrastructure, Ferrous Metals, Offshore Wind and the Heavy Engineering businesses."
- "The Infrastructure & Utilities segment secured order inflow of ₹ 44,357 crore, during the quarter ended June 30, 2026, registering a more than 100% growth on y-o-y basis"
- "The Energy – Green segment secured orders valued at ₹ 33,042 crore during the quarter ended June 30, 2026, registering a growth of 58% on y-o-y basis. The growth is on account of receipt of ultra-mega orders in the Offshore Wind business."
- "The decline reflects the deferment of certain anticipated orders and the high base effect" (Energy – Conventional order inflow down 90% YoY)

## Management Commentary — Margins

- "The contraction in margin is primarily on account of change in revenue mix and an increase in credit provisions on account of expected delays in collection of receivables." (Infrastructure & Utilities)
- "The margin is reflective of change in the sales mix within the portfolio." (Manufacturing & Products)
- "The marginal decline is reflective of higher manpower cost and forex variation adversely impacting hedged portfolio positions." (Technology, Platforms & Services)

## Management Commentary — Capacity / Expansion

- "Effective from April 01, 2026, pursuant to the Company’s Lakshya 2031 strategic plan, the Group has realigned its portfolio to enhance strategic focus, capital allocation efficiency and operational agility."
- "we successfully concluded the sale of Nabha Power Limited, consistent with our stated strategy of exiting the concessions portfolio."
- "we have signed the share purchase agreement with Hyderabad Metro Rail Limited (“HMRL”) ... to divest 100% of our stake in Hyderabad Metro SPV."
