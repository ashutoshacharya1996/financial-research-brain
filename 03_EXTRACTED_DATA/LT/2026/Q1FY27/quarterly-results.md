## Management Commentary — Demand

- "The financial year has commenced against the backdrop of geopolitical uncertainties. The Company has managed to maintain momentum by rotating its focus across sectors and geographies while maintaining robust cash flows. The performance for the quarter reflects our portfolio resilience." — S N Subrahmanyan, CMD
- "During the quarter, significant order wins were achieved across multiple businesses such as Residential & Commercial buildings, Transportation Infrastructure, Ferrous Metals, Offshore Wind and the Heavy Engineering businesses."
- Energy-Conventional: "The decline reflects the deferment of certain anticipated orders and the high base effect arising from an ultra-mega order secured in the CarbonLite Solutions business in the corresponding period of the previous year."
- Energy-Green: "The growth is on account of receipt of ultra-mega orders in the Offshore Wind business."
- "Domestic demand continues to remain healthy, with sustained expansion across both manufacturing and services activity."

## Management Commentary — Margins

- I&U: "The contraction in margin is primarily on account of change in revenue mix and an increase in credit provisions on account of expected delays in collection of receivables."
- I&U revenue: "The subdued performance is largely attributed to execution challenges in the Water & Effluent Treatment business."
- M&P: "The margin is reflective of change in the sales mix within the portfolio."
- TPS: "The marginal decline is reflective of higher manpower cost and forex variation adversely impacting hedged portfolio positions."
- Realty: "The decline in margin is primarily due to the change in sales composition."

## Management Commentary — Capacity / Expansion

- Segment realignment effective April 01, 2026: "pursuant to the Company's Lakshya 2031 strategic plan, the Group has realigned its portfolio to enhance strategic focus, capital allocation efficiency and operational agility." New Energy – Green segment carved out (Renewables + Offshore Wind); Realty now a separate reportable segment.
- Board approved "a Scheme of Amalgamation (the "Scheme") amongst the Company and L&T Power Development Limited, a wholly owned subsidiary of the Company" — subject to NCLT sanction.

## Named Catalysts

- "we have signed the share purchase agreement with Hyderabad Metro Rail Limited ("HMRL"), a Government of Telangana Enterprise, to divest 100% of our stake in Hyderabad Metro SPV." Results note (iii): "The conditions precedent to the closure are expected to be completed by September 30, 2026."
- Nabha Power divestment concluded June 25, 2026: "consistent with our stated strategy of exiting the concessions portfolio."
- Merger of L&T Power Development Ltd into L&T (NCLT approval pending).
