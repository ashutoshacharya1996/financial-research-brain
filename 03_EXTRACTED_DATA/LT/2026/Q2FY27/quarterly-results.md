## Guidance

No numeric FY27 guidance stated in this filing. CMD (verbatim): "We are well positioned to sustain this growth momentum and will continue to invest in innovation, digital capabilities, and technology-led businesses to drive growth and create long-term value for our stakeholders."

"The highest ever order book at ₹8.5 lakh crore provides multi-year revenue visibility."

## Management Commentary — Demand

- "The Company has delivered a strong quarter in an everchanging geopolitical landscape. This performance reflects continued customer confidence and our ability to secure large and complex orders across sectors and geographies. We achieved yet another record quarterly order inflow."
- "During the quarter, the Company secured several high-value orders led by Hydrocarbon – Offshore & Onshore, CarbonLite Solutions, Public Spaces & Airports, and Power Transmission & Distribution businesses."
- I&U order inflow -13%: "impacted by the deferment of select targeted orders."
- Energy-Green order inflow -45%: "The decline is on account of deferrals in the Solar business."
- Realty inflow -12%: "majorly due to lower pre-sales as some new launches are delayed."
- Outlook: "Economic activity was supported by buoyant domestic demand, sustained capital expenditure and continuing robustness in the services sector."
- "The global economic outlook is one of guarded optimism, aided by AI Data Center demand in several major economies and fresh investments in infrastructure, energy transition, technology, and industrial capacity."

## Management Commentary — Margins

- I&U: "The contraction in margin is primarily on account of cost pressures and change in sales mix."
- Energy-Conventional: "The decline in margin is primarily attributable to a higher proportion of orders in early stages of execution within the CarbonLite Solutions business."
- M&P: "The margin improvement is reflective of a more favourable execution profile of projects within the portfolio."
- TPS: "The slight moderation is reflective of higher manpower cost and adverse forex variation."
- Revenue: "driven by accelerated progress in the various business in the Projects, Products & Manufacturing (PP&M) portfolio."
- "Focused execution across our Projects, Products & Manufacturing businesses supported a higher revenue growth."

## Management Commentary — Capacity / Expansion

- "We are well positioned to sustain this growth momentum and will continue to invest in innovation, digital capabilities, and technology-led businesses"
- Energy-Green revenue +43%: "largely due to ramp up of execution in Solar jobs and commencement of execution in Offshore Wind projects."

## Named Catalysts
