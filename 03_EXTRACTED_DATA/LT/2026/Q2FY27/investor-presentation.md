## Management Commentary — Margins

- "Primarily impacted due to lower margin across portfolio and change in revenue mix (higher share of PP&M)"
- I&U: "Margin impacted by continuing cost pressures in WET projects and higher proportion of jobs below margin recognition threshold"
- Energy-Conventional: "Margin reflective of jobs in early stages in CarbonLite Solutions; PY margin included variation claims in CarbonLite Solutions and MRU business"
