## Guidance

Not stated as numeric guidance. Outlook paragraph (verbatim, selected):
- "Notwithstanding, India is expected to remain among the fastest-growing major economies, with the RBI forecasting real GDP growth of 7.1% for FY 2026-27, supported by healthy domestic demand, private investment activity, and infrastructure spending."
- "The Company will continue to leverage its capability and flexibility to successfully navigate the environment."
- "The Company remains focused on operational excellence and the adoption of technology to improve productivity and competitiveness and ensure long-term sustainable growth for its stakeholders."

## Management Commentary — Demand

- CMD (S N Subrahmanyan): "The Company has delivered a strong quarter in an everchanging geopolitical landscape. This performance reflects continued customer confidence and our ability to secure large and complex orders across sectors and geographies. We achieved yet another record quarterly order inflow."
- CMD: "The highest ever order book at ₹8.5 lakh crore provides multi-year revenue visibility."
- CMD: "We are well positioned to sustain this growth momentum and will continue to invest in innovation, digital capabilities, and technology-led businesses to drive growth and create long-term value for our stakeholders."
- Q2 order inflow drivers: "...several high-value orders led by Hydrocarbon – Offshore & Onshore, CarbonLite Solutions, Public Spaces & Airports, and Power Transmission & Distribution businesses."
- H1 order inflow drivers: "...strong order inflow momentum in Hydrocarbon - Offshore, Offshore Wind, Minerals & Metals, and Residential & Commercial Buildings businesses."
- Outlook: "The global economic outlook is one of guarded optimism, aided by AI Data Center demand in several major economies and fresh investments in infrastructure, energy transition, technology, and industrial capacity."
- Realty: "...majorly due to lower pre-sales as some new launches are delayed."

## Management Commentary — Margins

- Infrastructure & Utilities: "The contraction in margin is primarily on account of cost pressures and change in sales mix."
- Energy – Conventional: "The decline in margin is primarily attributable to a higher proportion of orders in early stages of execution within the CarbonLite Solutions business."
- Manufacturing & Products: "The margin improvement is reflective of a more favourable execution profile of projects within the portfolio."
- Technology, Platforms & Services: "The slight moderation is reflective of higher manpower cost and adverse forex variation."
- Realty: "The decline in margin is primarily attributable to fewer residential unit handovers during the quarter."

## Management Commentary — Capacity / Expansion

- Development Projects: "...the Green Energy business is in investment phase."
- Portfolio: Renewables and Offshore Wind in "Energy – Green" (per Q1 FY27 realignment, Lakshya 2031 plan); no new capacity figure stated in this filing.

## Named Catalysts

- Order book at "₹8.5 lakh crore" (CMD) "provides multi-year revenue visibility."
- Growth drivers listed by management: Hydrocarbon Offshore/Onshore, CarbonLite Solutions, Public Spaces & Airports, Power T&D (Q2 orders); Offshore Wind, Minerals & Metals, Residential & Commercial Buildings (H1 orders).
- Realty pipeline: "...due to lower pre-sales as some new launches are delayed." (negative for near-term Realty inflows; no date given).

## Named Risks

- Macro: "However, inflation remains a concern, arising from food price volatility, elevated energy costs, and weather-related disruptions."
- "Geopolitical imbalances, trade uncertainties, and climate-related risks continue to affect commodity prices and supply chains."
- "The Middle East continue to pose varied downside risks to global growth and investment activity."
- RBI action referenced in filing: "the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) raised the repo rate by 25 basis points to 5.50% and shifted its policy stance from ‘neutral’ to ‘calibrated tightening’."
- Operating: Infrastructure & Utilities margin "cost pressures and change in sales mix"; Energy – Conventional margin reflects "orders in early stages of execution within the CarbonLite Solutions business".
