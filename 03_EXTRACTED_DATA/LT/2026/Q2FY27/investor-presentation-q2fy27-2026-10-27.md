## Management Commentary — Demand

- "Highest ever quarterly order inflow led by receipt of Ultra Mega and Mega orders in the Energy-Conventional segment" (Slide 7).
- "International orders constitute 54% of the Order Book" (Slide 7).
- Energy – Conventional: "Receipt of Ultra Mega and Mega orders in Hydrocarbon and CarbonLite Solutions buoys order book" (Slide 17).
- Energy – Green: "Decline in order inflows mainly due to deferrals in the Solar business" (Slide 18).
- Manufacturing & Products: "Strong growth in order inflows aided by receipt of export orders in Precision Engineering & Systems business" (Slide 19).
- Infrastructure & Utilities: "Lower order inflows post a robust Q1" (Slide 16).
- Realty: "Decline in order inflows mainly due to lower pre-sales on account of launch deferrals" (Slide 22).

## Management Commentary — Margins

- Group: "Primarily impacted due to lower margin across portfolio and change in revenue mix (higher share of PP&M)" (Slide 8).
- Infrastructure & Utilities: "Margin impacted by continuing cost pressures in WET projects and higher proportion of jobs below margin recognition threshold" (Slide 16).
- Energy – Conventional: "Margin reflective of jobs in early stages in CarbonLite Solutions; PY margin included variation claims in CarbonLite Solutions and MRU business" (Slide 17).
- Manufacturing & Products: "Margin improvement primarily reflective of execution cost savings and improved job mix" (Slide 19).
- Technology, Platforms & Services: "Segment margin moderated due to pricing pressure in traditional services and forex variation in LTM, partly offset by one-time gain on SWC" (Slide 20).
- Group PAT drivers: "Supported by higher group surplus funds & improved yields and one-time gain on divestment of SWC business in LTTS" (Slide 9).

## Management Commentary — Capacity / Expansion

- Energy – Green: "Strong growth in revenue with commencement of large value projects in Solar and Offshore Wind business" (Slide 18).
- Hyderabad Metro: "Reduction primarily due to non-consolidation of Hyderabad Metro and decrease in Standalone debt" (Slide 9).
- Development projects: Green Assets / Hydrogen / Ammonia listed as "New business" in portfolio composition (Slide 11).
