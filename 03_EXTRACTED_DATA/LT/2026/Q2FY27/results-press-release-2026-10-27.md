## Management Commentary — Demand

- "The Company has delivered a strong quarter in an everchanging geopolitical landscape. This performance reflects continued customer confidence and our ability to secure large and complex orders across sectors and geographies. We achieved yet another record quarterly order inflow." (CMD)
- "The highest ever order book at ₹8.5 lakh crore provides multi-year revenue visibility."
- "During the quarter, the Company secured several high-value orders led by Hydrocarbon – Offshore & Onshore, CarbonLite Solutions, Public Spaces & Airports, and Power Transmission & Distribution businesses."
- I&U inflow: "registering a decline of 13% on y-o-y basis, impacted by the deferment of select targeted orders."
- Energy-Green inflow: "The decline is on account of deferrals in the Solar business."
- "The Indian economy maintained a good growth momentum during the first half of FY 2026-27. Economic activity was supported by buoyant domestic demand, sustained capital expenditure and continuing robustness in the services sector."

## Management Commentary — Margins

- I&U (5.7% vs 6.2%): "The contraction in margin is primarily on account of cost pressures and change in sales mix."
- Energy-Conventional (7.4% vs 8.2%): "The decline in margin is primarily attributable to a higher proportion of orders in early stages of execution within the CarbonLite Solutions business."
- Manufacturing & Products (15.7% vs 13.5%): "The margin improvement is reflective of a more favourable execution profile of projects within the portfolio."
- TPS (19.5% vs 20.0%): "The slight moderation is reflective of higher manpower cost and adverse forex variation."
- Realty (54.3% vs 67.6%): "primarily attributable to fewer residential unit handovers during the quarter."

## Management Commentary — Capacity / Expansion

- Development Projects: "(a) Nabha Power Limited up to the date of its divestment on June 25, 2026, (b) Hyderabad Metro till April 30, 2026"; "Green Energy business is in investment phase."
