- M&P: "Margin improvement primarily reflective of execution cost savings and improved job mix"
- TPS: "Segment margin moderated due to pricing pressure in traditional services and forex variation in LTM, partly offset by one-time gain on SWC divestment in LTTS"

- PAT: "Growth mainly due to strong pickup in activity levels in PP&M and improved treasury operations"
- SG&A: "Increase in SG&A cost mainly due to adverse forex variation in LTM & LTTS and higher credit cost in FS"
