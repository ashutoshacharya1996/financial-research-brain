## Management Commentary — Demand

"we are pleased to inform you that Paras Defence and Space Technologies Limited (“PARAS”) has received an order from the Defence Research and Development Organisation (DRDO), Ministry of Defence, valued at approximately ₹26.59 crore (including taxes), wherein the Company will be the Lead System Integrator and will also provide the complete design, analysis, fabrication, manufacturing and assembly of the Optics and Opto-Mechanical Unit for a Hyperspectral Mission.
