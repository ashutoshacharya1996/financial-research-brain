## Named Catalysts

- Continued/expanded related-party commercial relationship with Controp-Paras Technologies Private Limited (Associate Company) up to an aggregate ~Rs. 450 crore for FY26-27 — a scale roughly equal to FY26 full-year consolidated revenue (~Rs. 477 crore), indicating this associate relationship is a materially significant part of the group's commercial activity.
- New related-party ceiling of up to Rs. 150 crore approved for transactions with subsidiary Paras Anti-Drone Technologies Private Limited — signals continued investment/commercial activity in the counter-drone/anti-drone technology vertical (see also the June 2026 Guardian-1 Interceptor IP license from Tandem Defense/Powerus).

## Named Risks

- Both material RPT resolutions (Controp-Paras and Paras Anti-Drone) required disclosure that "promoter/promoter group are interested in the agenda/resolution" per the subsequent voting-results filing — standard related-party governance flag.
- **Note (forward pointer)**: The Paras Anti-Drone Technologies RPT resolution proposed in this Notice was subsequently REJECTED by shareholders at the AGM on September 11, 2026 (see agm-voting-results-2026-09-11.md) — 56.80% voted against.
