## Management Commentary — Capacity / Expansion

"Paras Semiconductors today signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) with the Government of Madhya Pradesh to establish an advanced semiconductor packaging OSAT facility in the state. As part of the agreement, the Government of Madhya Pradesh has allotted fifty acres of land on the strategically located Ujjain–Indore Corridor for the proposed facility."

"Backed by a proposed investment of ₹6,200 crore, the project is expected to strengthen India's semiconductor manufacturing ecosystem"

"The project aligns with the Government of Madhya Pradesh's Semiconductor Policy 2025"

Press release (about the business): "Paras Semiconductor, the semiconductor business of Paras Defence & Space Technologies Ltd., has been established to build advanced semiconductor capabilities for strategic and high-performance sensor applications."

Press release (about the company): "Paras is the only Indian company developing and manufacturing Optronic Periscopes in the Asia-Pacific region for submarine applications."
