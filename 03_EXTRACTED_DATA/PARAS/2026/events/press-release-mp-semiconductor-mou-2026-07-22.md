## Named Catalysts

- **MoU signing with Government of Madhya Pradesh (via MPSeDC)**: Announced July 22, 2026, for an advanced semiconductor packaging OSAT facility.
- **Proposed investment of ₹6,200 crore** by Paras Semiconductors Private Limited (subsidiary of PARAS).
- **Fifty acres of land allotted** on the Ujjain–Indore Corridor, Madhya Pradesh, for the facility.
- **Government of Madhya Pradesh Semiconductor Policy 2025** cited as the enabling policy framework; project stated to align with the state's broader vision for semiconductors, electronics manufacturing, AI, and data centres.
- **Potential future expansion into AI chips** and other advanced semiconductor technologies "as market opportunities evolve" — explicitly framed as optionality, not a committed roadmap.
- Quote from Dr. Mohan Yadav, Chief Minister of Madhya Pradesh, welcoming the investment and framing it within "Aatmanirbhar Bharat" ambitions.
