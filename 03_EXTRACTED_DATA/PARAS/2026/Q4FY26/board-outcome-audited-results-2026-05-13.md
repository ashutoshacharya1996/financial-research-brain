## Named Catalysts

- **Final Dividend**: Board recommended ₹1/- per equity share of ₹5/- face value (20%) for FY26 — record date/AGM/payment date "to be communicated in due course" (later fixed as record date 2026-08-28, AGM 2026-09-11 per subsequent filings)
- Segment mix shift: Consolidated FY26 Defence Engineering segment revenue (₹277.47 cr) overtook Optics & Optronic Systems (₹199.10 cr) for the first time, driven by Q4 Defence Engineering revenue of ₹111.55 cr (vs ₹53.15 cr Q4 FY25, +110% YoY)

## Named Risks

- Divestment of 58.02% stake in subsidiary Ayatti Innovative Private Limited completed March 30, 2026 (sold 15,20,000 equity shares for ₹6.99 cr consideration); Ayatti ceased to be a subsidiary — reduces consolidated scope going forward
- One-time incremental Gratuity impact of ₹1.68 cr included in Employee Benefits Expense (Q3 FY26 and FY26) due to Government's consolidation of labour codes effective Nov 21, 2025
