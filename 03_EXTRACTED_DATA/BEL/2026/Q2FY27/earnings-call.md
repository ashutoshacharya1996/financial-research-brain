## Guidance

- Closing remarks (verbatim): "We are going to meet our guidance given at the start of the year. We are working for that revenue growth of 15-plus percent, although there may be some positive surprises as you have predicted. But right now, we are confident we will have 15-plus. EBITDA margins of around 28%, the order inflow of INR25,000 crores minimum, excluding QRSAM and including QRSAM, INR50,000-plus crores. R&D investment of INR2,200-plus crores, capex of around INR1,200 crores. And defense to nondefense business, typically 90:10."
- Note: the Q1 call (27 Jul) said "INR55,000 plus crores, including QRSAM"; Q2 closing remarks say "INR50,000-plus crores" including QRSAM, but in the same call: "So total INR25,000 crores without QRSAM and with QRSAM INR55,000 crores, which was our guidance, we are sticking to that." and "guidance point of view, we still want to stick to INR25,000 and INR55,000 crores only." Management states no change; wording inconsistency flagged.
- Revenue upgrade question: "No. ... overall, we are planning as of now between 15% to 20%, more than 15% growth. That only we are expecting. If something more comes, it will be a positive surprise for us also."

## Management Commentary — Demand

- QRSAM: "based on our inputs also, order is expected on us very shortly. Hopefully, in next CCS, they may clear. They are clearing one by one all the big projects." / "So this program, as of now and more than 99%, I am confident it is coming to us very soon."
- Private-sector rumour: "I don't think at this point, that private thing is possible." (on QRSAM being opened to private sector) / "if government decides and if private sector comes up to that level, definitely, there will be chances of getting different type of orders to them"
- Delay explanation (CMD to JPMorgan analyst): "internally, we also knew it may be minimum August, September only. So it has not slipped that much." Analyst (Nomura) described QRSAM as "derailed by almost 7, 8 months vis-a-vis our early expectation."
- QRSAM size: "as of now, I'm expecting INR30,000 crores order to come to us." (no escalation expected: "mostly, we may not get any escalation")
- NGC (Next Generation Corvette): "at least NGC is cleared, and it was signed also by the parent PSUs, GSL and GRSE." "tentatively, we had, I think, indicated around INR20,000 crores worth of orders. We are sticking to the same value only." Timing: "it may take 1 day to 6 months of different different line items"; "all of them, 99%, will come in this financial year"; "maybe 1- or 2-line items may slip." Execution "more around 4-plus years".
- Other FY27 orders: "Shatrughat and Samaghat files have moved considerably well, and we hope to get both the orders one by one before March. And HAMMER also, hopefully, we may get before March."
- P-75I: "maybe P-75I may slip to next year. I'm not fully sure." (contrast Q1 call: NGC or P75I "100% probability of having at least one")
- Order mix: "So total INR25,000 crores without QRSAM and with QRSAM INR55,000 crores"; "if we get all of the NGC orders before March, then definitely, we will give a positive surprise to all of you."
- AMCA: "We have already submitted the bid." (L&T front-ending, 50-50% consortium) "we are hopeful we will be L1"; evaluation "may take 2 to 3 months ... Hopefully, before March".
- Drones: "Few orders we have got this year also, but that orders are of the order of INR50 crores, INR100 crores type of thing." "After our missile, arms and ammunition, the second most focused area for us is drone and counter drone."
- Competition/nomination: "whether it is nomination or competition, more or less similar margins will be there" ... analyst premise: "roughly 90% of the order book right now is on the nomination basis."
- Virupaksha radar: "we are confident maybe around '28, '29, we may get production orders for this radar." (development order now)
- Execution speed: "we have now started our on-time delivery score. We are improving a lot." / "we wanted to make it more uniform."
- Tamil Nadu Explosives Ltd: "This Tamil Nadu Explosives Limited is now owned by BEL." (hot-integration facility).
- Seeker capacity: "Combined IR and RF seeker, 1,000 plus we can make easily right now."
- HAPS: "it is still maturing. It is not a mature solution."

## Management Commentary — Capacity / Expansion

- "we have started proactively good work on QRSAM, QT and FOPM realization-related activities" ... "once we get the order, we will try to squeeze a few months".
- QRSAM supply chain: "around 200-plus MSME partners will be there and roughly around 70% of the orders which we are getting will be distributed across these 200-plus MSME partners."
- Machilipatnam (Nimmaluru) IR seeker factory: "a very good state-of-the-art I4.0 compliant infrastructure for these IR seekers manufacturing."
- MoU with MIDHANI and BEML (steering/control for combat vehicles): "we are looking at some technology development funds or some other funds from Government of India" — funding not finalised.

## Named Catalysts

- QRSAM (~Rs 30,000 crore) — next CCS meeting.
- NGC buyer-nominated-equipment orders from GRSE/GSL, ~Rs 20,000 crore total, staggered Nov 2026 - Mar 2027 (some may slip to April).
- Shatrughat/Samaghat, HAMMER before March 2027.
- AMCA price-bid opening: "2 to 3 months of technical evaluations ... Hopefully, before March".
- R&D-day product launches: "we may launch a few products at that time."

## Named Risks

- QRSAM delay (already 7-8 months vs early expectation per analyst); CCS timing outside management's control.
- P-75I "may slip to next year".
- Private-sector entry into missiles/air defence (analyst questions; management dismisses for QRSAM).
- NGC order timing: "maybe 1- or 2-line items may slip."
- Order book decline (68,717 vs 73,882 crore at 1 Apr); analyst note of "order backlog being down Y-o-Y".
- Wage revision due January 2027 (from Q1 call).
