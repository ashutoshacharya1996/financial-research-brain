## Guidance

- Overall: "We are going to meet our guidance given at the start of the year."
- Revenue: "overall, we are planning as of now between 15% to 20%, more than 15% growth. That only we are expecting. If something more comes, it will be a positive surprise for us also."; closing: "revenue growth of 15-plus percent, although there may be some positive surprises"
- Order inflow: "So total INR25,000 crores without QRSAM and with QRSAM INR55,000 crores, which was our guidance, we are sticking to that."; "guidance point of view, we still want to stick to INR25,000 and INR55,000 crores only."
- Order inflow (closing remark wording): "the order inflow of INR25,000 crores minimum, excluding QRSAM and including QRSAM, INR50,000-plus crores."
- EBITDA: "EBITDA margins of around 28%"
- R&D/capex/mix: "R&D investment of INR2,200-plus crores, capex of around INR1,200 crores. And defense to nondefense business, typically 90:10."
- No formal guidance raise despite 26% H1 growth: "No. Let me tell you, the thing is although the growth looks 20-plus percent overall still H1, but that is based on our strategy of making as much uniform our production as possible across quarters."

## Management Commentary — Demand

- QRSAM: "order is expected on us very shortly. Hopefully, in next CCS, they may clear."; "more than 99%, I am confident it is coming to us very soon."; size: "as of now, I'm expecting INR30,000 crores order to come to us."; escalation: "mostly, we may not get any escalation"
- QRSAM delay: "internally, we also knew it may be minimum August, September only. So it has not slipped that much. ... we are hopeful in next years, we may get positive surprise. So we are not alarmed by the delays right now."
- NGC: "at least NGC is cleared, and it was signed also by the parent PSUs, GSL and GRSE."; "tentatively, we had, I think, indicated around INR20,000 crores worth of orders. We are sticking to the same value only."; timing: "it may take 1 day to 6 months"; "all of them, 99%, will come in this financial year."; "some order may slip to April also"
- NGC is inside the ₹25,000 cr ex-QRSAM bucket: "NGC order was included in this INR25,000 crores"
- Others: "Shatrughat and Samaghat files have moved considerably well, and we hope to get both the orders one by one before March. And HAMMER also, hopefully, we may get before March."
- P-75I: "maybe P-75I may slip to next year. I'm not fully sure."
- AMCA (with L&T, 50:50 consortium): "We have already submitted the bid. ... we are hopeful we will be L1"; outcome "Hopefully, before March"
- Drones: "Few orders we have got this year also, but that orders are of the order of INR50 crores, INR100 crores ... I hope next year, we may get at least 1 or 2 big orders"
- Missiles: "our official LRLACM and Pralay missile, we are the DCPP partner for DRDO, and we expect some orders in future"; "60% to 70% of missile is electronics"
- Seekers: "Combined IR and RF seeker, 1,000 plus we can make easily right now. And if need arises, we can scale it up ... to 2 to 3x more."
- Virupaksha radar: "maybe around '28, '29, we may get production orders for this radar."
- Order book not a concern: "order book, we are not worried right now because as we have told INR68,000-plus crores we are having, and hopefully INR55,000-plus crores we are going to get before year-end."

## Management Commentary — Margins

- Nomination vs competition: "whether it is nomination or competition, more or less similar margins will be there, okay? In nomination, we don't get higher margins."
- Execution speed: "we have now started our on-time delivery score. We are improving a lot. And whatsoever we had committed to our own users, we are preponing by another 1 month, 2 months"; "we wanted to correct ourselves to give more uniform output across each quarter."

## Management Commentary — Capacity / Expansion

- QRSAM pre-work: "we have started proactively good work on QRSAM, QT and FOPM realization-related activities"; "around 200-plus MSME partners will be there and roughly around 70% of the orders which we are getting will be distributed across these 200-plus MSME partners."
- Tamil Nadu Explosives: "This Tamil Nadu Explosives Limited is now owned by BEL. For the next 30 years, at least it is there with BEL. So, we have already done good investment there. And some of the missiles integration activity, we have started there also."
- Seeker facility: "a very good manufacturing setup for these IR seekers at our new factory in Machilipatnam, near to Machilipatnam, Nimmaluru."
- Cyber: "we created this new SBU itself, Network and Cyber Security as a separate SBU"
- Execution horizon: NGC "3, 3.5 to 4 years is the maximum execution time line"; fuze "6 to 7 years more"

## Named Catalysts

- QRSAM (~₹30,000 cr): "next CCS"
- NGC line items (~₹20,000+ cr, electronics incl. MFStar radar, excl. missiles): staggered over next ~6 months, mostly before March 2027
- Shatrughat & Samaghat, HAMMER: "before March"
- AMCA (BEL–L&T) price-bid outcome: "Hopefully, before March"
- P-75I: possibly FY28
- Drone "1 or 2 big orders" next year; counter-drone exports: "we are hopeful to get good export orders very soon"

## Named Risks

- QRSAM "derailed by almost 7, 8 months vis-a-vis our early expectation" (analyst framing; CMD: "we are not alarmed")
- Private-sector entry into missiles: "if government decides and if private sector comes up to that level, definitely, there will be chances of getting different type of orders to them"
- No price escalation on delayed QRSAM: "mostly, we may not get any escalation"
- NGC line items may slip to April 2027
- Order backlog down YoY (analyst: "order backlog being down Y-o-Y")
- Detector technology dependence: "The basic detector still is not totally in our control"
