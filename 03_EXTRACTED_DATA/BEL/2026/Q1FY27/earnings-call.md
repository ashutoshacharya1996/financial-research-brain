## Guidance

- Order inflow: "For us, it is yearly target and which we have already given at the start of the year, the guidance. So, that is INR55,000 plus crores, including QRSAM. So, we are sticking to that"
- Order inflow decomposition: "total INR55,000 crores, out of that INR30,000 crores roughly will be QRSAM, then around INR15,000 crores plus is these platform orders, and remaining will be the other base orders of smaller quantities, support, services, or AMCs"
- EBITDA margin (CFO): "Overall on a yearly basis, we maintain the EBITDA margin of 28%, which we have given already."
- EBITDA margin (CMD): "We have variability of 25% to 31% EBITDA margins based on the product mix in quarter-to-quarter etcetera, but at the year end, let me again assure you, we will cross 28%."
- Revenue: "our balanced plan is 15% and I am confident we will reach 15%. ... we are confident to meet or exceed 15% guidance."
- QRSAM timing: "it may go to Q1/Q2, maximum by September we will get and I am still sticking to that. Just CCS only is waiting for all of us"
- Employee cost: "we expect the employee cost to turnover to be in the range of around 12% even in the coming years also"
- Exports: "this year we have given our internal guidance of around USD300 million we should definitely fetch out of the leads"; "we want to finally aim at 10% of our revenues through export ... in next five years."
- Closing remark (verbatim, contains figures inconsistent with rest of call): "The revenue growth of 15%, EBITDA margin 21% to 23%, order inflow of more than INR20,000 crores including QRSAM, the R&D investment more than INR2,200 crores, capex more than INR1,200 crores with a typical defence non-defence business of 90:10."

## Management Commentary — Demand

- "We are definitely going to meet the guidance given about the order inflow for this year."
- On weak Q1 inflow: "last year ... one or two orders we got in the week of April ... our first quarter orders were very good, but actually, that was more of a spillover of the January to March quarter. This year, it was more structured."
- "definitely two, three big programs are in the real pipeline of CCS or other type of approvals. So, we are not foreseeing any problem in getting these order inflow for this year."
- Platform orders: "Shatrughat and Samaghat we may get in another 3 to 6 months timeline. So, biggest is that after QRSAM. Then as I told NGC or P75I, one of these two definitely we may go through"; "at least 100% probability of having at least one of them and more than 50% probability of getting both of them."
- Sizes: "for Shatrughat and Samaghat, I think I told you around INR9,000 plus crores ... about Shakti, it will be around INR2,000 crores. And about HAMMER also it is around INR2,500 plus crores. So, these three orders we are going to get in this financial year itself."
- Kusha: "the order will be -- of the order of INR40,000 plus crores we are expecting ... So, it is still a long way to go."
- Drones/counter-drone: "drone and counter-drone, this market is growing like anything ... What we are more focused is large high-power laser-based or microwave-based DEW solution integrated D4 solutions."
- Competition (Netra 2): "We also participated in the bid, but unfortunately, we were not L1. ... Adani became L1 and they are the system integrator for this program."
- Order book mix: "roughly 30%, 30%, 30% is Army, Navy, Air Force."
- FY27 revenue drivers: "Akash Army we are going to generate revenue more than INR1,000 crores, around INR1,200 crores. D-29 EW systems, MPR Arudhra, BMP-2 upgrade, LCA LRUs, so these are around INR500 crores to INR1,000 crores each"; LRSAM: "around INR2,200 crores to INR2,300 crores -- INR2,100 crores is planned in this year."

## Management Commentary — Margins

- CFO: "Quarter-on-quarter, there could be variations because of the composition of product mix."; "We are telling it is due to product mix variation, not due to input cost."
- CMD on material cost: "traditionally also because of our product mix; our material cost varies from 50% to 59%. Typically, it was around 55% ... right now we are not foreseeing any pressure from this RMC."
- Other expenses decline (CFO): "During the previous year, the provisions relating to the liquidated damages ... was on the higher side ... Due to which current year that is not there."
- Wage revision: "from January '27 onwards the wage revision is due. So, for the current year, the provisions will be made for 3 months period, January to March '27." ; "Last time the hike was in January 2017. That was for a 10-year period."
- R&D vs margin: "I don't foresee that 6.5% or 6.8% right now to increase to 8% will hit on margins."
- Indigenisation: "aim is in the next 5 years zero import of any module, sub-module level thing"; "Overall, our turnover 78% to 80% comes from indigenous products and technologies."

## Management Commentary — Capacity / Expansion

- "Our main capex is for infrastructure for our what we call production-related activities, capacity enhancement for that."
- AMCA: "AMCA program we and L&T are really working together"; "27th August 2026 is the date of RFP submission."
- Missiles: "we also are aspiring or entering into this missile domain"; "in most of these missile programs, the major electronics today we are the leader"
- Exports: "we have received largest order of around INR2,000 crores worth next generation software defined radios"

## Named Catalysts

- QRSAM (~₹30,000 cr): CCS approval; "maximum by September" (2026)
- Shatrughat & Samaghat (~₹9,000+ cr): "another 3 to 6 months"
- NGC or P-75I: at least one in FY27
- HAMMER (~₹2,500+ cr) and Shakti Phase 4 (~₹2,000 cr): FY27
- AMCA RFP submission: 27 Aug 2026 (with L&T)
- Kusha (~₹40,000+ cr): long-dated, post DRDO trials
- Indigenisation policy release: "formally we will release our indigenization policy"

## Named Risks

- QRSAM delay: "procedural delays we knew"
- Q1 margin decline: product mix (EBITDA 25.83% vs ~28% guidance)
- Wage revision from Jan 2027 (3-month provision in FY27)
- Tejas/HAL delays: "it may if at all affect by few hundred crores here and there ... maybe INR200 crores, INR300 crores turnover maybe shifted by few months"
- Competition: lost Netra 2 SI role to Adani (L1); private players entering missiles
- Israel conflict (filing note) — no material impact stated
