## Guidance

- Order inflow: "So, that is INR55,000 plus crores, including QRSAM. So, we are sticking to that" (Manoj Jain)
- Order inflow ex-QRSAM breakdown: "total INR55,000 crores, out of that INR30,000 crores roughly will be QRSAM, then around INR15,000 crores plus is these platform orders, and remaining will be the other base orders of smaller quantities, support, services, or AMCs"
- Revenue: "Definitely, as I told last year also, target is 15%. Last month we may surprise you by maybe 0.5%, 1%, 1.5%." and "we are confident to meet or exceed 15% guidance."
- EBITDA margin: "current year EBITDA margin guidance we have given of 28%, we remain at that." (CFO) and "We have variability of 25% to 31% EBITDA margins based on the product mix in quarter-to-quarter etcetera, but at the year end, let me again assure you, we will cross 28%." (CMD)
- Export: "this year we have given our internal guidance of around USD300 million we should definitely fetch out of the leads which we are having." Long-term: "we want to finally aim at 10% of our revenues through export" within 5 years.
- Closing remarks (verbatim, transcript contains apparent errors — "21% to 23%" EBITDA and ">INR20,000 crores including QRSAM" conflict with 28% and Rs 55,000 crore stated elsewhere on the same call): "The revenue growth of 15%, EBITDA margin 21% to 23%, order inflow of more than INR20,000 crores including QRSAM, the R&D investment more than INR2,200 crores, capex more than INR1,200 crores with a typical defence non-defence business of 90:10."

## Management Commentary — Demand

- On weak Q1 inflow: "This year, it was more structured. So, whatsoever we were going to get by January to March, actually we got -- actually INR2,000 to INR3,000 more, I can tell you, because we thought around INR25,000, but we finally landed in around INR30,000 plus." ... "We are definitely going to meet the guidance given about the order inflow for this year."
- QRSAM: "QRSAM, we told we were highly optimistic to get it by March, but procedural delays we knew, that is why we told it may go to Q1/Q2, maximum by September we will get and I am still sticking to that." and "Just CCS only is waiting for all of us and hopefully, when CCS meets, I think they will clear QRSAM."
- Pipeline ex-QRSAM: "Shatrughat and Samaghat we may get in another 3 to 6 months timeline." "NGC or P75I, one of these two definitely we may go through ... at least 100% probability of having at least one of them and more than 50% probability of getting both of them." "HAMMER project also, we are going to get in this financial year and Shakti Phase 4 project."
- Order sizes: "for Shatrughat and Samaghat, I think I told you around INR9,000 plus crores, we may get from Shatrughat and Samaghat. And about Shakti, it will be around INR2,000 crores. And about HAMMER also it is around INR2,500 plus crores."
- Service split: "Army, Navy, Air Force, more or less it is similar. So, 30%, 30%, 30% out of the total 100% which we are having, so 90% is from defence."
- Project Kusha (DRDO): "I think the order will be -- of the order of INR40,000 plus crores we are expecting, but that will be minimum 28-29 which we had indicated last time also." ... "So, it is still a long way to go."
- Large platforms timing: "These large platform orders, they will start giving me big turnover after two years, not immediately."
- Naval follow-ons: "right now it is too early to predict the business volume of that" (P17B/P18/NGD); "these programs are definitely beyond '28, '29."
- AMCA: "27th August 2026 is the date of RFP submission." (with L&T)
- Competition — Netra 2: "Adani became L1 and they are the system integrator for this program." "the SI role in this one we missed."
- Tejas LRUs: "no Tejas will be delayed because of LRUs being supplied by BEL late." ... "in case they tell, it may if at all affect by few hundred crores here and there for us. So, maybe INR200 crores, INR300 crores turnover maybe shifted by few months"
- Execution FY27: "Akash Army we are going to generate revenue more than INR1,000 crores, around INR1,200 crores. D-29 EW systems, MPR Arudhra, BMP-2 upgrade, LCA LRUs, so these are around INR500 crores to INR1,000 crores each"; LRSAM: "around INR2,100 crores is planned in this year."
- Order book composition: "the top seven projects which are there in our order book as on 1st July" are Fuzes, LRSAM, LCA Mk1/Mk1A LRUs, BMP-2 upgrade, Ashwini Radar, EW suite Mi-17 V5, MPR Arudhra — "around INR20,000 crores plus of order book" (note CMD also said "out of INR76,000 crores" — inconsistent with Rs 72,258 crore).

## Management Commentary — Margins

- "Quarter-on-quarter, there could be variations because of the composition of product mix." ... "there is no particular cause of concern for the margin on the quarterly basis."
- On input costs: "Yes, Yes, Yes. We are telling it is due to product mix variation, not due to input cost."
- Material cost: "our material cost varies from 50% to 59%. Typically, it was around 55%" ... "right now we are not foreseeing any pressure from this RMC."
- Other expenses decline: "the provisions relating to the liquidated damages during this current quarter April to June of '22 was on the higher side based on the delivery schedule of those contracts which were executed during the previous year. Due to which current year that is not there." (CFO; "'22" appears to be a transcription error for the prior year)
- Wage revision: "current financial year from January '27 onwards the wage revision is due. So, for the current year, the provisions will be made for 3 months period, January to March '27." and "we expect the employee cost to turnover to be in the range of around 12% even in the coming years also"
- R&D and margins: "More you invest in R&D, likely chance of more EBITDA is there"; R&D currently "6.5% or 6.8%", aspiration 8% (analyst reference).
- Competitive pricing: not discussed on this call.

## Management Commentary — Capacity / Expansion

- "INR1,200 plus crores we are investing under capex which will take care of our production and other type of investments."
- "aim is in the next 5 years zero import of any module, sub-module level thing." (barring semiconductor components) — indigenisation policy to be released.
- Counter-drone/DEW: "we have got enough orders already in last three years for this 2 kilowatt laser-based DEW solution and which we have most of that, something like 80% of those orders already we have supplied also." Microwave DEW "prototype ready" with DRDO.
- Receivables: "So, receivables are good. Cash flows are good. There is not much concern on those front."
