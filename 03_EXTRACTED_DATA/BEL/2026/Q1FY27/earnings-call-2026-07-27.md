## Guidance

Verbatim, as stated on the call (statements are not consistent with one another within the transcript):

- Order inflow (CMD): "the guidance given about the order inflow for this year. So, that is INR55,000 plus crores, including QRSAM. So, we are sticking to that"
- Order inflow composition (CMD): "total INR55,000 crores, out of that INR30,000 crores roughly will be QRSAM, then around INR15,000 crores plus is these platform orders, and remaining will be the other base orders of smaller quantities, support, services, or AMCs"
- EBITDA margin (CFO): "current year EBITDA margin guidance we have given of 28%, we remain at that."
- EBITDA margin (CMD): "at the year end, let me again assure you, we will cross 28%."
- EBITDA quarterly variability (CMD): "We have variability of 25% to 31% EBITDA margins based on the product mix in quarter-to-quarter"
- Revenue growth (CMD): "target is 15%. Last month we may surprise you by maybe 0.5%, 1%, 1.5%. I don't know how much more we can have, but our balanced plan is 15% and I am confident we will reach 15%." and "we are confident to meet or exceed 15% guidance."
- Closing remarks (CMD): "The revenue growth of 15%, EBITDA margin 21% to 23%, order inflow of more than INR20,000 crores including QRSAM, the R&D investment more than INR2,200 crores, capex more than INR1,200 crores with a typical defence non-defence business of 90:10."
- Analyst premise (Bhavya Gandhi, not management): "you've guided earlier for 21% to 23% EBITDA margin"; CMD replied "Definitely yes."
- Export (CMD): "this year we have given our internal guidance of around USD300 million we should definitely fetch out of the leads which we are having." and "we want to finally aim at 10% of our revenues through export."
- Employee cost (CFO): "we expect the employee cost to turnover to be in the range of around 12% even in the coming years also"
- Wage revision (CFO): "from January '27 onwards the wage revision is due. So, for the current year, the provisions will be made for 3 months period, January to March '27."

## Management Commentary - Demand

- On Q1 order inflow: "last year, you may be knowing that we were supposed to get orders by 31st March, but one or two orders we got in the week of April. So, that is why we could not meet our actually previous year target, but our first quarter orders were very good, but actually, that was more of a spillover of the January to March quarter."
- "So, this year whatsoever we have received, INR307 plus crores it is what was we planned, that only. So, as such, there is nothing to worry. We are definitely going to meet the guidance given about the order inflow for this year."
- On quarterly phasing: "we don't publish quarter-by-quarter targets for this. That is our internal assessments only"
- "definitely two, three big programs are in the real pipeline of CCS or other type of approvals. So, we are not foreseeing any problem in getting these order inflow for this year."
- Service split: "Army, Navy, Air Force, more or less it is similar. So, 30%, 30%, 30% out of the total 100% which we are having, so 90% is from defence."
- Order book composition: "the main components are Electronic Fuze for Army, LRSAM for Navy, LCA LRUs for Air Force, BMP-2 upgrade again for Army, Ashwini Radar for Air Force, and EW suite Mi-17 V5 again for Air Force, MPR Arudhra Air Force."
- Order book concentration: "These seven, eight projects are around INR20,000 crores out of INR76,000 crores." and "top seven items which consist of around INR20,000 crores plus of order book today for me."
- Counter-drone: "the market is growing exponentially where there is an opportunity for big companies like BEL, mid-sized companies, private sector like Zen Technologies, so many other companies are there, and startups."
- Counter-drone positioning: "this large complex domain solution, right now we are the de facto leader."
- Netra 2: "We also participated in the bid, but unfortunately, we were not L1." and "in this particular program, Adani became L1 and they are the system integrator for this program." and "the SI role in this one we missed."
- Missiles: "in most of these missile programs, the major electronics today we are the leader" and "In two, three programs, we are actually collaborating with private fellows and then we have got some orders as DcPP partner."
- Tejas / LCA LRUs: "no Tejas will be delayed because of LRUs being supplied by BEL late." and "in case they tell, it may if at all affect by few hundred crores here and there for us. So, maybe INR200 crores, INR300 crores turnover maybe shifted by few months for me."
- Large platform orders timing: "These large platform orders, they will start giving me big turnover after two years, not immediately."
- Export: "Almost all products and technologies which we are right now delivering to our Indian customers, there is a huge demand of that in international market also." and "export we are having great leads right now. But leads to getting the order sometime takes a little bit more time." and "the leads which we are pursuing is at least four to five times more than that."
- Indigenous share: "Overall, our turnover 78% to 80% comes from indigenous products and technologies."

## Management Commentary - Margins

- CFO on Q1 margin: "Quarter-on-quarter, there could be variations because of the composition of product mix." and "it is due to product mix variation, not due to input cost."
- CFO: "Overall on a yearly basis, we maintain the EBITDA margin of 28%, which we have given already."
- Other expenses decline (CFO): "During the previous year, the provisions relating to the liquidated damages during this current quarter April to June of '22 was on the higher side based on the delivery schedule of those contracts which were executed during the previous year. Due to which current year that is not there."
- Raw material cost (CMD): "it is mainly because of product mix only." and "traditionally also because of our product mix; our material cost varies from 50% to 59%. Typically, it was around 55%"
- Input costs (CMD): "No, we are not foreseeing any change because of material clause inflation." and "we are in the continuous state of indigenization drive and that will indirectly compensate for this type of material cost escalations"
- R&D vs margin (CMD): "More you invest in R&D, likely chance of more EBITDA is there" and "I don't foresee that 6.5% or 6.8% right now to increase to 8% will hit on margins."
- Wage revision (CFO): "Last time the hike was in January 2017. That was for a 10-year period. So, the next hike is in 2027 January 1st." and "we cannot tell at this time what will be the hike"

## Management Commentary - Capacity / Expansion

- Capex (CMD): "Our main capex is for infrastructure for our what we call production-related activities, capacity enhancement for that. That as we have given you guidance, INR1,200 plus crores we are investing under capex"
- R&D (CMD): "more than INR2,200 crores R&D budget we have allocated for this year for all type of niche technology development, indigenization, and other collaborative R&Ds."
- Indigenization (CMD): "aim is in the next 5 years zero import of any module, sub-module level thing. So, barring components, semiconductor components"
- Diversification (CMD): "diversification into civilian domain like Rail, Metro, Aviation sector, so like that also we are doing, Space."
- Direct energy weapons (CMD): "we have got enough orders already in last three years for this 2 kilowatt laser-based DEW solution and which we have most of that, something like 80% of those orders already we have supplied also." and "microwave DEW based solutions right now still under evaluations, although we have the prototype ready."
- Execution plan FY27 (CMD): "Akash Army we are going to generate revenue more than INR1,000 crores, around INR1,200 crores. D-29 EW systems, MPR Arudhra, BMP-2 upgrade, LCA LRUs, so these are around INR500 crores to INR1,000 crores each one of them will give to us."
- LRSAM (CMD): "out of the INR3,000 crores which is leftover right now for LRSAM program, around INR2,200 crores to INR2,300 crores -- INR2,100 crores is planned in this year."
