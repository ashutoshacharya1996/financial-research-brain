## Guidance (FY27 / FY26-27, verbatim closing remarks by Manoj Jain)

- Revenue growth: "we are retaining our revenue growth of more than 15%. So definitely, we are going to have more than 15% as a revenue growth for '26-'27."
- EBITDA margin: "EBITDA margins will be more than 28%."
- Order inflow: "The order inflow we are expecting this year more than INR55,000 crores. That includes, of course, QRSAM, which we are expecting very soon."
- R&D investment: "this year, we have targeted a value of around INR2,200 crores investment in R&D."
- Capex: "the capex also, again, 20%-plus growth. So, we are targeting more than INR1,200 crores as a capital investment for this year."
- Defence/non-defence revenue mix: "defense to non-defense ratio, more or less, it will be 90% to 10%, maybe plus/minus 1%."
- Order book execution timeline (major line items, next 2-3 years): "Electronic fuses around INR4,300 crores still left for us for next 7 years. LRSAM around INR3,500 crores, LCA around INR3,200 crores, BMP-II upgrade around INR2,800 crores, Ashwini, around INR2,460 crores, Mi-17 V5around INR2,200 crores spare services, miscellaneous, again around INR2,500 crores." — Manoj Jain

## Management Commentary — Demand

- On order book composition: "the order book of around INR74,000 crores mainly consist of some big ticket few items... The electronic fuses, the LRFM, the LCA Mark 1/Mark 1A LRUs for Tejas, BMP-II upgrade, spare services and miscellaneous items, Ashwini radar then EW Suite for Mi-17 V5." — Manoj Jain
- On growth sustainability: "there will not be any downtrend. We have seen we are highly optimistic for the next 5 years, at least where our main, main leads are, and we are confident we will have higher trajectory only, not at all a lower trajectory." — Manoj Jain
- On QRSAM timing: "we are still fully optimistic that before June end, we may get this order. There is only 5% to 10% chance that it may slip to July... Worst case, it may slip by 1 more month." — Manoj Jain
- On QRSAM delivery: "once we sign the program within 18 months, we are supposed to give the first of production model." — Manoj Jain
- On upcoming large programmes: "NGC, there are some so many subsystems of NGC, Next Generation Corvette program, which definitely will come this year... The Shatrughat and Samghat EW solutions, we are hoping very, very soon. P-75I... HAMMER program, we are expecting very soon... Shakti Phase IV, we are expecting very soon. MFR-X radar for naval ships also we are expecting very soon." — Manoj Jain
- On P-75I submarine programme: "more than 50% to 60% of electronics in this program will be from BEL... there are around 6 subsystems... Communication Suite, Navigation Complex System, The Combat Weapon Control System, Combat Information System, Torpedo Fire Control System, Missile Fire Control System." — Manoj Jain; execution cycle "of the order of 5 years"
- On AMCA: "we are one of the 3 selected bidders for receiving the RFP... BEL and L&T. So, L&T was our lead bidder... L&T will receive the RFP mostly anytime in the next 15 days to 1.5 months." — Manoj Jain
- On data centre business: "we are in a very, very advanced stages of discussions with them to provide end-to-end totally homegrown data center solutions for a large number of customers. So, once we click on that, that orders we are expecting of the tune of INR2,000 crores to INR10,000 crores... The first order will be from the -- right now, maybe a few hundred crores we already have, but if good leads are there, we may expect around INR1,000 crores to INR5,000 crores type of business from the first segment of business." — Manoj Jain
- On exports: "The order book is healthy from export point of view. We have around USD96 million order book for this year and subsequent year." / "nondefense right now, it is 8% to 10%. We wanted to steadily increase it to 15% to 20%. And the same thing is about exports, which is 4% to 5% right now over a period of time... We want to increase it to more than 10% of our turnover... in near future of next 1, 1.5 years, the increase will be maybe 1% or 2%, not more." — Manoj Jain
- On export leads post Ops Sindoor: "after Ops Sindoor, we have got very good leads for truncated C4I solutions also, customized C4I solutions for various countries." — Manoj Jain

## Management Commentary — Margins

- On new technology margins: "margins should be better only when new technologies comes in because new technology means more value addition." — Manoj Jain
- On semiconductor cost exposure: "semiconductor per se is around 17% to 19% of our material cost, VoP... per se, semiconductor cost increase, if at all, it increases, it affects only this portion only. So overall, on the margin, it may not affect us that much." — Manoj Jain
- On indigenization and margins: "it is only and only indigenization, which has really helped all of us. That much I can assure you." — Manoj Jain (responding to question on why margins expanded despite PBT-norm pricing changes)
- On indigenous content level: "we are of the order of 80% to 85% mostly in our indigenous content for various programs... Our homegrown products or our DRDO driven products, sometimes that is even 90% also." — Manoj Jain
- On FY27 margin outlook / wage cost risk: "on the last quarter, we may expect a little bit more wage expenses [wage revision due 1/1/27]. But taking into consideration that our indigenization, our all-product mix of this year, based on that only, we... have arrived at the guidance." — Manoj Jain

## Management Commentary — Capacity / Expansion

- On capex plans: "last year, I think we had INR900 crores capital expenditure was booked. This year... it will be definitely much more than that. But we have much more bigger plans for the next 3 years. Some big projects at Palasamudram, at Chitrakoot, and Vellore facility, they are in pipeline for us in addition to our Ghaziabad and Bangalore." — Manoj Jain
- On capacity headroom: "we are not seeing any choking for us for next few years, maybe 3 to 5 years... But we are continuously adding so that we don't face this type of choking for next 10 to 15 years also." — Manoj Jain
- On compute infrastructure for AI/quantum: "of the order of minimum INR100-plus crores in last 2 years we have invested and at least around INR100 crores to INR200 crores worth of investments are in different stages of approval." — Manoj Jain (re: AI/quantum computing infrastructure at CRL Ghaziabad, CRL Bangalore, DSTC, Unmanned Systems SBU, Network & Cyber Security SBU, Palasamudram)
- Hiring / headcount: not discussed on this call — no explicit figures given.

## Named Catalysts

- QRSAM (Quick Reaction Surface-to-Air Missile) contract signing — expected "before June end", 5-10% chance of slipping to July; included in FY27 >₹55,000 crore order inflow guidance; first production model due within 18 months of signing.
- Next Generation Corvette (NGC) programme — "at least 50% of the subsystems we are hoping to get this year."
- P-75I submarine programme (~₹90,000 crore overall per analyst estimate, not confirmed by management) — BEL electronics share 50-60%; ~5 year execution cycle; advanced-stage discussions with MDL and MDL's foreign partner.
- AMCA (Advanced Medium Combat Aircraft) — BEL + L&T consortium among 3 selected bidders for RFP; RFP expected within "15 days to 1.5 months" of the call (i.e. ~June-July 2026).
- Data centre solutions — homegrown end-to-end solution in advanced discussion stage with C-DAC; potential order value ₹2,000-10,000 crore.
- Shatrughat and Samghat EW solutions, HAMMER programme, Shakti Phase IV, MFR-X radar (naval) — all described as expected "very soon."
- Project Kusha (DRDO-led air defence interceptor programme) — BEL supplying radars, control centres, communication systems; trial timing and configuration decided by DRDO, not BEL.
- Directed Energy Weapon (DEW) systems — BEL is DRDO's largest development-cum-production partner (CHESS for laser-based DEW, MTRDC for microwave-based DEW); some indigenous subsystem development also in progress internally.

## Named Risks

- QRSAM contract still unsigned as of call date; management itself flags "5% to 10% chance that it may slip to July," with a further possible 1-month slip in worst case.
- Supply chain: "the supply chain per se is definitely slightly affected, especially the Middle East crisis. And some of the subcomponents for major designs, major programs like LRSAM, etcetera, were coming from Middle East. So definitely, there was a delay of around 1, 1.5 months for us." — Manoj Jain (management characterizes overall annual impact as minor)
- Semiconductor imports: "all semiconductors right now are imported because still in India, that infrastructure is just coming up" — import dependency flagged, though management says overall margin impact limited (~17-19% of material cost).
- Wage cost step-up expected from Q4 FY27 due to wage revision due 1/1/27 — flagged by management as already factored into guidance.
- Receivable days up ~30 days YoY at FY26 year-end — attributed by management to "some constraints from the customer side during the previous year, which money was subsequently received in April and May" (temporary, not structural per management).
- Export order timing uncertainty: "in export, the real challenge is to acquire an order because there are so many geopolitical situations, complications, etcetera... there is always a doubt when it comes to export order acquisition." — Manoj Jain
