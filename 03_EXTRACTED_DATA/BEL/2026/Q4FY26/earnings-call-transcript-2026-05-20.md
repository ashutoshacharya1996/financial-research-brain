## Guidance

FY27 guidance given in closing remarks:
- "we are retaining our revenue growth of more than 15%. So definitely, we are going to have more than 15% as a revenue growth for '26-'27."
- "EBITDA margins will be more than 28%."
- "The order inflow we are expecting this year more than INR55,000 crores. That includes, of course, QRSAM, which we are expecting very soon."
- "this year, we have targeted a value of around INR2,200 crores investment in R&D."
- "the capex also, again, 20%-plus growth. So, we are targeting more than INR1,200 crores as a capital investment for this year."
- "defense to non-defense ratio, more or less, it will be 90% to 10%, maybe plus/minus 1%."
- On FY26 guidance track record: "we have met all the guidance parameters in the last financial year."
- Wage revision due "1/1/27" — "on the last quarter, we may expect a little bit more wage expenses" (Q4 FY27 impact flagged).

## Management Commentary — Demand

- QRSAM: "we are still fully optimistic that before June end, we may get this order. There is only 5% to 10% chance that it may slip to July... Worst case, it may slip by 1 more month."
- On order book growth trajectory: "every year we get some fixed set of orders. And every 3 to 4 years, we get some big-ticket projects... like this year, QRSAM, definitely, we are going to get now anytime soon... we are highly optimistic for the next 5 years... we are confident we will have higher trajectory only, not at all a lower trajectory."
- Named near-term order pipeline (Manoj Jain): "NGC, there are so many subsystems of NGC, Next Generation Corvette program, which definitely will come this year... at least 50% of the subsystems we are hoping to get this year only. The Shatrughat and Samghat EW solutions, we are hoping very, very soon. P-75I... 6 subcomponents within that... HAMMER program, we are expecting very soon... Shakti Phase IV, we are expecting very soon. MFR-X radar for naval ships also we are expecting very soon."
- P-75I submarine programme: "more than 50% to 60% of electronics in this program will be from BEL... 6 subsystems... Communication Suite, Navigation Complex System, The Combat Weapon Control System, Combat Information System, Torpedo Fire Control System, Missile Fire Control System." Execution cycle: "of the order of 5 years."
- AMCA: "we are one of the 3 selected bidders for receiving the RFP... L&T was our lead bidder... L&T will receive the RFP mostly anytime in the next 15 days to 1.5 months."
- Data centre business: "we are in a very, very advanced stages of discussions with them [C-DAC] to provide end-to-end totally homegrown data center solutions... once we click on that, that orders we are expecting of the tune of INR2,000 crores to INR10,000 crores... first order... maybe a few hundred crores we already have, but if good leads are there, we may expect around INR1,000 crores to INR5,000 crores type of business from the first segment."
- Non-defence and export mix: "nondefense right now, it is 8% to 10%. We wanted to steadily increase it to 15% to 20%... exports, which is 4% to 5% right now... around 4 to 5 years... We want to increase it to more than 10% of our turnover... in near future of next 1, 1.5 years, the increase will be maybe 1% or 2%, not more."
- Export leads: "after Ops Sindoor, we have got very good leads for truncated C4I solutions also, customized C4I solutions for various countries."
- Project Kusha / DRDO programs: "we are one of the largest Development-cum-Production Partner for them... various radars, various control centers, communication systems, they are in very, very advanced stage of delivery or prototype realization."
- Directed Energy Weapons (DEW): "we are their largest development production partner for most of the DEW programs" (DRDO's CHESS for laser-based DEW, MTRDC for microwave-based DEW); "we have also started indigenizing some of the critical subsystems of these programs on our own."

## Management Commentary — Margins

- "margins should be better only when new technologies comes in because new technology means more value addition."
- On semiconductor cost exposure: "semiconductor per se is around 17% to 19% of our material cost. VoP... semiconductor cost increase, if at all, it increases, it affects only this portion only. So overall, on the margin, it may not affect us that much."
- Indigenization as margin driver: "it is the indigenization, indigenization of critical technology, indigenization of modules, system, subsystems. That has definitely helped all of us for that... The more faster we do indigenization, the more profitable all of us will be."
- Indigenous content level: "we are of the order of 80% to 85% mostly in our indigenous content for various programs... Our homegrown products or our DRDO driven products, sometimes that is even 90% also... main limitation which we are foreseeing is the semiconductors only."
- On working capital / receivables: "receivables have gone up in the current year as compared to last year. There were some constraints from the customer side during the previous year, which money was subsequently received in April and May." Sustainable receivable cycle: "It has been in the constant around 140 to 150 days for the past 4, 5 years."
- On EBITDA/cash conversion (analyst-flagged, management response evasive): "the current cash position is reasonably okay for us to sustain the plans which we have got for the future" (did not directly address the low 19%/6.8% conversion ratios cited by the analyst).
- Middle East supply chain: "the supply chain per se is definitely slightly affected, especially the Middle East crisis. And some of the subcomponents for major designs, major programs like LRSAM, etcetera, were coming from Middle East. So definitely, there was a delay of around 1, 1.5 months for us... that setback was only for a few months."

## Management Commentary — Capacity / Expansion

- Compute/AI infrastructure: "of the order of minimum INR100-plus crores in last 2 years we have invested and at least around INR100 crores to INR200 crores worth of investments are in different stages of approval... creating at 3 to 4 places, including our CRL Ghaziabad, our CRL Bangalore, our DSTC, our Unmanned Systems, our Network and Cyber Security SBU and our Palasamudram facilities... we are going to create one separate high computing -- high-performance computing infrastructure very soon."
- Broader capex plans: "we have much more bigger plans for the next 3 years. Some big projects at Palasamudram, at Chitrakoot, and Vellore facility, they are in pipeline for us in addition to our Ghaziabad and Bangalore."
- Capacity headroom: "we are not seeing any choking for us for next few years, maybe 3 to 5 years... continuously adding so that we don't face this type of choking for next 10 to 15 years also."

## Named Catalysts

- QRSAM contract signing — expected by June end 2026, with only "5% to 10% chance" of slipping to July; worst case slips "1 more month"; first production model due within 18 months of signing.
- NGC (Next Generation Corvette) — ~50% of subsystems expected this year (FY27), balance may spill to next year.
- Shatrughat and Samghat EW solutions — expected "very, very soon."
- P-75I submarine programme (6 subsystems) — some orders expected this year (FY27); ~5-year execution cycle once received.
- HAMMER programme — expected "very soon."
- Shakti Phase IV — expected "very soon."
- MFR-X radar (naval ships) — expected "very soon."
- AMCA RFP — expected to be received by L&T (BEL as consortium partner) "in the next 15 days to 1.5 months" from call date (i.e., by ~early July 2026).
- Data centre total solution with C-DAC — orders of ₹2,000–10,000 crore expected once discussions conclude (no firm timeline given).
- Wage revision — due 01.01.2027; flagged as a Q4 FY27 cost pressure.

## Named Risks

- QRSAM timing risk: 5–10% chance of slipping from June to July 2026; "worst case, it may slip by 1 more month."
- Middle East supply-chain disruption: ~1–1.5 month delay to subcomponents for LRSAM and similar programmes.
- Semiconductor cost exposure: 17–19% of material cost, entirely import-dependent; India-based semiconductor supply "will take at least a few more years."
- Order book has been broadly flat versus revenue growth (analyst-flagged): "your order book has not grown this year... your annual order inflows are very similar to your revenue number" — management response affirmed reliance on periodic (3–4 year cycle) large "big-ticket" orders like QRSAM to sustain growth.
- Receivables/working capital cycle lengthened in FY26 vs FY25 (~30 days higher per analyst); management attributed to customer-side payment delays subsequently collected in April–May.
- Cash conversion (OCF/EBITDA) low at ~19% in FY26 (~6.8% in FY25); management gave no specific remediation commitment.
- Export order acquisition uncertainty: "the real challenge is to acquire an order because there are so many geopolitical situations, complications, etcetera... until we receive an order for us, it is a lead only."
- Q4 FY27 wage-cost step-up flagged due to 1/1/27 wage revision.
