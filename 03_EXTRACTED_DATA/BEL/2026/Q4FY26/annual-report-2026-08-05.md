"Moving forward, BEL is targeting a healthy growth of 15-20% in the financial year 2026-27."

## Management Commentary - Demand

- "Defence continued to be our mainstay contributing to 88% to total revenue in FY 2025-26, while non-defence contributed the remaining 12%."
- "Continued growth in the Indian economy and" ... "turbulence in the global security environment have led to" ... "accelerated military spending" (two-column interleaved text; fragments quoted separately)
- "Non-Defence segment businesses like Rail & Metro solutions, civil aviation solutions, network and cyber security solutions and other diversification areas will also continue contributing to growth."
- "achieve 84% of our turnover from indigenous products."
- Major FY26 orders (CMD): "Avionics for LCA, Mountain Radars, EW Suite for Helicopters, Air Defence Radars, EOIR Payloads for Airborne and Naval Platforms, EW Systems for Naval Platforms, Fire Control & Sighting System for Tanks, Mobile Communication Terminal, Network Systems, Counter Unmanned Aerial System, Upgrades, and Spares & Services."

## Management Commentary - Margins

- "Several indigenisation and cost reduction initiatives introduced in the past years helped increase EBITDA to ` 8,015 Crore, from ` 6,768 Crore in the earlier year."
- Strength listed: "Sustaining the growing EBITDA margins with operating leverage"

## Management Commentary - Capacity / Expansion

- "Advanced Night Vision Devices facility (ANVDF) at Nimmaluru in Andhra Pradesh was inaugurated by Hon'ble Prime Minister in October 2025."
- "A new facility for land-based Electronic Warfare System has been created at Ibrahimpatnam, Telangana."
- "The outdoor testing facility has been operationalised at Defence System Integration Complex, Palasamudram, Andhra Pradesh."
- "Formed and operationalised a joint venture with IAI, Israel (BEL IAI AeroSystems Pvt Ltd), for addressing product support requirement of MRSAM program."
- "A new Software Development Centre has been operationalised at Indore to develop mission critical software solutions and services."
- "Set up four new Regional Product Support Centres (RPSCs) at Jodhpur, Pathankot, Kolkata and Chabua"
- Manufacturing base: "9 state-of-the-art manufacturing facilities"
