## Headline Delta

Growth stalled in Q2 FY27 (operational revenue +3.4% YoY, standalone revenue -3.7%) while the order book shrank for two straight quarters (₹1,961 → ₹1,801 → ₹1,662 Cr), the June UAE export order was cancelled, and FY27 guidance was effectively cut from 25–30% revenue growth / ~14–15% EBITDA margin (Q4 FY26 call) to 20–25% / 12–14% without being acknowledged as a cut — partly offset by a ~₹500 Cr Nigeria heater order (largest export order to date) received Nov 3, after the quarter.

## Management Tone Delta

**Demand commentary:**
- Prior (Q4 FY26 press release, May 20): "JNK India is well-positioned to capture opportunities in both traditional and sustainable energy sectors in India and abroad."
- Current (Q2 FY27 call, Nov 6): "The performance reflects strong underlying business momentum despite temporary execution challenges during Q2 arising from shipment delays and monsoon-related disruptions." / "Large and technically complex projects can have longer decision-making cycles."
- Delta: **Deteriorating** — confident framing retained, but execution delays and longer decision cycles now explicitly cited; order book declining. Strength: High

**Margin commentary:**
- Prior (Q4 FY26 call, May 21): "our endeavour is to keep the margin around similar lines what we have achieved in Q3 and Q4. So that is about 14% and 15%."
- Current (Q1 FY27 call, Aug 12): "We also maintain our full year EBITDA margin guidance of about 12% to 14%." (Q2, Nov 6): "EBITDA margin of 12%-14% on the operational revenue."
- Delta: **Deteriorating** — guided band moved down ~200 bps and basis narrowed to operational revenue (excl. other income); H1 actual 10.6% below band. Strength: High

**Guidance:**
- Prior (Q4 FY26 call, May 21, verbatim tier_1): "Hence, we expect a revenue growth of around 25% to 30% in FY27."
- Current (Q1 call Aug 12): "Our revenue growth guidance of around 20% to 25% remains intact." (Q2 press release Nov 6): "continue to maintain our FY27 revenue growth guidance of 20–25% and EBITDA margin# guidance of 12–14%."
- Inconsistency: Aug 14 Corporate Presentation (two days after Q1 call): "On track for EBITDA margin guidance of 14 -15% and revenue growth of 25-30% for FY27". Q1 call, when challenged: "what we have announced was 12% to 14%. But for some reason, we are missing something, these numbers, we'll recheck them."
- Delta: **Lowered** (framed by management as "maintained"/"intact"; never acknowledged as a revision). Maintained between Q1 and Q2. Strength: High

## Key Signals Summary

1. **FY27 guidance effectively cut (unacknowledged)** — Lowered — Strength: High
   Evidence: May 21: "revenue growth of around 25% to 30% in FY27" / "about 14% and 15%" → Aug 12 & Nov 6: "20–25%" / "12–14%"; Aug 14 deck still showed 25–30% / 14–15%.

2. **Order book down two quarters running; UAE order cancelled** — Inflection down — Strength: High
   Evidence: ₹1,961.4 Cr (Mar) → ₹1,801 Cr (Jun) → ₹1,662 Cr (Sep); "the aforesaid order stands cancelled".

3. **Q2 FY27 growth stall** — Inflection down — Strength: High
   Evidence: Total operational revenue ₹187.5 Cr, +3.4% YoY; PAT ₹13.3 Cr, +2.0%; standalone revenue -3.7%, EBITDA -9.5%; H1 EBITDA margin 10.6% below 12–14% guidance.

4. **Nigeria ~₹500 Cr heater order (largest export order to date)** — Inflection up (post-quarter) — Strength: High
   Evidence: "we have secured an order worth approximately INR500 crores from JNK Global for a heater package for a project in Nigeria"; RPT with promoter JNK Global; balance scope "possibility" in "two to three months".

5. **Balance-sheet support: CRISIL outlook to Stable, limits ₹457 → ₹604 Cr** — Inflection up — Strength: Medium
   Evidence: "Crisil A-/Stable (Outlook revised from 'Negative; Rating Reaffirmed)". Offset: H1 OCF ₹2.8 Cr; cash ₹6.9 Cr.

6. **Diversification push (offshore, metals & minerals; non-heater 40% target)** — New mention — Strength: Medium
   Evidence: MOA amended (new CIN Nov 16, 2026); "we want to move this non-heating segment to around 40% of our revenue"; non-heater hit rate expected only 10–15%.
