## Headline Delta

Q1 FY27 growth is strong year-on-year (Total Income "Rs 186.0 cr", +80.6% YoY; PAT "Rs 9.6 Cr", 8.5x YoY), but the quarter reset sequentially: revenue fell 46% from Q4 FY26, consolidated EBITDA margin fell to 11.8% from 15.2% (management attributes this to a Chemdist operating loss), the order book fell 8.2% to "Rs 1,801 cr", and the flagship June export order was cancelled.

## Management Tone Delta

**Demand commentary:**
- Prior (Q4 FY26): "The Company reported strong order inflows of Rs 1,694.4 Cr during the year including critical and strategic projects of cracking furnace and green hydrogen, contributing to a total order book of Rs. 1,961.4 Cr as of March 31, 2026."
- Current (Q1 FY27, Aug 12 deck): "Our order book as on June 30, 2026 is Rs 1,801 cr, and our growth visibility remains strong, with a bidding pipeline of Rs. ~6,000 cr across domestic and international markets."
- Delta: **Stable on wording, Deteriorating on numbers** — order book down 8.2% QoQ while management language stays "strong". Pipeline is described as "Rs. ~6,000 cr" with a "balanced 50:50" split. Signal Strength: **High** (numbers contradict the tone).

**Margin commentary:**
- Prior (Q4 FY26): "EBITDA margin 15.2% in Q4 FY26; margin pressure flagged by analysts despite PAT surge" (extraction note; the verbatim Q4 call quote was not accessible). Q4 press release: "EBITDA totalled Rs. 52.3 Cr, up 89.9% YoY, with a margin expansion of 165 bps to 15.2%."
- Current (Aug 12 deck): "Importantly, we have maintained an EBITDA margin of 11.8% in Q1FY27, representing our ability to deliver strong growth while maintaining a stable margin profile."
- Current (Aug 19 transcript): "as the business scales up through the upcoming quarters, we expect the operating leverage to improve meaningfully." and "Chemdist breakeven: ... by year-end, we should be able to get it into the green."
- Delta: **Deteriorating** (QoQ) with management expecting recovery — "stable margin profile" wording does not match the 340 bps QoQ fall. Signal Strength: **High**.

**Guidance:**
- Prior (Q4 FY26, as recorded in the prior delta and the Q4 call extraction; not verbatim): FY27 revenue growth 25–30%; EBITDA margin 14–15%. The prior analysis (delta-2026-june22-update.md) recorded these as "maintained".
- Current (Aug 14 analyst-meet deck, slide 10): "On track for EBITDA margin guidance of 14 -15% and revenue growth of 25-30% for FY27."
- Current (Aug 19 transcript, call held Aug 12): "Our revenue growth guidance of around 20% to 25% remains intact. We also maintain our full year EBITDA margin guidance of about 12% to 14%."
- Delta: **Lowered in the transcript; Maintained in the Aug 14 slide. Source conflict unresolved.** The two statements are six days apart and use different ranges. The company's own words in the transcript ("remains intact") imply no change, so the apparent reset (25–30% to 20–25%; 14–15% to 12–14%) is not confirmed as a revision. Carried forward to the Q2 file, where the Nov 6 release again states 20–25% / 12–14%.

## Source Conflicts and Caveats (carried through, not resolved)

1. **FY27 guidance.** Aug 14 slide 10: 25–30% revenue / 14–15% EBITDA margin. Aug 19 transcript: 20–25% / 12–14%, "remains intact". Both recorded verbatim above. Nov 6 release (Q2 file) also states 20–25% / 12–14%.
2. **Standalone Q1 figures differ by filing.**
   - Aug 11 deck (`investor-presentation-2026-08-11.md`): standalone EBITDA margin 5.6%, standalone PAT "-0.8 Cr"; the extraction notes an "Operating Expense" row identical to the consolidated row.
   - Aug 12 revised deck (`investor-presentation-revised-2026-08-12.md`): standalone EBITDA margin 14.0%, PAT Rs 13.5 Cr. Aug 11 deck is superseded.
   - Aug 19 transcript: standalone EBITDA margin "14%".
   - Board outcome OCR (Aug 11): "Profit for the period/year (V-VI) 135.46" (INR million; OCR, partly garbled).
   - The revised deck figures are used in the tables above; the Aug 11 figures are not relied on.
3. **Q1 FY26 revenue.** Press release CEO quote reads "compared to Rs. 10.30 cr in Q1FY26"; the press-release table and both decks show 103.0. The table value is used.
4. **Revenue basis.** Q1 uses "Total Income" (includes other income). The Q2 file uses "Total operational revenue". Sequential comparisons across these bases are not like-for-like (see Q2 file).
5. **YoY comparability.** Board outcome Annexure A (OCR): "numbers pertaining to the quarters ended June 30, 2026, March 31, 2026 and year ended March 31, 2026 are not comparable with quarter ended June 30, 2025 to the extent of aforesaid acquisition accounting". Verify against PDF.
6. **New Labour Codes impact (Rs 9.22 million)** — OCR text; verify against PDF.
7. **Senior management change (Annexure C).** Two personnel designated w.e.f. Aug 11, 2026; one ceased. Names are garbled in the extraction and not transcribed.
8. **IPO proceeds (Monitoring Agency, Aug 13).** Fully utilised; "this is the final Monitoring Agency report". Delay table shows the working-capital object completion as "Fiscal 2027" against the offer-document "Fiscal 2026" proposal.
9. **June 8 order value.** Range only (Rs 100–300 cr per the June 8 extraction). Q1 filings do not state a value.
