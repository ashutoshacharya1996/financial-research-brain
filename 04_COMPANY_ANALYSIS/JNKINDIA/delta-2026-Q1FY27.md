## Headline Delta

Q1 FY27 growth was strong (consolidated revenue +80.6% YoY, EBITDA 3.1x, PAT 8.5x), but three things moved against the Q4 FY26 setup: order book fell QoQ (Rs 1,961 Cr to Rs 1,801 Cr), the first large export order (UAE/ADNOC) was cancelled, and the company's own FY27 guidance now appears in two conflicting versions (12 Aug call vs 14 Aug presentation) which this report flags and does not reconcile.

## Management Tone Delta

**Demand commentary:**
- Prior (Q4FY26 press release, 20 May 2026): "JNK India is well-positioned to capture opportunities in both traditional and sustainable energy sectors in India and abroad." Annual report (Sep): "We enter FY 2027 with a stronger platform, a healthy order book, enhanced execution capabilities and a broader opportunity landscape."
- Current (Q1FY27 press release, 11 Aug): "We are seeing a healthy opportunity set across both international and domestic markets, with increasing participation in international projects. This provides us with strong visibility for order inflows." Call: "We continue to see encouraging traction across both domestic and international markets." and "both refinery and fertilizer, the order finalization should happen in Q2, Q3."
- Delta: Stable tone, but evidence shifted from realised orders (FY26 inflows 1,694 Cr) to pipeline (>6,000 Cr) and expected Q2/Q3 finalisation. Confirmation of conversion is pending (Q1 inflow not disclosed; Sep orders only in the "Significant" band, Rs 0-100 Cr). Signal strength: Medium.

**Export / UAE order:**
- Prior (8 Jun 2026 filing, per earlier extraction): CC7 Emirates Engineering Solutions incinerator package for TA'ZIZ Salt Project, ADNOC; "first major international order". Prior delta: "validates export capability".
- Current (16 Jul filing): "the aforesaid order stands cancelled due to not receiving approval from the project licensor being a mandatory requirement for the performance and acceptance of the Goods under the Contract." Call: "The large export order received on June 8, 2026, was subsequently canceled solely due to the technical approval requirements ... Importantly, the cancellation happened at a very early stage, and we had not incurred costs against this order." Deck: "this was an isolated incident, with no material costs incurred and no execution having commenced." Filing: "This will have no impact on declared revenue and associated profit." Mgmt: "for future projects, we have received the qualification from them" (licensor not named).
- Delta: Deteriorating on the specific order (Inflection down, High); partly mitigated by licensor qualification for future projects, new licensors in USA and Malaysia (Emkay deck) and Iraq branch. Order value was never stated in the filing.

**Margin commentary:**
- Prior (Q4FY26 press release): "EBITDA totalled Rs. 52.3 Cr ... margin expansion of 165 bps to 15.2%"; FY26 13.3%; named risk in prior extraction: margin below 14-15% guidance range.
- Current (Q1FY27 press release): "Importantly, we have maintained an EBITDA margin of 11.8% in Q1FY27, representing our ability to deliver strong growth while maintaining a stable margin profile." Call: "with Chemdist being ... the first quarter and just the initial stages. So there has been a loss of ... INR 3.6 crores." Chemdist: "as the business scales up through the upcoming quarters, we expect the operating leverage to improve meaningfully." Cost: "most of the orders are already placed, and we placed the order on our sub vendor on a fixed price basis." New segments: "in the similar margin range of whatever our guided EBITDA margins are there, anywhere from 12% to 14%."
- Delta: Deteriorating vs Q4 exit rate and vs 14-15%; "maintained" framing is relative to FY26-ish levels and is not supported by the Q4 15.2% comparator. Standalone 14.0% is stable vs FY26. Signal strength: High.

**Guidance (CONFLICT — flagged, not reconciled):**
- Prior (Q4FY26, per earlier extractions, snippet-sourced, no verbatim quote): FY27 revenue growth 25-30%, EBITDA margin 14-15%.
- Current, version A — Q1FY27 earnings call, 12 Aug 2026 (transcript filed 19 Aug): "Our revenue growth guidance of around 20% to 25% remains intact. We also maintain our full year EBITDA margin guidance of about 12% to 14%." — Mr. Arvind Kamath. On analyst citing prior 14-15%: "For our understanding, what we have announced was 12% to 14%. But for some reason, we are missing something, these numbers, we'll recheck them. But there is no change from like what -- from the last 3 months or 6 months, nothing has changed on -- as far as our margin projection goes." — Mr. Dipak Bharuka.
- Current, version B — corporate presentation filed 14 Aug 2026 (Emkay conference), slide 10: "On track for EBITDA margin guidance of 14 -15% and revenue growth of 25-30% for FY27."
- Neither the Q1 press release nor the revised 12 Aug deck gives numeric guidance. The annual report extract gives none either.
- Delta: Unresolved conflict. The company has published both 20-25% / 12-14% and 25-30% / 14-15% for FY27 within two days. Management on the call said the 12-14% number needed a recheck, with no follow-up correction in the extracted corpus. No determination is made here of which is current. Signal strength: High (guidance integrity). Downstream users should treat FY27 guidance as unknown within these ranges until a company clarification appears.

**Working capital / funding:**
- Prior: Annual report / Emkay deck history cites "Constrained working capital and bank limits, impeding ability to bid for larger projects" (pre-2024).
- Current: "we should be able to manage the working capital for quite some time now"; but also "debt raising, I mean, yes, absolutely. I think that's a fair conclusion. But from the contract receipt point of view, we might need a bank guarantee limit enhancement basically." CRISIL facilities enhanced to Rs 604 Cr.
- Delta: Mixed; limit enhancement achieved on rating side, bank guarantee needs flagged by mgmt. Signal: Medium.
