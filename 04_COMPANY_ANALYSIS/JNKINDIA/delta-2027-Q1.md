## Headline Delta

Q1 FY27 growth accelerated (Total Income +80.6% YoY vs +69.2% in Q4 FY26) but EBITDA margin fell to 11.8% from 15.2% in Q4 FY26, the order book fell to Rs 1,801 cr from Rs 1,961.4 cr, the June 8 UAE export order (Rs 100-300 cr) was cancelled on 16 Jul 2026, and FY27 guidance is stated two different ways in the company's own documents (call: 20-25% / 12-14%; Aug 14 deck: 25-30% / 14-15%), which management said it would "recheck".

## Management Tone Delta

**Demand commentary:**
- Prior (Q4 FY26 press release, Arvind Kamath, 20 May 2026): "JNK India is well-positioned to capture opportunities in both traditional and sustainable energy sectors in India and abroad." Prior (Q4 FY26 call file, snippets, not verbatim): "Strong execution of major projects for refinery and petrochemical clients; emerging green hydrogen orders; 97.5% of order book from Indian customers".
- Current (Q1 FY27 call): "Our current overall opportunity pipeline is more than INR 6,000 crores with a broadly balanced 50:50% mix between international and domestic opportunities." Also "Africa remains an important market for us, particularly countries such as Nigeria and Ethiopia", and "the order finalization should happen in Q2, Q3" (refinery and fertilizer). Dangote: "Phase 2 is still under the discussion, and we stand a good chance of getting it."
- Current (press release): "We are seeing a healthy opportunity set across both international and domestic markets, with increasing participation in international projects."
- Delta: Mixed. Pipeline language broadened and more specific (Improving) while realised orders weakened (order book down; export order cancelled; Q1 inflows not given). Signal strength: Medium.

**Margin commentary:**
- Prior (Q4 FY26 press release, Arvind Kamath): "EBITDA totalled Rs. 52.3 Cr, up 89.9% YoY, with a margin expansion of 165 bps to 15.2%." Named risk in that file: "Operating Profit margin (EBITDA at 13.3%) below guidance midpoint of 14-15% for FY26".
- Current (press release): "Importantly, we have maintained an EBITDA margin of 11.8% in Q1FY27, representing our ability to deliver strong growth while maintaining a stable margin profile." Call: "as the business scales up through the upcoming quarters, we expect the operating leverage to improve meaningfully." Pricing: "most of the orders are already placed, and we placed the order on our sub vendor on a fixed price basis." New business: "in the similar margin range of whatever our guided EBITDA margins are there, anywhere from 12% to 14% in that range."
- Delta: Deteriorating sequentially (15.2% to 11.8%), "maintained" framing is YoY-based; margin outlook language shifted toward back-end loading ("upcoming quarters"). Signal strength: High.

**Guidance:**
- Prior (Q4 FY26 call file / Aug-14-consistent): "Revenue growth of 25-30% guided for FY27. EBITDA margins guided at 14-15% for FY27." (file notes: "No verbatim management quote confirmed.")
- Current (call, Chairperson Arvind Kamath): "Our revenue growth guidance of around 20% to 25% remains intact. We also maintain our full year EBITDA margin guidance of about 12% to 14%."
- Current (Aug 14 Emkay deck, slide 10): "On track for EBITDA margin guidance of 14 -15% and revenue growth of 25-30%".
- Analyst on call (Kamlesh Bagmar): "we were guiding roughly around 14% to 15% margin. So now we are saying 12% to 14%." Management (Dipak Bharuka): "what we have announced was 12% to 14%. But for some reason, we are missing something, these numbers, we'll recheck them. But there is no change from like what -- from the last 3 months or 6 months, nothing has changed on -- as far as our margin projection goes."
- Delta: UNRESOLVED / CONFLICTING. Cannot be classified as Maintained or Lowered from the extracted files. Taken at face value the call figures are lower than the prior-period figures on both revenue (20-25% vs 25-30%) and margin (12-14% vs 14-15%); the Aug 14 deck repeats the prior figures. No later corrective filing exists in the extracted files through 2026-10-25. Signal strength: High (guidance figures are concrete numbers).

## Conflicts and Superseded Items (Unresolved)

1. **FY27 guidance.** Call: revenue 20-25%, EBITDA margin 12-14%. Aug 14 Emkay deck: revenue 25-30%, EBITDA margin 14-15%. Prior-period (Q4 FY26 call file, secondary sources): 25-30% and 14-15%. Management on call asserts "there is no change" while saying "we'll recheck them". No correcting filing in the extracted files. Not resolved here.
2. **Standalone Q1 FY27 P&L.** Original deck (2026-08-11): standalone EBITDA Rs 9.5 Cr (5.6%), PAT Rs -0.8 Cr. Revised deck (2026-08-12): EBITDA Rs 23.8 Cr (14.0%), PAT Rs 13.5 Cr. The original file notes its operating expense line equals the consolidated figure in the revised deck. Consolidated figures are identical across all decks. Board-outcome results tables are garbled so cannot adjudicate. Not resolved.
3. **UAE order status.** 8 Jun 2026 filing: order Rs 100-300 cr, completion Dec 2027 (prior delta files treated it as a confirmed addition to the order book). 16 Jul 2026 filing: "the aforesaid order stands cancelled". Prior delta wording ("no longer a forward indicator — it is a confirmed regulatory filing") is superseded. Reason wording varies: counterparty "not receiving approval from the project licensor"; call "solely due to the technical approval requirements"; decks "inability to secure the requisite technical approval from the licensor". Whether the Rs 1,801 Cr order book includes or excludes the order is not stated. Order value was never disclosed beyond the range. A replacement order is not mentioned ("Any statement on re-tendering or replacement of the order" is listed as missing).
4. **Press-release typo.** Press release text: "compared to Rs. 10.30 cr in Q1FY26"; its table and decks show Rs 103.0 Cr. Table value used.
5. **Unbilled revenue.** "around INR 200 crores" vs "around INR 200 crores to INR 221 crores".
6. **Q4 FY26 baseline quality.** Q4 FY26 earnings-call file has no verbatim management quotes (403 on source); Q4 guidance and "97.5% domestic" come from snippets; prior "Operating Profit" (Rs 212.3 Cr, 25.3%) uses a different definition from EBITDA. Margin comparisons are therefore indicative.
7. **Record date.** Record-date notice table shows "September 18, 2026" while register closure is Sep 19-25, 2026 (administrative; no signal).
8. **QRSAM.** No QRSAM award or reference exists in JNKINDIA extracted files; the unresolved QRSAM item in the repository belongs to BEL. Not attributed here.
