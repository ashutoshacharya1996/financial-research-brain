## Headline Delta

Growth stalled in Q2 FY27: total operational revenue was "Rs 187.5 Cr", +3.4% YoY against +80.6% in Q1, PAT rose only 2.0% YoY, and the order book fell 7.7% QoQ to "Rs. 1,662 crores" (15.3% below March). Guidance appears reset lower (14–15% / 25–30% in the Aug 14 slide vs 12–14% / 20–25% in the Nov 6 release), with a source conflict still open. Post-quarter order intake (Sept 28 flare orders, about Rs 30 cr; Nov 3 related-party Nigeria heater order, about Rs 500 cr per the deck) is the main counter-signal.

## Management Tone Delta

**Demand commentary:**
- Prior (Q1 FY27, Aug 12 deck): "Our order book as on June 30, 2026 is Rs 1,801 cr, and our growth visibility remains strong, with a bidding pipeline of Rs. ~6,000 cr across domestic and international markets."
- Current (Q2 FY27, Nov 6 release): "Beyond our existing order book, we continue to see healthy traction across opportunities, with an order pipeline of around Rs. 5,000 Crores (excluding recent order wins)."
- Delta: **Deteriorating** — pipeline down from "~6,000 cr" to "around Rs. 5,000 Crores", though the Q2 figure excludes recent wins and the Q1 basis is not stated, so the comparison is not like-for-like. Signal Strength: **Medium**.

**Margin commentary:**
- Prior (Q1 FY27, Aug 12 deck): "Importantly, we have maintained an EBITDA margin of 11.8% in Q1FY27, representing our ability to deliver strong growth while maintaining a stable margin profile."
- Current (Q2 FY27, Nov 6 release): "despite temporary execution challenges during Q2 arising from shipment delays and monsoon related disruption."
- Delta: **Deteriorating (cautious)**. Management moves from "stable margin profile" to naming execution problems. Signal Strength: **Medium** (no quantified impact given).

**Guidance:**
- Prior (Q1 FY27): Aug 14 deck, slide 10: "On track for EBITDA margin guidance of 14 -15% and revenue growth of 25-30% for FY27." Aug 19 transcript: "Our revenue growth guidance of around 20% to 25% remains intact. We also maintain our full year EBITDA margin guidance of about 12% to 14%."
- Current (Q2 FY27, Nov 6 release): "we remain confident in our growth outlook and continue to maintain our FY27 revenue growth guidance of 20–25% and EBITDA margin# guidance of 12–14%." Footnote: "#based on Total Operational Revenue."
- Delta: **Maintained at 20–25% / 12–14%** versus the Aug 19 transcript, but **Lowered** versus the Aug 14 slide (25–30% / 14–15%). Because the Aug 14 slide and the Aug 19 transcript disagree and the company has used "maintain" language throughout, the revision is recorded as **apparent, not confirmed**. Signal Strength: **High** on the numbers, unresolved on the direction.
- Seasonality (Nov 6 release): "H2 historically contributing around 60–70% of our annual revenue, driven by progress in ongoing projects and anticipated order conversions."

**Capacity / expansion commentary:**
- Prior (Q1 FY27): "We are entering the off-shore, metals & minerals and focusing more on renewable energy."
- Current (Q2 FY27): "Alongside strengthening our core business, we are also focused on expanding into new and adjacent opportunities across renewable energy and related industries."
- Delta: **Stable** in direction; the offshore and metals & minerals references from Q1 are not repeated. Signal Strength: **Low**.

## Source Conflicts and Caveats (carried through, not resolved)

1. **FY27 guidance.** Aug 14 slide 10: 25–30% / 14–15%. Aug 19 transcript and Nov 6 release: 20–25% / 12–14%. Nov 6 wording is "maintain"; Aug 19 wording is "remains intact". Not reconciled.
2. **Revenue basis (Q1 vs Q2).** Q1 uses "Total Income" (Rs 186.0 Cr). Q2 uses "Total operational revenue" (Rs 187.5 Cr). The H1 total is Rs 370.2 Cr, which is Rs 3.3 Cr below the sum of the two reported quarters (Rs 373.5 Cr). The Q1 EBITDA gap runs the same way: H1 EBITDA Rs 39.4 Cr against a Q1 + Q2 sum of Rs 42.7 Cr (Q1 "incl. other income"). Both gaps are about Rs 3.3 Cr, which is consistent with Q1 other income of about Rs 3.3 Cr. This is inference from the numbers; no source states it.
   - Derived like-for-like Q1 operational revenue: Rs 370.2 − 187.5 = Rs 182.7 Cr. Derived Q1 operational EBITDA: Rs 39.4 − 20.8 = Rs 18.6 Cr, a 10.2% margin on Rs 182.7 Cr. On this basis Q2's 11.1% margin is up about 90 bps QoQ, not down 70 bps as reported.
   - Both views are shown. The reported figures are used in the tables; the derived ones are flagged.
3. **H1 revenue growth.** Table: 30.4%. CEO quote: "31.2% YoY growth in total operational revenue". Both recorded.
4. **Standalone Q1 margin.** Revised Aug 12 deck: standalone EBITDA margin 14.0%. Q2 deck: H1 standalone EBITDA Rs 38.4 Cr and Q2 Rs 18.2 Cr imply Q1 standalone EBITDA of about Rs 20.2 Cr; against standalone revenue from operations of about Rs 163.6 Cr (H1 Rs 335.3 Cr less Q2 Rs 171.7 Cr), that is about 12.3%. Not reconciled to the 14.0% in the Q1 deck; both recorded. The Q2 deck's standalone 10.4% is used in the table.
5. **Order-flow basis.** Q2 Sept-30 order book is stated; the June 8 export order's presence in the Jun-30 book is not stated. Derived intake (~Rs 48.5 Cr) is therefore not reliable.
6. **Flare/incinerator orders.** Release: "orders from an Indian customer for Flares and Incinerators". Sept 29 filing and Q2 deck: flare packages only.
7. **Chart labels.** Deck chart label "Nov-27*" (footnote "*Till 5th November'26") appears to be a typo for Nov-26; recorded as printed.
8. **Q4 FY26 standalone and FY26 guidance.** Prior-period guidance (25–30% / 14–15%) was recorded in the June analyses from search snippets, not verbatim; the Aug 14 slide is the first verbatim source for those figures.
