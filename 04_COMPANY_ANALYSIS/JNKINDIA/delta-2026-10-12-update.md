## Headline Delta

The June thesis anchor, the first export order (UAE / ADNOC TA'ZIZ), was cancelled on 16 Jul 2026 (licensor approval not received), the order book fell from Rs 1,961 cr to Rs 1,801 cr, and management guidance is now stated two different ways (25-30% growth / 14-15% margin in the 14 Aug deck vs 20-25% / 12-14% on the call), even as consolidated revenue (+80.6% YoY) and PAT (8.5x) kept growing strongly.

## Management Tone Delta

**Demand commentary:**
- Prior (Q4 FY26 press release, 20 May 2026): "JNK India is well-positioned to capture opportunities in both traditional and sustainable energy sectors in India and abroad." And "strong order inflows of Rs 1,694.4 Cr during the year".
- Current (11 Aug press release): "We are seeing a healthy opportunity set across both international and domestic markets, with increasing participation in international projects. This provides us with strong visibility for order inflows." Call: "Our current overall opportunity pipeline is more than INR 6,000 crores with a broadly balanced 50:50% mix between international and domestic opportunities."
- Delta: Tone broadly Stable-to-positive, but evidence shifted from realised inflows to pipeline. Hit rates disclosed: "20% to 25%" traditional and "anything around 10% to 12%, not more than that" in new segments. At those rates, Rs 6,000 cr of pipeline would imply a few hundred crore to ~Rs 1,500 cr of wins; this is an illustrative reading, not company guidance. Timing claim: "we expect all this pipeline should get finalized in this financial year." Strength: Medium (language intact, hard inflow evidence weaker).

**Margin commentary:**
- Prior (20 May): Q4 "EBITDA totalled Rs. 52.3 Cr, up 89.9% YoY, with a margin expansion of 165 bps to 15.2%"; FY26 13.3%.
- Current (11 Aug): "Importantly, we have maintained an EBITDA margin of 11.8% in Q1FY27, representing our ability to deliver strong growth while maintaining a stable margin profile." Call: "for JNK India alone, we have achieved an EBITDA of 14%, which is as per the guideline". New-business margins: "anywhere from 12% to 14% in that range."
- Delta: Consolidated margin below the Q4 exit rate and below the 14-15% FY27 guidance; framed by management as "stable". The June read of "Inflection up, 14-15% achievable" is not confirmed on a consolidated basis in Q1. Strength: Medium. Commodity: "most of the orders are already placed, and we placed the order on our sub vendor on a fixed price basis."

**Guidance:**
- Prior (May call, per Q4 extraction): FY27 revenue growth 25-30%, EBITDA margin 14-15%. Maintained in June deltas.
- Current: TWO CONFLICTING STATEMENTS.
  - Emkay corporate presentation (14 Aug): "On track for EBITDA margin guidance of 14 -15% and revenue growth of 25-30% for FY27".
  - Earnings call (transcript dated 19 Aug in file, call held 12 Aug): "Our revenue growth guidance of around 20% to 25% remains intact." and "We also maintain our full year EBITDA margin guidance of about 12% to 14%."
  - Q&A: analyst: "we were guiding roughly around 14% to 15% margin. So now we are saying 12% to 14%." Management: "For our understanding, what we have announced was 12% to 14%. But for some reason, we are missing something, these numbers, we'll recheck them."
- Delta: UNRESOLVED. If the call figures stand, guidance is lowered on both revenue (by ~5 pts) and margin (by ~2 pts at both ends of the range); if the deck stands, maintained. Management itself said it would "recheck". Both press release and investor decks state no guidance. Do not treat either as final until the company clarifies. Strength: High (conflict itself is the signal).

## New Mentions This Period

- Order cancellation: "The large export order received on June 8, 2026, was subsequently canceled solely due to the technical approval requirements." Filing (16 Jul): "due to not receiving approval from the project licensor being a mandatory requirement for the performance and acceptance of the Goods under the Contract. Consequently, the aforesaid order stands terminated." Also: "This will have no impact on declared revenue and associated profit."
- Offshore and metals and minerals entry: "We are entering the off-shore, metals & minerals and focusing more on renewable energy." Object clause amendment (Annexure B.2) "Opens new revenue streams by enabling the Company to bid for and execute large-scale, turnkey industrial projects in the heavy industries sector (steel, mining, cement etc)." Subject to shareholder and statutory approval.
- Iraq branch office: "Subject to the necessary approval, setting up of Branch Office of the Company overseas in the Republic of Iraq." Call: "this will be more of a registered paper office."
- Bidding pipeline Rs ~6,000 cr, 50:50 domestic/export, ~60% heating equipment.
- Litigation at JNK Global level: "the matter is sub judice, we would not like to comment on that as of now." (JNK Global is the Korean partner and the export order conduit.)
- Revenue recognition: call says "it's whatever now we are going on the input method"; Emkay deck says method "updated for the shift toward large, project-based orders, resulting in a more uniform margin profile".
- Average project ticket size "increased to Rs. 500-1,000 Cr" (Emkay deck).
- Senior management personnel changes (Annexure C): M. Pravin Pulujkar and Mr. Prasad Phatak designated Senior Management Personnel w.e.f. 11 Aug 2026; Mr. Ravikumar Mudali Vallathur ceased to be designated SMP (employment continues).
- New Labour Codes: impact "of Rs. 9.22 million on standalone financial results under Employee Benefit Expense" (OCR).
- Crisil rating action (above). Flare package orders (above). Investor meets: Emkay conference 14 Aug (Mumbai); in-person 1x1 meetings 22 Sep (Mumbai). 16th AGM notice and FY26 annual report dispatched (letter 4 Sep; AGM date not stated).
