## Headline Delta

Execution is accelerating (revenue +25% then +26.5% YoY; PAT growth up from +8% in Q1 to +20% in Q2) while the order book has fallen two quarters running (Rs 73,882 crore on 1 Apr to Rs 68,717 crore on 1 Oct) and the Rs 30,000 crore QRSAM order has slipped again; guidance was left unchanged, with Rs 55,000 crore FY27 inflow now needing roughly Rs 47,700 crore (computed) in H2.

## Management Tone Delta

**Demand commentary:**
- Prior (Q4 FY26, May): "QRSAM order expected Jun–Jul 2026"; pipeline QRSAM, P-75I, AMCA, data centre solutions (extraction snippets).
- Q1 call (27 Jul): "maximum by September we will get and I am still sticking to that." "Just CCS only is waiting for all of us and hopefully, when CCS meets, I think they will clear QRSAM."
- Current (Q2 call, 3 Nov): "Hopefully, in next CCS, they may clear. They are clearing one by one all the big projects." and "more than 99%, I am confident it is coming to us very soon." CMD also: "internally, we also knew it may be minimum August, September only. So it has not slipped that much."
- Delta: **Mixed** — the commitment date slipped (Jun–Jul to "maximum by September" to "next CCS") while confidence language rose and NGC was cleared. Signal strength: High. No QRSAM contract filing exists in the evidence through 15 Nov 2026.
- Other demand deltas: NGC (Next Generation Corvette) "cleared", ~Rs 20,000 crore of buyer-nominated electronics expected "in a staggered way", "99%" within FY27 (new, positive). P-75I moved from Q1 call "at least 100% probability of having at least one of them [NGC/P-75I]" to Q2 "maybe P-75I may slip to next year" (negative). AMCA bid "already submitted" (RFP date was 27 Aug); L1 outcome "hopefully, before March". Shatrughat/Samaghat (~Rs 9,000+ crore), HAMMER (~Rs 2,500+ crore), Shakti Ph4 (~Rs 2,000 crore) all still "before March".

**Margin commentary:**
- Prior (Q4 FY26): FY27 EBITDA guided >28%; FY26 ~29%.
- Q1 call: Q1 25.83%; "We have variability of 25% to 31% EBITDA margins based on the product mix in quarter-to-quarter etcetera, but at the year end, let me again assure you, we will cross 28%."
- Current (Q2): H1 27.43%; "EBITDA margins of around 28%." Margin not a focus of analyst questions; on nomination vs competitive bids "more or less similar margins will be there."
- Delta: **Stable guide, improving realised** — Medium. Wage revision from 1 Jan 2027 flagged in Q1 (3 months' provision in FY27); CFO expects employee cost at ~12% of turnover.

**Guidance:**
- Prior (Q4 FY26): revenue growth >15%; EBITDA >28%; order inflow >Rs 55,000 crore including QRSAM; capex >Rs 1,200 crore; R&D Rs 2,200 crore.
- Current (Q2 call closing): "revenue growth of 15-plus percent ... EBITDA margins of around 28%, the order inflow of INR25,000 crores minimum, excluding QRSAM and including QRSAM, INR50,000-plus crores. R&D investment of INR2,200-plus crores, capex of around INR1,200 crores." In Q&A: "guidance point of view, we still want to stick to INR25,000 and INR55,000 crores only." Asked about a revenue upgrade given 26% H1 growth: "No." FY26 Annual Report: "targeting a healthy growth of 15-20% in the financial year 2026-27."
- Delta: **Maintained** — no upgrade despite H1 +26%. Wording flag: closing remark says "INR50,000-plus crores" incl. QRSAM vs Rs 55,000 crore elsewhere on the same call; Q1 call closing remarks also contained errors (EBITDA "21% to 23%", inflow ">INR20,000 crores"). Treated as transcript noise; management says guidance unchanged.
