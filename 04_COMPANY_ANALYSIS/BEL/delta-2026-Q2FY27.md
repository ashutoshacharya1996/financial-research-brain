## Headline Delta

## Management Tone Delta

**Demand commentary:**
- Prior (Q1 FY27 call, Jul 27): "maximum by September we will get and I am still sticking to that." / "we are just waiting for the CCS approval and that time we will get the good news about QRSAM."
- Current (Q2 FY27 call, Nov 3): "So, as such, and based on our inputs also, order is expected on us very shortly. Hopefully, in next CCS, they may clear." / "So, this program, as of now and more than 99%, I am confident it is coming to us very soon."
- Delta: **Deteriorating on timing; conviction language stronger.** The September window passed without an award. The wording moved from a date ("maximum by September") to "very shortly" and "hopefully, in next CCS". Probability language rose to "more than 99%". Signal strength: High.
- Platform orders (prior): "at least 100% probability of having at least one of them and more than 50% probability of getting both of them."
- Platform orders (current): NGC "around INR20,000 crores to be worth of orders we are expecting in a staggered way, but all of them, 99%, will come in this financial year." P-75I: "maybe P-75I may slip to next year."
- Delta: **Mixed.** NGC confidence **Strengthening** (High). P-75I **Deteriorating** (High).

**Margin commentary:**
- Prior: "We are telling it is due to product mix variation, not due to input cost." / "Quarter-on-quarter, there could be variations because of the composition of product mix."
- Current: "Whether it is nomination or competition, more or less similar margins will be there, okay? In nomination, we don't get higher margins." / "that only differentiates us from others" (on indigenisation and unique product content).
- Delta: **Stable on margin structure; new explicit statement** that nomination orders carry no margin premium. Relevant because the June 2026 file describes QRSAM as nomination-based ("BEL was a nominated production agency"). Signal strength: Medium.

**Guidance:**
- Prior (Q1): FY27 inflow ">₹55,000 cr including QRSAM... we are sticking to that"; EBITDA "we remain at that" (28%). Revenue growth >15% (Q4).
- Current: Revenue growth "between 15% to 20%, more than 15% growth"; inflow "INR25,000 crores without QRSAM and with QRSAM INR55,000 crores ... we are sticking to that"; "Whatsoever guidance we have given to you at the year start, we are going to meet or exceed that." EBITDA not restated in reviewed pages.
- Delta: **Maintained**, with the revenue range articulated. Signal strength: Medium.

## Key Signals Summary

1. **Order book fell for a second straight quarter** — Inflection down — Strength: High
   Evidence: "The order book position as on 01/10/2026 is INR68,717 crores" vs ₹72,258 cr (Jul 1) and ₹73,882 cr (Apr 1). Down ₹3,541 cr (-4.9%) QoQ. Derived book-to-bill ~0.50.

2. **QRSAM moved past management's "maximum by September" window** — Inflection down (timing) — Strength: High
   Evidence: "maximum by September we will get and I am still sticking to that." (Jul 27) → "order is expected on us very shortly. Hopefully, in next CCS, they may clear." (Nov 3). Prior June file flagged that "next slip (if any) to August-September would raise concern."

3. **P-75I moved to next year; NGC firmed up** — Inflection down (P-75I) / Strengthening (NGC) — Strength: High
   Evidence: "maybe P-75I may slip to next year." vs Q1 "one of these two definitely we may go through". NGC: "around INR20,000 crores ... all of them, 99%, will come in this financial year."

4. **PAT growth accelerated to +19.8% YoY in Q2 (derived) from +8.2% in Q1** — Acceleration — Strength: High
   Evidence: "The profit after tax has increased to INR2,589 crores up to Q2 as compared to INR2,255 crores previous year" (H1 +14.81%); Q2 "Rs.1541 Crore as against Rs.1286 Crore" (derived +19.8%).

5. **Implied Q2 EBITDA margin rebound to ~28.6% (derived)** — Inflection up — Strength: Medium
   Evidence: "The EBITDA up to Q2 is 27.43%." (H1) vs "The EBITDA margin up to Q1 is 25.83%." (Q1). Approximate; a Q2 figure was not stated.

6. **Management reaffirms ₹55,000 cr FY27 inflow with explicit ₹25,000 cr ex-QRSAM split** — Maintained — Strength: High
   Evidence: "total INR25,000 crores without QRSAM and with QRSAM INR55,000 crores, which was our guidance, we are sticking to that." Implied QRSAM component (derived) ~₹30,000 cr, consistent with the Q1 management figure.

7. **Nomination orders carry no margin premium** — Neutral (margin structure) — Strength: Medium
   Evidence: "In nomination, we don't get higher margins."
