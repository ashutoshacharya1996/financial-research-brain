## Headline Delta

BEL's order book shrank in Q1 FY27 (Rs 72,258 Cr on 1 Jul 2026 vs Rs 73,882 Cr on 1 Apr 2026) on only Rs 3,754 Cr of quarterly inflow, QRSAM slipped again (from "July 2026" to "maximum by September") and no extracted file shows it signed since, and the call transcript carries two irreconcilable sets of FY27 guidance (28% EBITDA / Rs 55,000 Cr vs 21-23% EBITDA / >Rs 20,000 Cr) that this report flags and does not reconcile.

## Key Facts Surfaced (as requested)

### 1. Order book fell
- Prior: Rs 73,882 Cr as on 1 April 2026. "Our total order book of ` 73,882 Crore as on 1 April 2026 continues to be healthy" (annual-report-2026-08-05.md).
- Current: Rs 72,258 Cr as on 1 July 2026. "The order book position of the company as on 1st July, 2026 stood at Rs.72,258 Crore." (results-board-outcome-2026-07-27.md); also "The order book position as on 1st July 2026 is INR72,258 crores" (earnings call).
- Change: -Rs 1,624 Cr (about -2.2%) QoQ. Delta type: **Inflection down** (book was Rs 73,882 Cr and, per the June 22 delta, was expected to jump on QRSAM; it contracted instead). Strength: **High** (concrete numbers).
- Mechanics, from stated figures only: Q1 revenue Rs 5,533 Cr vs Q1 inflow Rs 3,754 Cr, i.e. inflow was about 0.68x revenue. Roll-forward 73,882 + 3,754 - 5,533 = 72,103, vs reported 72,258 (about Rs 155 Cr gap; my arithmetic, not stated by the company; may reflect order-value adjustments, not explained in the extracted files).
- The 1 Apr figure is attributed in the board-outcome extraction to the Integrated Annual Report, not to the results filing.

### 2. Q1 order inflow Rs 3,754 Cr
- Source: "orders acquired in this quarter is INR3,754 crores." (earnings call).
- Context vs prior: FY26 inflow Rs 30,045 Cr ("Your Company received orders valued at ` 30,045 Crore during FY 2025-26.", annual report), so Q1 is about 12.5% of last year's full-year inflow and about 6.8% of the Rs 55,000 Cr FY27 guidance figure. No Q1 FY26 inflow comparison is in the extracted files (Missing).
- Management framing: "last year ... one or two orders we got in the week of April ... our first quarter orders were very good, but actually, that was more of a spillover of the January to March quarter." and "we don't publish quarter-by-quarter targets for this."
- Ambiguous quote, flagged: "this year whatsoever we have received, INR307 plus crores it is what was we planned, that only. So, as such, there is nothing to worry." The "INR307 plus crores" figure does not match 3,754 and its meaning is not explained in the transcript extract (possible transcription error). Not interpreted here.
- Delta type: **Deceleration/weak start vs guided run-rate**, Strength: **High**.
- Post-quarter order-win filings (all aggregate "additional orders since last disclosure"): Rs 572 Cr (13 Jul, covering 22 Jun to 13 Jul, so partly inside Q1 and possibly already in the 3,754), Rs 847 Cr (31 Jul), Rs 541 Cr (10 Aug), Rs 730 Cr (26 Aug), Rs 648 Cr (17 Sep), Rs 533 Cr (13 Oct). Sum of the five disclosures dated 31 Jul to 13 Oct = Rs 3,299 Cr; including 13 Jul = Rs 3,871 Cr. These are small, base-business orders (communication equipment, avionics, jammers, etc.). None is a large platform order. Overlap with Q1 inflow cannot be fully resolved from extracted data.

### 3. QRSAM slipped: July -> "maximum by September"; signed since?

- June 19 investor meet: "QRSAM deal is expected to be officially signed by July 2026, with initial product deliveries commencing within 18 months" (investor-meet-2026-06-19.md). June 22 delta already flagged "next slip (if any) to August-September would raise concern."

Just CCS only is waiting for all of us and hopefully, when CCS meets, I think they will clear QRSAM."
- Delta type: **Inflection down / Weakening signal** (second slip; now explicitly gated on a CCS meeting with no date). Strength: **High**.
- **Signed since? No evidence of signing in any extracted file.** Checked: grep for "QRSAM" across 03_EXTRACTED_DATA/BEL/2026 hits only the Q1 call, Q4 call, catalyst-update-qrsam-2026-06, investor-meet-2026-06-19 and order-win-2026-05-25 (all pre-signing statements). None of the six post-call order-win filings (13 Jul to 13 Oct, max Rs 847 Cr each) is large enough or described as QRSAM. The extraction run log lists no QRSAM-related item among the 12 extracted or 20 skipped (skipped are administrative/AGM/director/trading window items by title). The September deadline has now passed (today 2026-10-19). Caveat: absence in extracted files is not proof it was not signed; the extraction scope was records discovered after 2026-06-23 with status pending_extraction (32 records), and an unextracted/uncollected filing could exist. Treat as "unconfirmed / apparently not signed".
- Related inconsistency in QRSAM sizing: June files say Rs 30,000 Cr total with BEL share about Rs 21,000 Cr (70%); the Q1 call says "total INR55,000 crores, out of that INR30,000 crores roughly will be QRSAM". Not reconciled.

So, we are not foreseeing any problem in getting these order inflow for this year."
- Delta: **Deteriorating on timing, unchanged on confidence** - Strength: High (a dated commitment slipped; no new dated commitment other than "3 to 6 months" for Shatrughat/Samaghat).

**Margin commentary:**
- Prior: "EBITDA margin 29% maintained across FY26"; FY27 ">28%" guided; brokerages "cautiously optimistic" (Q4 snippets).
- Current: "Quarter-on-quarter, there could be variations because of the composition of product mix." and "Overall on a yearly basis, we maintain the EBITDA margin of 28%". Q1 actual 25.83%.
- Delta: **Deteriorating in print, maintained in words** - Strength: High. Contradicted by the closing-remarks "21% to 23%" (see Key Fact 4).

**Guidance:**
- Prior: FY27 order inflow >Rs 55,000 Cr incl. QRSAM; revenue >15%; EBITDA >28%; R&D Rs 2,200 Cr; capex >Rs 1,200 Cr.
- Current: Each of those restated on the call, plus the conflicting closing-remarks set (21-23% EBITDA; >Rs 20,000 Cr inflow).
- Delta: **Maintained (nominally) / internally inconsistent - unresolved**. Not classed as Raised or Lowered because the transcript supports both readings.
