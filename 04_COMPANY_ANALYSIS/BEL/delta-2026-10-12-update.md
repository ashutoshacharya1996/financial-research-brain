## Headline Delta

Q1 FY27 delivered a revenue acceleration (+25.27% YoY vs the 15% guide) but the profit line and the guidance narrative softened: EBITDA margin was 25.83% (vs ~29% FY26), PAT grew only 8.17%, order book slipped to Rs.72,258 Cr (from Rs.73,882 Cr), and the June-era QRSAM "July signing" catalyst did NOT happen. Management now says "maximum by September", still awaiting CCS, and no QRSAM signing appears in any extracted event through 30 Sep 2026.

## Management Tone Delta

**Demand commentary:**
- Prior (June 19 investor meet, secondary source): "QRSAM deal is expected to be officially signed by July 2026, with initial product deliveries commencing within 18 months"
- Current (Q1 call, CMD): "we were highly optimistic to get it by March, but procedural delays we knew, that is why we told it may go to Q1/Q2, maximum by September we will get and I am still sticking to that." and "Just CCS only is waiting for all of us and hopefully, when CCS meets, I think they will clear QRSAM."
- Delta: Deteriorating on timing (second slip: Feb/Mar -> July -> "maximum by September"), but intact on commitment. Strength: High.
- Additional: management gave named FY27 order candidates: "Shatrughat and Samaghat ... INR9,000 plus crores", "Shakti ... around INR2,000 crores", "HAMMER ... INR2,500 plus crores" ("these three orders we are going to get in this financial year itself"). Naval: "at least 100% probability of having at least one of them [NGC / P75I] and more than 50% probability of getting both". Improving breadth, Strength: Medium (no dates, government approvals pending).

**Margin commentary:**
- Prior (Q4 FY26 baseline): ">28% EBITDA FY27 guided"; brokers "cautiously optimistic" on margin sustainability
- Current (CMD): "We have variability of 25% to 31% EBITDA margins based on the product mix in quarter-to-quarter etcetera, but at the year end, let me again assure you, we will cross 28%." CFO: "We are telling it is due to product mix variation, not due to input cost."
- Delta: Stable guidance, weaker print. Q1 at 25.83% requires roughly 29%+ in the remaining quarters to cross 28% for the year. Strength: Medium. (Closing remarks stated 21-23%, see conflicts.)

**Guidance:**
- Prior: FY27 order inflow >Rs.55,000 Cr; revenue growth >15%; EBITDA >28%; R&D Rs.2,200 Cr; capex >Rs.1,200 Cr
- Current: Q&A: "that is INR55,000 plus crores, including QRSAM. So, we are sticking to that"; revenue "target is 15%"; "we maintain the EBITDA margin of 28%"; R&D and capex unchanged. CMD composition: "INR30,000 crores roughly will be QRSAM, then around INR15,000 crores plus is these platform orders, and remaining will be the other base orders". Closing remarks, as extracted, say: "EBITDA margin 21% to 23%, order inflow of more than INR20,000 crores including QRSAM".
- Delta: **Maintained** on the Q&A figures; the closing-remarks figures conflict and are unreconciled (see Flags). New: defence/non-defence mix stated at 90:10.

   Evidence: "maximum by September we will get and I am still sticking to that"; "Just CCS only is waiting for all of us". June claim was July signing.

2. **Revenue +25.27% but PAT +8.17%; EBITDA 25.83% vs ~29% FY26** - Acceleration (revenue) / Inflection down (margin) - Strength: High
   Evidence: "growth of 25.27%" vs "PAT ... growth of 8.17%"; "The EBITDA margin up to Q1 is 25.83%." Mgmt attributes to mix, not cost; year-end ">28%" now needs a stronger back-half mix.

3. **Order book shrank and Q1 inflow was light** - Inflection down - Strength: High
   Evidence: Rs.72,258 Cr (1 Jul) vs Rs.73,882 Cr (1 Apr); Q1 inflow Rs.3,754 Cr vs Rs.55,000 Cr annual guide. Q2-to-date small orders (~Rs.3,338 Cr) do not change the picture; the guide is back-end loaded on QRSAM.

   Evidence: Shatrughat/Samaghat ~Rs.9,000+ Cr, HAMMER ~Rs.2,500+ Cr, Shakti ~Rs.2,000 Cr; NGC/P-75I "more than 50% probability of getting both". All unsigned, approval-dependent.

6. **Export build-out and indigenisation** - Emerging/Strengthening - Strength: Medium

7. **Netra 2 lost to Adani; LCA LRU timing; wage revision from Jan 2027** - New Mention (risks) - Strength: Low-Medium
