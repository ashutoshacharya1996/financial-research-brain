## Management Tone Delta

**Demand commentary:**
- Prior (June 19 investor meet / Q4 call): QRSAM "expected Jun–Jul 2026"; FY27 pipeline QRSAM + P-75I + AMCA + data centre solutions.
- Current (Q2 call, 3 Nov): "at least NGC is cleared, and it was signed also by the parent PSUs, GSL and GRSE"; QRSAM "Hopefully, in next CCS ... more than 99%, I am confident it is coming to us very soon"; "maybe P-75I may slip to next year."
- Delta: **Mixed — Stable confidence, deteriorating timing.** NGC (~₹20,000 cr) is a hard win; QRSAM slipped ~4+ months past June guidance; P-75I downgraded from "100% probability of at least one [NGC/P-75I]" to likely FY28. — Signal strength: High

**Margin commentary:**
- Prior (Q4 FY26): FY26 margin ~29%; FY27 guide ">28%"; brokerages "cautiously optimistic".
- Current: Q1 "We are telling it is due to product mix variation, not due to input cost."; "at the year end, let me again assure you, we will cross 28%"; Q2 closing: "EBITDA margins of around 28%". New: wage revision "from January '27 onwards ... provisions will be made for 3 months".
- Delta: **Stable guidance, weaker delivery** — H1 27.43% below 28%; ">28%" softened to "around 28%" by Q2. — Signal strength: Medium

**Guidance:**
- Prior: FY27 revenue >15%; EBITDA >28%; order inflow >₹55,000 cr (incl. QRSAM ~₹21–30K cr); capex >₹1,200 cr; R&D ₹2,200 cr.
- Current: revenue "between 15% to 20%, more than 15%" (AR: "targeting a healthy growth of 15-20%"); inflow "total INR25,000 crores without QRSAM and with QRSAM INR55,000 crores ... we are sticking to that" (closing remark: "including QRSAM, INR50,000-plus crores"); EBITDA "around 28%"; capex "around INR1,200 crores"; R&D "INR2,200-plus crores".
- Delta: **Maintained** (no formal raise despite H1 +26%; no formal cut). Note wording drift: inflow "55,000" vs "50,000-plus" in same call; margin ">28%" → "around 28%". Q1 transcript closing remark ("EBITDA margin 21% to 23%, order inflow of more than INR20,000 crores") treated as transcription error. — Signal strength: Medium

## Key Signals Summary

1. **Order intake far behind guidance; order book shrinking** — Inflection down — Strength: High
   Evidence: H1 inflow ₹7,334 cr vs ₹55,000 cr guide; order book ₹73,882 → ₹68,717 cr (−7.0%, derived). Guidance retained only on NGC + QRSAM landing in Dec–Mar.

2. **Revenue execution acceleration** — Acceleration — Strength: High
   Evidence: "revenue from operations increased to INR12,824 crores ... growth of 25.97%" (H1) vs FY26 +16%; mgmt still guides only "more than 15%" — implies H2 deceleration/pull-forward.

3. **QRSAM slippage (third consecutive timing miss)** — Weakening — Strength: High
   Evidence: June: July 2026 → Q1 call: "maximum by September" → Q2 call: "Hopefully, in next CCS". Prior delta flagged "next slip (if any) to August-September would raise concern" — condition met and exceeded.

4. **NGC clearance — first big platform landed** — New mention — Strength: High
   Evidence: "at least NGC is cleared ... around INR20,000 crores worth of orders we are expecting in a staggered way, but all of them, 99%, will come in this financial year."

5. **Margin below guide; gross margin compression** — Deceleration — Strength: Medium
   Evidence: Q1 EBITDA 25.83%, H1 27.43% vs ">28%"; material-cost ratio ≈54% vs 47–49% YoY (derived); attributed to product mix.

6. **Cash conversion turned positive** — Inflection up — Strength: Medium
   Evidence: H1 OCF ₹1,469 cr vs (₹881 cr); receivable days 176 → 140.
