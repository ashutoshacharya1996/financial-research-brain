## Basis-of-comparison warning (read first)

**The prior-period extracted data and the current data are on different accounting bases.** The Composite Scheme (Anchemco / Asia Investments / Gabriel) became operative on 2026-05-22 and was applied by "pooling of interest method, applied retrospectively" (Q1 results note 1). Q4 FY26 extractions (pre-restatement) show FY26 consolidated revenue Rs 4,667 cr, Q4 consolidated revenue Rs 1,210 cr, Q4 consolidated EBITDA Rs 117 cr (9.7%). The restated figures in the Q1 FY27 filings show FY26 consolidated revenue Rs 52,451.94 mn (Rs 5,245 cr) and Q4 FY26 consolidated revenue Rs 13,807.88 mn (Rs 1,381 cr); restated Q4 consolidated EBITDA is Rs 1,372 mn (9.9%).

Therefore every QoQ/YoY delta below uses the **restated comparatives printed in the Q1 FY27 documents themselves** (restated-vs-restated). Deltas against the old Q4 FY26 extraction (e.g. 9.7% -> 8.7% margin, or Rs 1,210 cr -> Rs 1,426 cr revenue) would mix bases and are NOT reported as signals. Per the auditor, restated comparatives "to the extent relating to the Automotive Undertaking" are not limited-reviewed (see Flags).

## Headline Delta

Profit growth decoupled from operating margin as the group pivoted from organic compounding to a promoter-linked, debt-and-equity-funded acquisition programme: consolidated EBITDA margin fell to 8.7% (Q1 FY26 9.8%; Q4 FY26 9.9%) and consolidated EBITDA fell 9.5% QoQ on +3.3% revenue, while the company committed to Rs ~2,231 cr (HL Mando Anand, from promoter holding company), US$98.44 mn (HL Klemove India), a Rs 50 cr FORVIA seating JV, and a Rs 1,000 cr unsecured 8.15% NCD issue — moving the balance sheet away from the "net cash" position cited in the prior delta.

## Management Tone Delta

Reliability note: Q4 FY26 "management" commentary was extracted from secondary sources (GuruFocus snippets, Motilal Oswal initiation, Phillip Capital) because the transcript returned 403. For Q1 FY27 the earnings-call transcript (call held 2026-07-22) is NOT in the evidence set - only the notice (`earnings-call-transcript-2026-07-29.md`) and audio link. No Q&A, verbal demand or margin commentary exists in extracted data for Q1. Tone deltas below are therefore low-to-medium confidence and rest on slides, press releases, and a rating agency rationale.

**Demand commentary:**
- Prior: "Strong Revenue Growth Amid Market Challenges" (GuruFocus headline, Q4 FY26); "30% jump in EV two-wheelers"; new OEM orders "from OEMs like Mahindra and Mobis" (Q4 FY26 investor presentation extraction).
- Current: "2W Industry: 22.8% YoY growth in production - Sales: 21% YoY - Key drivers: Strong demand from key customers."; "PV Industry: 16.8% YoY growth - Sales: 5.5% YoY growth - Key drivers: Mainly traction in Small Cars"; "CV Industry: 15.2% YoY growth - Sales: 13.3% YoY growth - Key drivers: Rising demand from cabin dampers"; "GIL E2W market share at 57% in Q1 FY27" (slide 14-15). Crisil: "supported by robust performance across the two-wheeler segment, aftermarket business, new OEM programme launches and contributions from the acquired businesses."
- Delta: **Stable-to-Improving, with a segment split** - 2W (+21%) and CV (+13.3%) grew in line with their industries, while PV (+5.5%) lagged PV industry production (+16.8%) by ~11 points (derived; industry figure is production, Gabriel's is sales). The only previously un-numbered claim (EV 2W) now has a market-share figure (57%). Signal strength: Medium.

**Margin commentary:**
- Prior: "Standalone EBITDA grew 16% vs revenue 19% in Q4 - slight margin dilution; framed as temporary" and "Margins improved due to operational efficiencies" (sunroof JV, 15.1% FY26 vs 12-14% target) (Q4 FY26 extraction).
- Current: Company deck gives no explanation - "Consolidated EBITDA margin shown at 8.7% ... no explanatory commentary stated in this deck" (extraction note). New slide titled "CORE 90 (COST REDUCTION IN 90 DAYS)" with sub-items "RMC", "WORKING CAPITAL", "FIXED MANUFACTURING COST OPTIMISATION", "KEY TO SUCCESS IS SPEED & URGENCY" (no numeric target). Explanation comes only from Crisil: "operating margin moderated to 8.7% from 9.6% during the same period, owing to commodity cost inflation, lag in pass-through of raw material cost increases and the inclusion of relatively lower-margin businesses acquired under Project Rise." Crisil also: "expected to support operating margin of 9-10%".
- Delta: **Deteriorating** - "framed as temporary" dilution has become a ~100 bp YoY consolidated decline and a first-ever named cost-reduction emergency programme (new mention). The cause is attributed to commodity pass-through lag, which implies a recoverable component, but the only explanation is third-party. Signal strength: High (numbers), Medium (cause).

**Guidance:**
- Prior: Group revenue "Rs 50,000 crore by 2030" (management target per Motilal citation); sunroof JV EBITDA margin target 12-14%; Genos commercial production "expected by Q4 of the calendar year"; no FY27 revenue guidance (Q4 FY26 extraction).
- Current: "we remain firmly committed to our aspiration of achieving Group revenues of Rs 50,000 crore by 2030" (Mr. Mahendra K. Goyal, Group CEO and MD, Jupiter deck) and "these initiatives are expected to further enhance the Company's earnings per share" (~13% pro forma FY26 EPS accretion from HL Mando, "incremental to ~38% from Project Rise"). Nothing on FY27 revenue/margin, order book, sunroof margin target, Genos timing or fastener plant in any Q1 FY27 extraction.
- Delta: **Maintained (long-term aspiration only; first direct management attribution) - no operating guidance; sunroof margin and Genos guidance not repeated.** Signal strength: Medium. Per instruction, no order book or operating guidance was reported this period.
