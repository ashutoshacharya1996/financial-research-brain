## Headline Delta

Q2 FY27 revenue growth accelerated to +22.9% YoY (consolidated Rs 1,608 cr) but operating profitability did not follow: consolidated EBITDA margin stayed ~8.7-8.8% (vs 9.6% a year ago and 9.9% in Q4 FY26), the growth in profit came mostly from the equity-accounted JV line (+39.6% YoY), and the balance sheet moved from near-zero debt to ~Rs 1,270 cr of borrowings as Project Jupiter (HL Mando Anand 28.99% + HL Klemove India 29.99%) closed.

## Key Signals Summary

1. **Debt-funded, promoter-sourced consolidation** — Inflection — Strength: High
   Evidence: "Gabriel is expected to raise Rs 1,000–1,200 crore of debt primarily towards acquisition-related funding requirements" (Crisil, tier_1 filing); consol. borrowings ~Rs 12.7 bn vs ~Rs 2.1 bn; finance cost Rs 147.3 mn vs 62.8 mn YoY.
2. **Operating margin lag despite revenue acceleration** — Inflection down — Strength: High
   Evidence: consol. EBITDA margin 8.8% (Q2 FY27) vs 9.6% (Q2 FY26) and 9.9% (Q4 FY26); standalone 8.2% vs 8.8%; core PBT (pre-JV share) +4.5% YoY on revenue +22.9%.
3. **Associate/JV earnings now a primary driver** — Acceleration — Strength: High
   Evidence: share of profit Rs 483.0 mn vs 346.0 mn (+39.6%); Crisil: "nearly 45% of such profit historically being upstreamed through dividends". Note equity-accounted profit is non-cash until dividended.
4. **2W/EV strength vs PV/CV lag** — Acceleration (2W) / Deceleration relative to industry (PV, CV) — Strength: Medium
   Evidence: H1 2W +25.4% vs industry +19.5%; PV +7.3% vs +21%; CV +12.8% vs +24.7%; E2W share 61%.
5. **Governance: related-party intensity and institutional dissent** — New — Strength: Medium
   Evidence: acquisition of HL Mando stake from promoter holding co; Goyal CEO appointment remuneration "Salary: Rs 40 million per annum" plus MD compensation at Anand Automotive Pvt Ltd; public institutions voted 35.85% against PwC re-appointment, 47.38% against new AOA, 41.22% against Sec 186 limit of Rs 4,000 cr, 25.32% against Goyal appointment (all passed on promoter votes).
6. **Credit upgrade** — Positive — Strength: Medium
   Evidence: Crisil AA -> AA+/Stable (30-Jul); NCD "Crisil AA+/Stable (Assigned)".
