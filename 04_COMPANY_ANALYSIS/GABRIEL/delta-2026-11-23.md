## Headline Delta

Gabriel has turned from a net-cash ride-control company into a levered (₹1,000 crore NCD at 8.15%) holding-and-operating platform via Project Jupiter (₹2,231 crore HL Mando Anand stake bought from the promoter + ₹935 crore HL Klemove ADAS stake). Revenue growth sped up (consolidated +15.5% YoY in Q1 to +22.9% in Q2). EBITDA margins fell year on year in both quarters (consolidated 9.6–9.8% → 8.7–8.8%), so the profit growth now comes from associates' share of profit, not from the core business.

## Management Tone Delta

**Demand commentary:**
- Prior (Q4 FY26 / June): "30% jump in EV two-wheelers"; "secured new orders from OEMs like Mahindra and Mobis"
- Current (Q1/Q2 FY27 decks): 2W/3W "Key drivers: Strong demand from key customers"; "GIL E2W market share at 61%" (H1) vs "57%" (Q1); new LOIs "from River Mobility, Ultraviolette, Piaggio"; PV "Key drivers: Mainly traction in Small Cars" while GIL PV +7.3% vs industry +21%
- Delta: **Improving in 2W/EV; deteriorating relative share in PV/CV** — Strength: Medium (deck text, no transcript)

**Margin commentary:**
- Prior: Sunroof JV "EBITDA of INR 65.4 crores with a margin at 15.1%"; Motilal Oswal EBITDA CAGR 23% > revenue CAGR 22% (margin expansion)
- Current: Crisil: "operating margin moderated to 8.7% from 9.6% ... owing to commodity cost inflation, lag in pass-through of raw material cost increases and the inclusion of relatively lower-margin businesses acquired under Project Rise"; deck consolidated EBITDA margin "8.8%" vs "9.6%"
- Delta: **Deteriorating** — Strength: High (numbers changed direction)

**Guidance:**
- Prior: Group revenue ₹50,000 crore by 2030; sunroof EBITDA margin 12–14%
- Current: "we remain firmly committed to our aspiration of achieving Group revenues of ₹50,000 crore by 2030" (Goyal, Jul 21); new: Project Jupiter "~13% EPS accretion (incremental to ~38% from Project Rise) on a proforma FY26 basis"; Crisil (not management): operating margin 9–10% medium term, cash accrual ₹450–650 crore. Sunroof margin band not reiterated.
- Delta: **Maintained (group aspiration) + new EPS-accretion claim** — no explicit revenue/margin guidance cut found. Strength: Medium

## Key Signals Summary

1. **Margin inflection down despite faster revenue growth** — Inflection down — Strength: High
   Evidence: Consolidated EBITDA margin 9.6% → 8.8% (Q2 YoY), 9.8% → 8.7% (Q1 YoY); Crisil: "lag in pass-through of raw material cost increases"; standalone operating margin 7.12% vs 8.06% in Q1.

2. **Revenue acceleration led by 2W/EV share gains** — Acceleration — Strength: High
   Evidence: Consolidated revenue +15.5% (Q1) → +22.9% (Q2); "GIL E2W market share at 61%"; 2W/3W sales +25.4% vs industry +19.5% (H1).

3. **Balance sheet re-levered for related-party consolidation** — Inflection down (balance sheet) / New mention — Strength: High
   Evidence: Standalone non-current borrowings Rs 490 Mn → Rs 11,217 Mn; interest coverage 43.20x → 11.15x; HL Mando stake bought from the promoter, partly paid in new shares (promoter 63.55% → 66.29%).

4. **Sunroof JV reversal** — Inflection down — Strength: High
   Evidence: IGSS H1 FY27 revenue −12.7%, PAT Rs 46 Mn vs Rs 160 Mn (−71.1%). In June this unit was the best signal (15.1% margin, above guidance).

5. **Earnings mix shifting to associates (Dana Anand strong; HL Mando/Klemove adding from Q3)** — Acceleration — Strength: Medium
   Evidence: Share of profit Rs 483 Mn (+39.6% YoY); Dana Anand Q2 PAT +52.6%; pre-associate consolidated PBT only +4.5%.

6. **Credit upgrade AA → AA+** — Inflection up — Strength: Medium
   Evidence: "Crisil Ratings Limited has revised and upgraded the Credit Rating of Gabriel India Limited."
