## Headline Delta

Gabriel India's capital structure changed materially this period: a ₹1,881 crore preferential equity allotment to promoter entity Asia Investments Private Limited (AIPL) — for non-cash consideration, to part-fund the acquisition of HL Mando ANAND India Private Limited — plus a separate ₹1,000 crore unsecured NCD raise, both executed within 48 hours of each other, and both arriving the same week an already-scheduled non-deal roadshow was abruptly cancelled "due to some exigencies." This is a direct continuation of the May 22, 2026 Scheme of Arrangement (which brought a promoter-group demerged undertaking into Gabriel India) — but where that filing explicitly stated "no external capital raise, no dilution to minority shareholders from this filing alone," this period's preferential allotment is exactly that: dilution to minority shareholders via new shares issued to the promoter, which supersedes the earlier benign assessment.
