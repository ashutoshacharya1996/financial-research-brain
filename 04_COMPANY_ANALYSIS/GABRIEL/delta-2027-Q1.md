## Headline Delta

Gabriel pivoted from an organic "suspension plus sunroof/EV adjacencies, net cash, margin expansion" story to an M&A-led, debt-funded group build (HL Mando Anand 28.99% for ~Rs 1,881 Cr in shares plus ~Rs 350 Cr cash, HL Klemove India 30% for USD 98.44 mn, FORVIA seating JV, Rs 1,000 Cr NCD at 8.15%) while Q1 FY27 margins moved the opposite way to the prior thesis (standalone EBITDA margin 8.4% vs 9.3% a year ago on revenue +18.9% YoY; consolidated 8.7% vs 9.8%).

**Margin commentary:**
- Prior: "Standalone EBITDA grew 16% vs revenue 19% in Q4 — slight margin dilution; framed as temporary"; "Margins improved due to operational efficiencies" (sunroof JV); Motilal: "PAT CAGR (55%) significantly exceeds Revenue CAGR (22%)"; Delta file June: "Q4 dip appears transient — not structural".

**Guidance:**
