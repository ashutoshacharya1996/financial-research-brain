## Headline Delta

Q2 FY27 is the inflection quarter on the top line and orders: record order inflow of ₹1,47,665 cr (+28% YoY) and revenue growth accelerating to +16% YoY. Margin is the offset: EBITDA rose only 1% and the margin fell to 8.7% (10.0% a year ago), so the +33% PAT rests partly on other income, lower finance costs and a one-time LTTS divestment gain.

## Management Tone Delta

**Demand commentary:**
- Prior (Q1 FY27): "The Company has managed to maintain momentum by rotating its focus across sectors and geographies while maintaining robust cash flows." Presentation: "Strong addressable prospects pipeline of ~ Rs 15 trillion for the near term" (slide 9).
- Current (Q2 FY27): CMD: "The Company has delivered a strong quarter in an everchanging geopolitical landscape... We achieved yet another record quarterly order inflow." Also: "The highest ever order book at ₹8.5 lakh crore provides multi-year revenue visibility." Presentation: "Strong addressable prospects pipeline of ~ Rs 10 trillion for the near term" (slide 7).
- Delta: **Improving on orders; pipeline quantification lower.** Orders improved (Strength: High; record inflow). The near-term addressable pipeline fell from ~₹15 trn to ~₹10 trn (Strength: Medium). Neither extract states whether the basis or definition changed. Flagged, not resolved.

**Margin commentary:**
- Prior (Q1 FY27): "EBITDA margin was impacted by lower execution levels, forex variation in IT subsidiaries and higher ECL provisions" (presentation, slide 9).
- Current (Q2 FY27): Group: "Primarily impacted due to lower margin across portfolio and change in revenue mix (higher share of PP&M)" (presentation, slide 8). Manufacturing & Products, counter-example: "The margin improvement is reflective of a more favourable execution profile of projects within the portfolio."
- Delta: **Deteriorating at group level; mixed by segment** — Strength: High. Group margin fell to 8.7% (10.0% year-ago). Manufacturing & Products improved.

**Guidance:**
- Prior (Q1 FY27 file): No numeric guidance in Q1 filings. FY27 10–12% growth (Q4 call, reiterated 20 Jun per summary) not restated.
- Current (Q2 FY27 filings): No numeric guidance stated in the board outcome or presentation. Outlook: "The global economic outlook is one of guarded optimism, aided by AI Data Center demand in several major economies..." and "...India is expected to remain among the fastest-growing major economies, with the RBI forecasting real GDP growth of 7.1% for FY 2026-27..."
- Delta: **Maintained by default, not restated.** No revision found. H1 revenue (+11%) is inside the 10–12% band. Q2 (+16%) is above it. Strength: Medium. Note that the H1 "softer" warning (20 Jun summary) is not borne out in H1 revenue growth, though H1 margin fell.

## Source Conflicts and Extraction Caveats (not silently resolved)

1. **Operating cash flow (excl. FS), Q1 FY27.** Q1 presentation: ₹43 bn ("Cash flow from Operations (excl Financial Services) 58 43"). Q2 presentation: H1 ₹180 bn and Q2 ₹128 bn, which implies Q1 ₹52 bn. Difference of ₹9 bn. Possible restatement or definitional change; not explained in either extract. Unresolved. The H1 FY26 comparator (₹106 bn = ₹58 bn + ₹48 bn) reconciles, so the gap is in the FY27 Q1/H1 figures.
2. **Pipeline basis.** "~ Rs 15 trillion" (Q1) and "~ Rs 10 trillion" (Q2) are both labelled "near term." The extraction does not state whether the basis changed. Read as a reported change; not as a confirmed decline in opportunity.
3. **Divestment gain.** The LTTS SWC divestment gain is named as a PAT driver but its amount is not extracted. Underlying PAT cannot be computed from these sources.
4. **Board outcome annexures.** Annexures II and III are referenced but not extracted. The document runs 33 pages; the first ~15 were read (results narrative, outlook, Annexure 1).
5. **Call content.** Q2 audio and transcript are not in local evidence. Management quotes are from the board outcome and presentation only.
6. **Realty margin swing.** Realty EBITDA margin: 36.9% (Q1), 54.3% (Q2). The Q2 filing explains the YoY decline ("fewer residential unit handovers") but not the QoQ rise. Not reconciled.
7. **Q2 FY26 comparatives** come from prior-year columns of Q2 FY27 filings. No separate Q2 FY26 extraction exists.
8. **K9 Vajra-T value.** Unresolved carry-over from the Q1 file: baseline "₹5,000–10,000 crore range"; 20 Jun summary "~₹7,500 crore"; June update "₹7,628 crore" (Dec 2024). Q2 extracts do not restate it.
