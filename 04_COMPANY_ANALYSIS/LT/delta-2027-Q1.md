## Headline Delta

Q1 FY27 delivered record order book (₹778,954 crore, +5% vs Mar'26) and +14% order inflows, but revenue grew only +7% YoY (below the 10-12% FY27 guidance band) with EBITDA down 3% in absolute terms and margin down 90 bps to 9.0%, confirming the "H1 softer" warning rather than a recovery; PAT +14% was supported by non-operating items not itemised in the extracts.

## Management Tone Delta

**Demand commentary:**
- Prior (Q4 FY26 baseline, delta-2026-Q4): "Order inflows beat guidance; order book +28% YoY - demand environment strong; Middle East disruptions are execution, not demand, issue". Prior (20 Jun analyst meet): "H1 will be softer due to execution headwinds, expect an anticipated pickup in H2 with PPM margins broadly stable"; "H2 to be stronger as Middle East conditions normalize".
- Current (results release): "significant order wins were achieved across multiple businesses such as Residential & Commercial buildings, Transportation Infrastructure, Ferrous Metals, Offshore Wind and the Heavy Engineering businesses." Deck: "Order Inflow growth aided by ultra-mega offshore wind orders from Europe and sustained domestic private sector ordering". Outlook: "However, the adverse implications of the extended disruption in supply chains and elevated energy prices could interrupt the growth momentum."
- Delta: Demand Improving (inflow breadth, domestic private ordering); near-term execution Deteriorating vs the "H2 normalisation" framing, since the release now says disruption is "extended" - Strength: High (for inflows), Medium (for tone).

**Margin commentary:**
- Prior: FY27 "stable margins"; PP&M margins ~7.8% (flattish); "PPM margins broadly stable".
- Current: "The contraction in margin is primarily on account of change in revenue mix and an increase in credit provisions on account of expected delays in collection of receivables." (Infrastructure & Utilities); "The margin is reflective of change in the sales mix within the portfolio." (Manufacturing & Products); "The marginal decline is reflective of higher manpower cost and forex variation adversely impacting hedged portfolio positions." (Technology, Platforms & Services).
- Delta: Deteriorating - Strength: High. Group margin 9.0% (-90 bps YoY) is not "stable", and the new credit-provision language ("expected delays in collection of receivables") is a quality signal absent in prior periods.

**Guidance:**
- Prior: "FY27: 10-12% growth in both order inflows and revenue. PP&M margins ~7.8% (flat)"; reiterated at 20 Jun analyst meet. Lakshya 2031: revenue CAGR 12-15%, order inflow CAGR 10-12%, ROE 16-17%.
- Current: Results release: "No numeric guidance". Deck: "No numeric FY27 guidance stated in this presentation." Only outlook item: "Strong addressable prospects pipeline of ~ Rs 15 trillion for the near term".
- Delta: Neither raised nor lowered in the extracted Q1 documents - the 10-12% guidance is not restated (not withdrawn either). Classify as "Not restated / unverified pending transcript". Strength: Medium. Q1 revenue (+7%) is below the band.
