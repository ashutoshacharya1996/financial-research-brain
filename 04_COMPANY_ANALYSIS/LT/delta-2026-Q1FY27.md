## Headline Delta

Q1 FY27 delivered the "soft H1" the June 22 analyst meet warned of (revenue +7% YoY vs +11% in Q4 FY26 and +12% in FY26; EBITDA margin 9.0% vs 9.9% a year ago), while order inflow (+14% YoY, Rs 108,014 crore) pushed the order book to a record Rs 778,954 crore (+5% over Mar-26) -- backlog growth is outrunning revenue, and the prior FY27 10-12% guidance is neither confirmed nor changed in the evidence.

## Management Tone Delta

**Demand commentary:**
- Prior (Q4 FY26 call, snippets; June 20 analyst meet): "Order inflow guidance surpassed in FY26 despite revenue miss"; "H1 will be softer due to execution headwinds; expect an anticipated pickup in H2 with Middle East conditions normalizing."
- Current (Q1 FY27 press release): "Domestic demand continues to remain healthy, with sustained expansion across both manufacturing and services activity." Deck: "Private capex recovery broadening"; "Tech capex supercycle continues -- AI, cloud, data centers"; near-term addressable prospects pipeline "~ Rs 15 trillion".
- Delta: Stable demand tone, order-side Improving; execution-side Deteriorating as forewarned -- Signal strength: Medium. The "H1 soft" warning is borne out by Q1; H2 pickup is NOT yet evidenced (no Q2 data, no call).

**Margin commentary:**
- Prior: Q4 FY26 "stable margins" for FY27; PP&M ~7.8% flattish; Moody's note (Jul 2026 press release) expects "healthy operating margins".
- Current: "The contraction in margin is primarily on account of change in revenue mix and an increase in credit provisions on account of expected delays in collection of receivables." (Infra & Utilities); "However, the adverse implications of the extended disruption in supply chains and elevated energy prices could interrupt the growth momentum."
- Delta: Deteriorating -- Strength: High (9.9% -> 9.0%). Note new language on "expected delays in collection of receivables" (credit provisions).

**Guidance:**
- Prior: FY27 10-12% growth in both order inflows and revenue; "stable margins"; Lakshya 2031 (revenue CAGR 12-15%, ROE 16-17%); reiterated 2026-06-20.
- Current: No numeric guidance in any Q1 document ("Not applicable"/"No numeric guidance"). Outlook only: "the Company remains committed to delivering sustainable shareholder value..."; CMD: "we remain confident of maintaining growth while capitalising on emerging opportunities."
- Delta: **Neither confirmed nor changed** (not a "Maintained" and not a "Withdrawn"). The call transcript, where guidance would have been reiterated or revised, is absent. Treat 10-12% as unverified-for-Q1; Q1 revenue +7% implies the remainder of FY27 must run above 10-12% for the guide to hold. Strength: Medium (information gap).

## Key Signals Summary

1. **Revenue deceleration to +7% with margin down 90 bps** -- Deceleration / Inflection down -- Strength: High
   Evidence: "Revenues at Rs 67,942 crore, up 7%"; "EBITDA 6,318 6,116 (3%)"; "EBITDA margin was impacted by lower execution levels, forex variation in IT subsidiaries and higher ECL provisions". Consistent with the June 22 "H1 softer" warning; 10-12% FY27 guide now requires a H2 step-up that is unevidenced.

2. **Order inflow/backlog expansion -- ultra-mega, international-led** -- Acceleration -- Strength: High
   Evidence: "The Company secured orders worth Rs 108,014 crore, registering a y-o-y growth of 14%"; order book "Rs 778,954 crore ... a 5% growth over Mar'26"; post-quarter: TenneT 2 GW offshore wind (Ultra-Mega*), ADNOC Offshore (Ultra-Mega*), Middle East offshore development (Ultra-Mega*), Middle East gas compression (ultra-mega*), PT&D (Mega*), AI Factory (Mega*), Metals & Minerals (Mega*), NTPC Lara LNTP (Mega*). Rupee values undisclosed (bands only). Heavy Middle East exposure sits alongside Middle East being the cited execution drag.

3. **AI factory / data-centre build-out becomes a real business line** -- New mention / Emerging -- Strength: Medium
   Evidence: "has secured India's largest single-cluster AI infrastructure -- an NVIDIA B300 AI Factory -- for a US-based AI cloud innovator"; "The integrated AI Factory, with capacity of 10,000 B300 NVIDIA GPUs, will be hosted at Vyoma's Chennai data centre." Caveat: existing Data Center business revenue only Rs 36.6 cr (0.025% of standalone revenue); order value is a band only.

   Evidence: Nabha Rs 3,632.35 cr received; Hyderabad Metro slips to Dec-31-2026; Realty EV Rs 6,300 cr scheme approved by shareholders (99.07%); Data Center Rs 1,400 cr slump sale. Capital recycling supports balance sheet (cash Rs 851 bn) but Net D/E rose 0.35 -> 0.39.

   Evidence: Net cash from operations "30.5 (20.2)" Rs bn; CFO excl FS "58 43"; "expected delays in collection of receivables"; despite NWC at 4.9% of revenue.
