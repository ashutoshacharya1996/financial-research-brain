## Management Tone Delta

**Demand commentary:**
- Prior (Q4 FY26): "order book up 28% YoY to INR 7.40 trillion, surpassing order inflow guidance" (Q4 extraction, quoting the call). Baseline characterised this as "demand strong; Middle East disruptions are execution, not demand, issue."
- Current (Q1 FY27): CMD: "The financial year has commenced against the backdrop of geopolitical uncertainties. The Company has managed to maintain momentum by rotating its focus across sectors and geographies while maintaining robust cash flows." Also: "...we remain confident of maintaining growth while capitalising on emerging opportunities."
- Delta: **Stable on demand, deteriorating on execution** — Strength: High. Orders are still growing, but the revenue conversion slowed.

**Margin commentary:**
- Prior (Q4 FY26): "EBITDA margins narrowed to 10.4% due to cost pressures" (Q4 extraction). FY27 guided to "stable margins" (PP&M ~7.8%, flattish).
- Current (Q1 FY27): "EBITDA margin was impacted by lower execution levels, forex variation in IT subsidiaries and higher ECL provisions" (investor presentation, slide 9). Infrastructure & Utilities: "Margin decline reflects change in revenue mix and higher ECL provisions."
- Delta: **Deteriorating** — Strength: High. The "stable margins" language from Q4 is not borne out by Q1.

**Guidance:**
- Prior (Q4 FY26 extraction; reiterated 20 Jun 2026 per analyst-meet extraction): FY27 "10–12% growth in both order inflows and revenue"; "H1 will be softer due to execution headwinds, expect an anticipated pickup in H2 with PPM margins broadly stable." (The 20 Jun extraction is from secondary summaries; the NSE PDF returned 403.)
- Current (Q1 FY27 filings): No numeric FY27 guidance stated in the board outcome or investor presentation. Outlook: "the adverse implications of the extended disruption in supply chains and elevated energy prices could interrupt the growth momentum." Presentation: "Subdued revenue growth primarily attributable to execution stage of the order book and supply chain constraints in the Middle East."
- Delta: **Maintained by default, not restated.** Q1 revenue (+7%) sits below the 10–12% band, which is consistent with the June "soft H1" flag. No revision found in the Q1 extracts. Strength: Medium.
