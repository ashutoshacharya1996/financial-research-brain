## Headline Delta

Q1 FY27 delivered the "soft H1" that management warned of in June (revenue +7% YoY, EBITDA -3% YoY, EBITDA margin down to 9.0% from 10.4% in Q4 and 9.9% a year ago) while order momentum stayed strong (inflow +14% YoY to Rs 1.08 lakh crore, order book Rs 7.79 trillion, +27% YoY); reported PAT +14% was lifted by lower finance cost and divestment-related items, not operations. No numeric FY27 guidance appears in any Q1 document, so the June 10-12% guidance is neither reaffirmed nor revised in the extracted record.

## Management Tone Delta

**Demand commentary:**
- Prior (June 22): "H1 will be softer due to execution headwinds, expect an anticipated pickup in H2" / "H2 to be stronger as Middle East conditions normalize" (analyst-meet-2026-06-20.md, as summarised from secondary sources)
- Current (Q1 results pack CMD): "The financial year has commenced against the backdrop of geopolitical uncertainties. The Company has managed to maintain momentum by rotating its focus across sectors and geographies while maintaining robust cash flows." Presentation: "Strong addressable prospects pipeline of ~ Rs 15 trillion for the near term"; "Order Inflow growth aided by ultra-mega offshore wind orders from Europe and sustained domestic private sector ordering"
- Delta: **Demand Stable-to-Improving, execution Deteriorating** — Strength: High. Orders and pipeline are strong; revenue conversion is weak. Note the demand pickup is increasingly European offshore wind and domestic private, while Energy-Conventional order awards were "deferred".
- Middle East specifically: June framing was "execution, not demand" and "normalizing in H2". Q1 text does NOT confirm normalisation. It says "supply chain constraints in the Middle East" (presentation), "supply chain disruptions arising from the West Asia conflict in the Solar business" (results pack), and in Outlook "the resurgence of geopolitical tensions in West Asia ... remains key risks". Counter-evidence of demand: at least four Middle East awards since July (ADNOC, offshore ultra-mega, KOC, BESS, Dubai APM, Latifa, PDO, PT&D Saudi/UAE) and Moody's (Jul 6) citing "large-scale EPC projects in West Asia continue to scale". Delta on the "normalising" thesis: **Not confirmed; language more cautious** — Strength: Medium.

**Margin commentary:**
- Prior (June 22): "PPM (Project and Product segment Margins) broadly stable" / FY27 PP&M about 7.8% flattish (Q4FY26 call file)
- Current: "The contraction in margin is primarily on account of change in revenue mix and an increase in credit provisions on account of expected delays in collection of receivables." (I&U); "EBITDA margin was impacted by lower execution levels, forex variation in IT subsidiaries and higher ECL provisions" (group)
- Delta: **Deteriorating vs "broadly stable"** — Strength: High. Group margin fell 90 bps YoY. The "PPM / PP&M 7.8%" metric cannot be tracked because segments were realigned from 1-Apr-2026 under Lakshya 2031 (results pack: "Effective from April 01, 2026 ... the Group has realigned its portfolio"). Comparability of the 7.8% reference is unresolved (see Flags). Rising ECL provisions tied to "expected delays in collection of receivables" is a new credit-quality mention.

**Guidance:**
- Prior: FY27 10-12% growth in order inflows and revenue; "H1 softer / H2 pickup"; Lakshya 2031 (Rs 5.8 trn revenue, Rs 7.75 trn/yr inflows, ROE 16-17%)
- Current: None numeric in any Q1 document. Results pack: "No numeric FY27 guidance in this document"; presentation: "None stated"; call content not extracted. Only qualitative Outlook: "the adverse implications of the extended disruption in supply chains and elevated energy prices could interrupt the growth momentum."
- Delta: **Not reaffirmed, not revised (silent in extracted record)** — Strength: Medium. Tracking vs the June guide: Q1 revenue +7% is below the 10-12% full-year run-rate (so H2 must run above 12% to land in range, which is the same H2-dependent shape management described); Q1 order inflow +14% is above the 10-12% target. The tone has moved from "pickup in H2" to a written caution that supply-chain and energy-price disruption "could interrupt the growth momentum". Re-confirm with the actual call transcript before treating guidance as intact.
