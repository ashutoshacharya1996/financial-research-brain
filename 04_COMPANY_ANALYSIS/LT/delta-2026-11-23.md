## Headline Delta

Order momentum inflected sharply upward — Q2 FY27 order inflow hit a record ₹1,47,665 cr (+28% YoY), H1 inflow +22% (roughly double the 10–12% FY27 guide) and the order book reached ₹8.54 trn (+28% YoY) — but EBITDA margin kept sliding (10.4% Q4 FY26 → 9.0% Q1 → 8.7% Q2), so the +33% Q2 PAT growth was carried by other income, lower finance cost and tax, not operations.

## Management Tone Delta

**Demand commentary:**
- Prior (June 20 analyst meet, tier_3): "H1 will be softer due to execution headwinds, expect an anticipated pickup in H2"
- Current (Q2 press release, tier_1): "We achieved yet another record quarterly order inflow. Focused execution across our Projects, Products & Manufacturing businesses supported a higher revenue growth. The highest ever order book at ₹8.5 lakh crore provides multi-year revenue visibility."; "We are well positioned to sustain this growth momentum"
- Delta: **Improving** — Strength: High. Q1 (+7% revenue; "Subdued revenue growth primarily attributable to execution stage of the order book and supply chain constraints in the Middle East") validated the soft-H1 warning; Q2 (+16%) arrived earlier and stronger than the "H2 pickup" framing.

**Margin commentary:**
- Prior: "PPM margins broadly stable"; PP&M ~7.8% FY27 guide; Q4 "EBITDA margins narrowed to 10.4% due to cost pressures"
- Current: Q1 "EBITDA margin was impacted by lower execution levels, forex variation in IT subsidiaries and higher ECL provisions"; Q2 "Primarily impacted due to lower margin across portfolio and change in revenue mix (higher share of PP&M)"; I&U "continuing cost pressures in WET projects and higher proportion of jobs below margin recognition threshold"; Energy-Conventional "jobs in early stages in CarbonLite Solutions; PY margin included variation claims"
- Delta: **Deteriorating** — Strength: High. Margin pressure is now portfolio-wide (I&U, Energy-Conventional, Energy-Green, TPS all down YoY in Q2; only M&P up).

**Guidance:**
- Prior: FY27 revenue and order inflow growth 10–12%; PP&M margin ~7.8%; Lakshya 2031 revenue CAGR 12–15%
- Current: No numeric guidance in tier_1 filings/decks collected (transcripts not collected). Q1 outlook: "we remain confident of maintaining growth"; Q2: "well positioned to sustain this growth momentum"
- Delta: **Not found / unconfirmed** — actuals: H1 revenue +11% (in range), H1 inflow +22% (well above range), PP&M margin 7.0% H1 (below ~7.8%). Revision status requires the 27-Oct-2026 call transcript.

## Key Signals Summary

1. **Record order inflow, book ₹8.54 trn** — Acceleration — Strength: High
   Evidence: "Record Order Inflows at ₹ 147,665 crore, up 28%"; H1 ₹2,55,678 cr (+22%) vs 10–12% FY27 guide; order book +28% YoY, 54% international.

2. **EBITDA margin down three quarters running** — Inflection down — Strength: High
   Evidence: 10.4% (Q4 FY26) → 9.0% (Q1) → 8.7% (Q2, vs 10.0% YoY); PP&M 7.0% H1 vs ~7.8% guide; "lower margin across portfolio"; CarbonLite 5.8% vs 19.0%.

3. **PAT growth driven by below-the-line items** — New mention (quality flag) — Strength: High
   Evidence: Q2 EBITDA +1% vs PAT +33%; other income +67% ("one-time gain on divestment of SWC business in LTTS"), finance cost -38%, deferred tax credit ₹337 cr.

4. **Cash conversion / working capital at record** — Emerging — Strength: High
   Evidence: NWC 1.7% of revenue (10.2% a year ago); CFO ex-FS Q2 ₹128 bn (+170%).

5. **Revenue re-accelerated in Q2 after soft Q1** — Inflection up — Strength: Medium
   Evidence: +7% Q1 → +16% Q2; "driven by accelerated progress in the various business in the Projects, Products & Manufacturing (PP&M) portfolio."

6. **New-economy themes (AI DC, offshore wind HVDC, BESS, T&D) emerging in order flow** — Emerging — Strength: Medium
   Evidence: Together AI Mega order; TenneT ultra-mega FCA; 6 GWh BESS; PT&D Mega.

## Red Flags

- Margin erosion with rising ECL (H1 ₹2,353 cr, +26%) and receivable-delay provisions in I&U; WET cost pressures.
- Earnings quality: Q2 PAT growth far exceeds EBITDA growth (other income, tax).
- Middle East concentration rising (three ME ultra-mega wins in Aug 2026; 63% of Q2 inflow international) while management flags ME "downside risks".
- Several large wins are LNTP / conditional (NTPC Lara, DVC Chandrapura, Tashkent ultra-mega); gas-compression contract relates to an FY26 LoA.
- Deal slippage: HMRL divestment pushed twice (→ 31-Dec-2026); Vyoma DC BTA pushed from 31-Oct-2026 to 31-Mar-2027.
- Near-term prospect pipeline cited lower (~₹15 trn → ~₹10 trn).
- Segment head of Transportation Infrastructure resigned (27-Jul-2026; internal successor).
