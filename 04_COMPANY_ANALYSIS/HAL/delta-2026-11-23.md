## Headline Delta

Revenue growth inflected from +1.8% YoY in Q4 FY26 (FY26 +7%) to +14.4% in Q1 FY27 and +14.9% in Q2 FY27 (H1 FY27 +14.7% to ₹13,128 cr), running ahead of the "10% to 12% minimum" FY27 guidance. But the 27.8% Q1 EBITDA margin (ICRA calculation) is below the 30–31% guided band, and no filing confirms the LCA Mk1A deliveries promised for "August or September".

- Current: No management call in evidence. Closest primary-source statement is the CMD in the Aravalli release (2026-08-26): "The signing of this contract marks a significant step forward in India's pursuit of self-reliance in aero-engine technologies." The PIB release filed by HAL says "The ALHs will execute complex missions across all terrains and operational situations for the Indian Army and the Indian Air Force."
- Delta: Stable (no new demand commentary). The ALH AoN partly confirms the prior pipeline statement. Signal strength: Low–Medium

**Margin commentary:**
- Prior: "the EBITDA margin, we have always maintained around 30%, 31%, and it will be maintained." / "So there will not be any cost increase as such for '26-'27."

- Delta: Deteriorating on data, with no commentary (Q1 is 220–320 bp below the guided band; the seasonality caveat applies). Signal strength: Medium

**Guidance:**
- Prior: "it will be in the range of 10% to 12% minimum" (revenue FY27); EBITDA "around 30%, 31%"; LCA "by August or September, we should be able to start the deliveries" and "20, this year"; HTT-40 "around 20 plus this year".
- Current: No updated guidance in evidence. H1 revenue growth of +14.7% is ahead of the revenue guidance. The LCA delivery window (Aug/Sep 2026) has passed and no filing confirms a first delivery. LCA status: **not found**.
- Delta: Maintained (not restated). The revenue path is tracking above guidance. LCA milestone status is unverified and overdue against the stated window.
