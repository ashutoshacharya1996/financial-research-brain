## Key Signals Summary

1. **Revenue and profit growth roughly doubled vs FY26** — Acceleration — Strength: High
   Evidence: "(a) Revenue from operations  5,51,528  13,94,332  4,81,914  33,08,979" (Q1 FY27 vs Q1 FY26 = +14.4%); PAT "1,58,061 ... 1,37 715" (+14.8%). Prior: "Revenue (full year) ... +7% YoY"; "5.4% YoY increase in Q4 net profit to ₹4,196.04 crore". Caveat: FPQ sales "provisionally recognised by considering the prices of 2024-25".

2. **Governance and board-composition deficiency persists, plus finance-team churn and an auditor change** — Inflection down (new in this tracking set) — Strength: High
   Evidence: "the Company is not complying with Regulation 17(1) of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, as amended, and Section 149(4) of the Companies Act, 2013"; "In the absence of requisite number of independent directors, the Company is not able to reconstitute an Audit Committee". Only one independent director appointment is on file (Dr. Nikhil Agarwal, 9 Sep) and the 28 Aug AGM states "Audit Committee and NRC is not in valid constitution". Finance roles: GM (Finance) superannuation 30.06; ED (Finance) transfer 29.07 and ED (Finance) superannuation 30.09.

3. **Pipeline broadened by approvals, not orders** — Strengthening — Strength: Medium
   Evidence: DAC "about Rs 1,10,000 crore" AoN including ALH; Aravalli final contract for IMRH/DBMRH. Neither has a disclosed HAL value; MALE UAV and Mark-1A/HTT-40 statuses are silent.

4. **JV portfolio clean-up with going-concern and related-party features** — New Mention — Strength: Medium
   Evidence: HATSOFF: "the Company's current liabilities exceeded its current assets by Rs.8,262.39 lakhs"; 2 Oct: HAL takes CAE's 50% at "NIL consideration", "not at Arm's length". HALBIT: "uncertainty that may cast doubt upon the Company's ability to continue as a going concern". Infotech HAL: Q1 note "NCLT ordered for the commencement of corporate insolvency resolution process", then dissolved 17 Aug.

5. **Other income contributes ~42% of PBT** — Medium: other income 90,281 lakh (+20.5%) vs PBT 2,12,528 lakh; operating profit not disclosed.
