## Source Conflicts and OCR Caveats (carried through, not resolved)

1. **Q4 FY26 revenue and PAT differ between sources.** The earnings-call extraction states Q4 revenue ₹13,942.40 crore and Q4 PAT ₹4,196.04 crore. Both match the consolidated columns of the Q1 filing. Standalone figures in the same filing are revenue ₹13,943.32 crore and PAT ₹4,184.28 crore. Which basis the baseline intended is not stated in the extraction.
2. **FY26 revenue and PAT.** The earnings-call extraction states ₹33,088.82 crore and ₹9,115.52 crore. These match the consolidated FY26 columns (33,08,882 and 9,11,552 lakh). Standalone FY26 figures are ₹33,089.79 crore and ₹9,075.67 crore. The June 2026 delta files used the call figures without a basis label.
3. **Order book date.** The Q4 extraction labels ₹2,54,538 crore as "April 1, 2025 (as stated in filing)". The date looks inconsistent with a FY26 figure and could not be checked in the Q1 or Q2 documents.
4. **Q4 FY26 source quality.** No verbatim transcript was accessible. Q4 management quotes are snippet paraphrases.
5. **Q1 filing OCR.** Table figures carry spacing artifacts. The extraction note says internal arithmetic was checked (for example 5,51,528 + 90,281 = 6,41,809), but the filing itself has not been verified. HALBIT net liability reads "Rs 1146. 11 lakhs" in the extraction. The unit as printed is Rs lakh, so this is read as ₹1,146.11 lakh. Verify.
6. **Q1 column headers not captured.** See Basis and Column Mapping.
