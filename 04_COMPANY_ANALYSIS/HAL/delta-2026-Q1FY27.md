## Headline Delta

## New Mentions This Period

- Aravalli helicopter engine (3,500-4,000 shp) contract with SAFHAL, for the 13-ton IMRH and naval DBMRH — "a new-generation high-power helicopter engine in the 3,500 to 4,000 shp power class, named 'Aravalli'". Safran CEO: "This is the first time; Safran HE has taken up such a class of Engine as Co-development." No contract value, timeline, or delivery date stated.
- ALH Acceptance of Necessity at the DAC meeting of 7 Sep 2026 — "approval for Acceptance of Necessity (AON) for procurement of Advanced Light Helicopters (ALH)" for "the Indian Army and the Indian Air Force". AoN is "in-principle administrative approval"; no quantity, value or named supplier. Total DAC AoNs about Rs 1,10,000 crore.
- HATSOFF Helicopter Training Pvt Ltd: HAL to acquire CAE Canada's 50% (3,84,04,205 shares) "at NIL consideration", making it a "100% Wholly-Owned Subsidiary of HAL". Completion "tentatively within 60 days" of the STA. "The transaction is not at Arm's length." HATSOFF turnover FY24/FY25/FY26: Rs 66.49 / 69.55 / 80.95 cr. MOD-DDP administrative approval with DIPAM concurrence received.
- Audit Committee non-reconstitution — "In the absence of requisite number of independent directors, the Company is not able to reconstitute an Audit Committee in pursuance of Section 177 of the Companies Act, 2013 and regulation 18(1) of SEBI (LODR)... till date of approval of financial results." (Note 1). A later routine independent-director appointment (10 Sep) appears only in the skipped-record list; contents not read.
- JV going-concern / insolvency notes: HATSOFF "current liabilities exceeded its current assets by Rs.8,262.39 lakhs" (about Rs 82.6 cr, derived, versus FY26 turnover of Rs 80.95 cr) and "substantial erosion of networth"; HALBIT "Material Uncertainty Related to Going Concern"; Multirole Transport Aircraft Ltd in-principle approval for voluntary liquidation (needs GoI approval); Infotech HAL (see discrepancy below); HAL-Edgewood "has not prepared the financial statements for the three months period ended 30 June 2026, and accordingly not considered for consolidation."
- FPQ provisional pricing at 2024-25 rates (Note 7) and gratuity-ceiling employee cost non-comparability (Note 8).
- Auditor emphasis of matter on notes (Note 3 LCA TD store flood inventory; JV going-concern notes) with "unmodified opinion" from "Gupta Nayar & Co., Chartered Accountants". Cost auditor for FY27: "M/s Murthy & Co. LLP".

Data discrepancy to resolve: the 12-Aug results (Note 11) say "NCLT ordered for the commencement of corporate insolvency resolution process of Infotech HAL Limited and appointed Interim Resolution Professional", while the 20-Aug filing says the company "has been dissolved by the NCLT, Bengaluru Bench vide its order dated 17th August, 2026". HAL held 50%. The extracted data does not explain the sequence or any financial impact; both are recorded as stated.

## Key Signals Summary

1. **Hard-number visibility lost: no EBITDA, order book, inflows or guidance in the Q1 FY27 filing** — Dropped — Strength: High
   Evidence: "Not stated in this document" for guidance and all commentary; EBITDA/order book/order inflows/capex "not stated". Prior quarter's anchors (Rs 2,54,538 cr book; 30-31% margin guide; 10-12% growth) can neither be confirmed nor refuted.

2. **FPQ revenue at 2024-25 prices without escalation, pending 4th PPRC** — New mention — Strength: High
   Evidence: "FPQ sales has been provisionally recognised by considering the prices of 2024-25"; "Existing FPQ, arising out of 3rd PPRC, is up to 2022-23. 4th PPRC is under progress". Reported YoY growth (+14.5% revenue, +14.8% PAT, derived) is provisional; a pricing finalisation could move revenue/profit either way (direction not stated in the data).

3. **Governance: Audit Committee cannot be reconstituted; JV going-concern/insolvency flags** — New mention — Strength: High
   Evidence: Note 1 (Reg 18(1) LODR non-compliance); HALBIT "Material Uncertainty Related to Going Concern"; HATSOFF current liabilities exceed current assets by Rs 8,262.39 lakh; Infotech HAL CIRP then NCLT dissolution; HAL-Edgewood excluded from consolidation for lack of financials.

4. **HATSOFF 100% acquisition at nil consideration, related-party, not arm's length** — New mention — Strength: Medium
   Evidence: "at NIL consideration"; "The transaction is not at Arm's length." Strategic rationale stated is only "full management control" and "streamline decision-making". The target carries the going-concern note above, so HAL takes over a loss-making, net-worth-eroded entity (the nil price is consistent with that; the extracted data gives no HATSOFF profit or net worth).

5. **YoY profit growth of about 15% with other income and D&A both rising faster than revenue** — Acceleration (revenue/PAT), quality caveat — Strength: Medium
   Evidence: Revenue 5,51,528 vs 4,81,914; PAT 1,58,061 vs 1,37,715; other income 90,281 vs 74,894 (+20.5% derived, 42.5% of PBT); D&A 30,377 vs 18,502 (+64.2% derived).

6. **Programme pipeline additions: Aravalli engine (SAFHAL) and ALH AoN** — New mention — Strength: Medium
   Evidence: Aravalli "selected to power HAL's upcoming 13-ton Indian Multi-Role Helicopter (IMRH) and its naval variant"; ALH AoN within about Rs 1,10,000 cr DAC approvals with "approximately 98%" from Indian industry. Neither gives a value accruing to HAL.
