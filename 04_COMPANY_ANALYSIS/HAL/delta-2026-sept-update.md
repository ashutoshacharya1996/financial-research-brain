## New Mentions This Period

### **Aravalli Helicopter Engine Programme (Aug 26, 2026) — LANDMARK DEFENCE MILESTONE**

**Contract Details:**
- Final contract signed between HAL and Safran Helicopter Engines SAS via joint venture SAFHAL
- Scope: Design, development, manufacture, supply, lifecycle support of new-generation high-power helicopter engine (3,500–4,000 shp class)
- Platform: Powers HAL's 13-ton Indian Multi-Role Helicopter (IMRH) and naval variant Deck-Based Multi-Role Helicopter (DBMRH)
- Strategic note: "First time Safran HE has taken up such a class of Engine as Co-development" (emphasis on India's technical advancement)

**Management Commentary:**
- CMD Ravi K: "The Aravalli engine programme will not only support the operational requirements of the IMRH and DBMRH but will also contribute meaningfully to the development of a robust domestic aerospace industrial ecosystem."
- Civil market expansion flagged: "Beyond military applications, the engine programme also offers significant potential for future civil market opportunities, including offshore transportation, utility missions, and VVIP transport."
- MRO capability building: "Programme is also expected to further catalyse the development of indigenous Maintenance, Repair and Overhaul (MRO) capabilities in India."

**Signal Strength:** Very High. This contract represents HAL's entry into indigenous aero-engine co-development, a capability gap India has long struggled with. Success here de-risks India's helicopter platforms (IMRH, DBMRH, ALH, LCH, LUH) and reduces dependence on Safran imports over time.

### **MALE UAV Tender — 87 Units, ₹30,000 Crore (Bid submission June 16; Status as of Sept 7: Under Evaluation)**

**Background:**
- IAF requirement: 87 Medium-Altitude Long-Endurance (MALE) UAVs with surveillance and strike capabilities
- Tender value: ₹30,000 crore (largest indigenous UAV procurement in India's history)
- Award mechanism: Split order between L1 and L2 bidders (2 production lines)
- HAL status: Among 10 bidders (Adani Defence, Tata Advanced Systems, L&T, Solar Defence, others)
- Bid submission deadline: June 16, 2026 (extended twice by MoD)

**Current Status (as of Sept 7, 2026):** Award decision still pending. No L1/L2 announcement.

**Signal:** Medium. If HAL wins or co-wins (L2), this would be a transformational order. However, competitive intensity is high (10 bidders), and split order means max ₹15,000 crore even if L1. No updates since bid submission indicate evaluation is proceeding normally without public milestones.

### **Q1FY27 Governance and Going-Concern Flags (Aug 12 Board Filing)**

**Board Composition Non-Compliance:**
- Company not complying with SEBI Regulation 17(1) and Companies Act Section 149(4) due to insufficient independent directors
- Audit Committee and Nomination & Remuneration Committee NOT constituted since April 5, 2026
- This is a material governance red flag not previously flagged in prior delta

**HATSOFF Helicopter Training JV Going-Concern Risk:**
- HATSOFF (joint venture for helicopter training) has material uncertainty related to going concern
- Accumulated losses eroding networth: Rs. 3,129.90 crore as at June 30, 2026
- This is distinct from core HAL operations but indicates portfolio management risk

**FPQ Pricing Uncertainty:**
- Fixed Price Quotation (FPQ) prices for contracts from 2023-24 onwards not yet firmed up
- 4th Periodic Price Revision Committee (PPRC) under progress
- Prices provisionally recognized using 2024-25 indices without escalation — creates accounting/revenue recognition uncertainty
- This is a legacy issue but still unresolved as of Q1FY27

**Auditor Change (June 29, 2026):**
- Notified via NSE filing June 29, 2026
- Successor auditor not explicitly identified in extraction; change details minimal
