## Data Conflicts and Quality Flags (not resolved by guessing)

1. Standalone vs consolidated basis mismatch with June baseline. June deltas cite Q4 FY26 revenue Rs 13,942.40 cr and PAT Rs 4,196.04 cr, and FY26 revenue Rs 33,088.82 cr / PAT Rs 9,115.52 cr (search-snippet sourced). Q1 filing shows standalone Q4 revenue Rs 13,943.32 cr (13,94,332 lakh), standalone Q4 PAT Rs 4,184.28 cr (4,18,428), standalone FY26 revenue Rs 33,089.79 cr (33,08,979) and PAT Rs 9,075.67 cr (9,07,567). Consolidated OCR comparatives (13,94,240; 4,19,604) match the June figures. So June figures appear consolidated while this delta's headline YoY numbers are standalone. FY26 PAT consolidated vs standalone gap and the Rs 1 cr revenue gap are NOT verified here.
2. OCR garbling. Consolidated table is partly illegible; "1,37 715" has a space; consolidated figures flagged low confidence. Extraction note says verify against the PDF.
3. Order book date label. Q4 extract says order book Rs 2,54,538 cr vs Rs 1,89,302 cr "as on April 1, 2025" (+34%); the as-of date of the Rs 2,54,538 cr figure is ambiguous in the source. Carried forward unchanged.
4. Q4 YoY PAT growth +5.4% (June) cannot be cross-checked: Q4 FY25 comparative not in corpus.
5. HATSOFF framing. Q1 filing: HATSOFF is a JV with "substantial erosion of networth" and current liabilities exceeding current assets by "Rs.8,262.39 lakhs", while "management is of the opinion that company is a going concern"; separately, HAL is "still awaiting the delivery of the Military Dhruv Cockpit" and funding of cost escalation "will be decided". The 02-Oct filing states "Nil" consideration and "not at Arm's length". Auditor doubt vs management opinion is unreconciled; related-party characterisation of a counter-party deal is as stated in the filing and not interpreted here.
6. HALBIT Avionics (JV) also carries a going-concern uncertainty paragraph ("may cast doubt upon the Company's ability to continue as a going concern") - not previously tracked.
7. Infotech HAL: Q1 filing says the NCLT admitted the petition on "22nd August 2025" (insolvency, Section 10); dissolution order dated 17-Aug-2026 per the 20-Aug filing; the Q1 results (12-Aug) predate the dissolution. Financial impact of the dissolution: not stated.
8. CMD timing: Ravi K quoted as "CMD" in the 26-Aug press release; appointment listed as a material event in the 31-Aug filing. Appointment effective date not extracted.
9. ALH AoN: the Rs 1,10,000 cr is aggregate across all services' proposals; do not attribute to HAL.
10. Dividend: Rs 10 per share (200%) shown consistently in two filings; record/payment dates not extracted.
