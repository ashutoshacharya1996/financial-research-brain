## Headline Delta

Q2 FY27 revenue grew 14.9% YoY (Rs 7,613 cr) — slightly faster than Q1's 14.4% and above the FY27 guide of 10–12% — but profit growth slowed to 10.6% YoY (PAT Rs 1,839 cr vs 14.8% in Q1) while H1 operating cash flow fell ~79% YoY (Rs 1,578 cr vs Rs 7,382 cr) on an inventory build; in the same window HAL changed statutory auditor, lost two Executive Directors (Finance), had ICRA AAA withdrawn at its own request, and still reported no order book, orders or guidance update.
