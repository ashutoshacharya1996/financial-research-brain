### Basis Conflict (surfaced, not resolved)

- Prior delta/baseline uses Q4 FY26 revenue "Rs 171 crore" and FY26 revenue "Rs 478 crore" (media-reported). The Q1 FY27 Board outcome prints Q4 FY26 standalone revenue as 12,558 lakh (Rs 125.6 crore) and FY26 standalone as 41,654 lakh (Rs 416.5 crore). The AGM Notice states consolidated FY26 turnover as "Rs 476.57 crore". Rs 478 crore (media) vs Rs 476.57 crore (AGM Notice) are also not identical. Whether the Rs 171 crore figure is consolidated is not stated in the extracted files. Q1 FY27 consolidated figures were not extracted, so a like-for-like consolidated comparison against the 30-40% guidance (stated on a Rs 478 crore base) cannot be made.
- Implied FY27 revenue "Rs 621-669 crore" in prior deltas is computed on the Rs 478 crore (consolidated/media) base. Annualising standalone Q1 (Rs 118 crore x 4 = Rs 472 crore) is not a valid comparison and is not used.
