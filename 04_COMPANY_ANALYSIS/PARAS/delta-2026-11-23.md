## Headline Delta

Consolidated revenue growth re-accelerated to +42.9% YoY in Q2 FY27 (H1 FY27 +40.3%, at the top of the 30–40% FY27
guidance) with owners' PAT +46.8% and EBITDA margin up to 29.5%. But the growth is almost entirely Optics
(+94% YoY), while Defence Engineering's segment margin fell to 9.0% from 25.1%. On governance, minority shareholders
voted down a ₹150 cr related-party transaction (RPT) with the anti-drone subsidiary. The stock fell 9.4% from June
to November despite the results.

## Key Signals Summary

1. **Revenue and profit growth re-accelerated, tracking at the top of guidance**: Acceleration. Strength: High.
   Evidence: consolidated revenue Q2 FY27 ₹15,112 lakh vs ₹10,572 lakh (+42.9%); H1 +40.3%; owners' PAT Q2 ₹30.3 cr (+46.8%), H1 ₹51.5 cr (+45.1%); consolidated EBITDA margin 29.5% vs 28.1%.

2. **Growth is narrow (optics-led); Defence Engineering profitability collapsed**: Inflection down. Strength: High.
   Evidence: Optics revenue +94.2% YoY with a 60.3% segment margin. Defence Engineering revenue +13.7%, but its segment result fell to ₹688 lakh from ₹1,686 lakh (margin 9.0% vs 25.1%). Standalone traded-goods purchases were ₹2,328 lakh vs ₹136 lakh in Q1. The filing gives no explanation.

3. **Governance: minority shareholders rejected the ₹150 cr RPT with the 55% anti-drone subsidiary**: New mention (negative). Strength: High.
   Evidence: AGM Resolution 6 got 43.2% for and 56.8% against. The Controp-Paras ₹450 cr RPT passed with only 58.6% for. The company re-filed the same ₹150 cr limit by postal ballot (Oct 29) and had to correct the notice after "an incorrect version of the Postal Ballot Notice was uploaded". The MD was "concerned or interested in Agenda Item no 6".

4. **₹6,200 cr semiconductor (OSAT) ambition**: New mention. Strength: High (strategic), with high execution and funding risk.
   Evidence: the MoU and press release of Jul 22 (50 acres allotted). This is about 8x consolidated equity of ₹770 cr, and no funding plan is disclosed. News headline (tier_3): "shares fall 1% despite Rs 6,200 crore semiconductor facility plan".

5. **Cash generation and credit quality improved**: Inflection up. Strength: Medium–High.
   Evidence: H1 consolidated CFO +₹8,644 lakh vs −₹1,409 lakh. ICRA upgraded the rating to A (Stable) on Sep 30. Offsetting this, inventories rose 21% in six months.

6. **Order flow visibility weakened**: Deceleration / Dropped. Strength: Medium.
   Evidence: only one order was filed in five months (DRDO ₹26.59 cr), and no order book update has appeared since the June ~₹928 cr figure. The ₹80.28 cr DRDO order seen in a tier_3 headline is unconfirmed.
