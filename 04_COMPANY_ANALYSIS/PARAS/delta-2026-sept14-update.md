## Headline Delta

For the first time in this dataset's history of PARAS, a shareholder resolution has been **voted down**: Resolution No. 6, approving a Material Related Party Transaction with subsidiary **Paras Anti-Drone Technologies Private Limited**, failed with 56.8% of votes cast against it — a governance inflection that arrives in the same AGM where the *other* RPT resolution on the ballot (with associate company Controp-Paras Technologies) barely passed, drawing 41.4% opposition. This is a marked change from the confident, guidance-issuing, roadshow-heavy tone captured in the June 22 delta and warrants treating PARAS's related-party governance as an active risk item going forward.

## New Mentions This Period

- **Resolution No. 6 REJECTED — Material RPT with Paras Anti-Drone Technologies Pvt Ltd (Subsidiary)**: "Resolution no. 6 was not passed with requisite majority" — 3,175,312 votes against (56.80%) vs 2,414,946 in favour (43.20%). The specific reasons for rejection are not disclosed in this filing. This is the single most important governance signal in PARAS's extracted history to date.
- **Resolution No. 5 — Material RPT with Controp-Paras Technologies Pvt Ltd (Associate Company): passed, but only narrowly** — 58.57% in favour vs 41.43% against. Also worth flagging: shareholders are broadly skeptical of PARAS's related-party dealings this AGM season, not just the one that failed.
- **Governance procedural note**: Chairman Mr. Sharad Virji Shah did not attend the AGM; Managing Director Mr. Munjal Sharad Shah chaired in his place, and for Item No. 6 specifically, an Independent Director (Dr. Hina Gokhale) had to chair because Mr. Munjal Shah was "concerned or interested" in that item — consistent with standard related-party recusal procedure, but notable given the item's failure.
