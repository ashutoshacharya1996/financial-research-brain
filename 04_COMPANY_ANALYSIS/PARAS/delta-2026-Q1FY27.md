## Key Signals Summary

1. **Resolution 6 (RPT with Paras Anti-Drone Technologies Pvt Ltd) defeated at 17th AGM** — Inflection down (governance) — Strength: High
   Evidence (voting results, 2026-09-11): "Resolution No.(s) 1 to 5 have been passed with the requisite majority. However, Resolution no. 6 was not passed with requisite majority." Scrutinizer report: for 2,414,946 votes (43.1992%), against 3,175,312 votes (56.8008%), all by public institutions/non-institutions; promoter group (4,28,68,582 shares) did not vote as interested party. Notice sought ~Rs. 150 crore for FY 2026-27 (lease and commercial arrangements). Resolution 5 (Controp-Paras, up to Rs. 450 crore) passed but with only 58.57% in favour (3,274,018 votes), i.e. a thin public-shareholder majority. Implication: minority holders are resisting related-party scale-up; the anti-drone subsidiary cannot transact above the materiality threshold without fresh approval, which sits oddly beside the new Powerus counter-drone licence (the licence filing states the counterparty is not a related party; whether manufacturing is routed through the anti-drone subsidiary is not disclosed).

2. **Optics & Optronic segment acceleration** — Acceleration — Strength: High
   Evidence: "a. Optics and Optronic Systems 6,890 5,976 4,250 19,910"; revenue +62.1% YoY and +15.3% QoQ; segment result 2,642 vs 1,435 in Q4. This is the core franchise (optronic periscopes, electro-optics).

3. **Revenue growth consistent with guidance but quarterly lumpiness high** — YoY +37.3% (stable vs guided band), QoQ inflection down -25.3% — Strength: Medium
   Evidence: "a) Revenue from Operations 12,791 17,131 9,319 47,657". Defence Engineering revenue "5,901 11,155 5,069 27,747" fell 47% QoQ; YoY growth in that segment only 16.4%.

4. **Rs. 6,200 crore semiconductor OSAT MoU** — New Mention (capex) — Strength: High on scale / Low on certainty
   Evidence: "Backed by a proposed investment of Rs. 6,200 crore". For scale: FY26 consolidated revenue was Rs. 476.6 crore (47,657 lakh) and Q1 FY27 PAT Rs. 20.7 crore. No funding plan, timeline, incentives or customers disclosed. Large dilution/financing question; this is a transformative-risk item.

5. **ICRA upgrade to [ICRA]A (Stable)** — Inflection up — Strength: Medium
   Evidence: "upgraded the ratings to [ICRA]A from [ICRA]A-". Outlook changed to Stable from Positive (normal after an upgrade). No rationale document extracted.

6. **Counter-drone licence and DRDO space-payload order** — New Mention — Strength: Medium/Low
   Evidence: "exclusive license ... solely within India"; 12-month renewable term; no consideration disclosed. DRDO order ~Rs. 26.59 crore (about 5.6% of FY26 revenue), delivery by 27 Sep 2027. No updated total order book.

7. **Krasny Paras exit** — New Mention — Strength: Low
   Evidence: "The amount of consideration has not been finalised as it is dependent on the due diligence process." Associate net worth Rs. 0.63 crore, turnover contribution 0.12%; immaterial financially, clean-up of a non-core associate.
