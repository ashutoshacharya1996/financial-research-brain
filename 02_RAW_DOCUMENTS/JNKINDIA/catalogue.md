## Notes
- Q4 FY26 key metrics: Revenue ₹344.6 crore (+69.2% YoY), PAT ₹33 crore (+149.5% YoY), EBITDA margin 15.2%
- FY26 full year: Revenue ₹838 crore (+68% YoY), PAT +114.6% YoY
- Order book as of March 31, 2026: ₹1,961 crore; 97.5% domestic customers
